Open Minis is a *brilliant* app, and is based on roughly the same [iSH](https://ish.app/?utm_source=taoofmac.com&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_content=external_link)-derived ARM64 core I used for [`ios-linuxkit`](https://github.com/rcarmo/ios-linuxkit?utm_source=taoofmac.com&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_content=external_link) (which I [wrote about when I released it](/space/blog/2026/05/16/1130)).

I went all-out on generic Linux runtime support–Alpine, language runtimes, compilers and all the CLI tools I could get working–whereas Minis made the much sharper product choice of using CLI tools as the *internals* of an iOS agent. That is a brilliant inversion, and much closer to what Siri AI ought to be.
