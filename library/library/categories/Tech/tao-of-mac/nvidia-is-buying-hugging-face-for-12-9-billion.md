Well, this is unexpected. There were rumours while I was away–and Hugging Face reportedly turned down a $500 million NVIDIA investment earlier this year–but buying the entire thing for $12.9 billion doesn’t fit my mental model of NVIDIA, given its… spotty Open Source report card. They certainly have the money, given their current valuation and a [quarterly revenue run rate closing in on $100 billion](https://www.theverge.com/tech/985387/nvidia-hundred-billion-dollar-quarterly-revenue?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com).

This feels like the same sort of stack consolidation that happened as public cloud took off and [GitHub was acquired](/space/links/2018/06/03/2216#microsoft-is-said-to-have-agreed-to-acquire-github): owning the place where models live is a lot more ecosystem presence than pushing Nemotron models out in a corner and hoping people notice. I guess it makes sense. I am just not sure Hugging Face under NVIDIA is quite the same proposition…
