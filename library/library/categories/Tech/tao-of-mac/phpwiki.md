[PhpWiki](https://sourceforge.net/projects/phpwiki/?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) is the wiki engine I used before I replaced it with [Yaki](/space/projects/yaki), [Sushy](https://github.com/rcarmo/sushy?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) and [Sashimi](/space/projects/sashimi). It was a PHP-based wiki engine that used MySQL as its backend database.

Hardly anything from PhpWiki remained, since I tried to build something that was, in many respects, completely against its original philosophy.
