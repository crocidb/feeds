The RP2040 and RP2350 are dual-core ARM (and now also RISC-V) microcontrollers designed by the Raspberry Pi Foundation.

They are used in the Raspberry Pi Pico and other boards, and have become a popular choice for embedded projects due to their low cost and ease of use (dethroning the [ESP8266](/space/hw/esp8266) and [ESP32](/space/hw/esp8266) in many cases).
