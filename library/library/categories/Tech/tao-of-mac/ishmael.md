[Ishmael](https://ishmael.textualize.io/?utm_source=taoofmac.com&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_content=external_link) caught my attention because it takes one of [iA Writer](/space/apps/ia_writer)’s best qualities–an editor that disappears while I am writing–and applies it to an entire manuscript, with chapters, outlines, bookmarks, notes and export to Word, EPUB, HTML and Markdown. I usually begin long pieces in iA Writer and finish them in [VS Code](/space/apps/code), with folders, filenames and scripts holding everything together, so I am curious to see how much of that clutter Ishmael can replace.

It was created by the genius [Will McGugan](https://github.com/willmcgugan?utm_source=taoofmac.com&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_content=external_link), who also created [Rich](https://github.com/Textualize/rich?utm_source=taoofmac.com&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_content=external_link), [Textual](https://github.com/Textualize/textual?utm_source=taoofmac.com&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_content=external_link) and [PyFilesystem](https://github.com/PyFilesystem/pyfilesystem2?utm_source=taoofmac.com&utm_medium=web&utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_content=external_link). I don’t write novels, but I have demonstrably spent over two decades writing fairly long pieces here, and Ishmael focuses on things I care about: organising a messy draft, reading it without editing clutter and leaving the sentences to me. I can’t wait to see if there is going to be an iPad version.
