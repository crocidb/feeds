 Back in winter 2009 I was working pretty hard on the 3D engine that would power my next iPhone/iPad game: ["SHMUP"](https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/shmup/id337663605?mt=8). To demo the work in progress required to generate videos, a task far from being trivial on a smartphone: Slow CPU/GPU, Little RAM, no TV output, no storage space, no real multitasking. Hence I had the idea to have the engine generate its own videos. This is how I did it, maybe it will inspire someone..[ More...](https://fabiensanglard.net/shmup_generate_videos/)
