This month we participated in Catjam 2026 and released a humble point-and-click called [Lepore Express Line](https://hundredrabbits.itch.io/lepore-express-line). It features many cameos from all our other projects, maybe you can recognize a few? It's written in Uxntal, a concatenative assembly language for a 16-bit ordinator. Have a look at the [source](https://wiki.xxiivv.com/etc/flick.tal.txt). Devine drew the backgrounds, while Rek drew the characters and foreground elements. It's been a while since we've collaborated on an illustration.

Internet on this part of the coast continues to be unreliable, the signal floats in and out of the anchorage, varying in strength, often gone entirely for days. When hoisting the hotspot aloft doesn't work, we stand at the top of mountains in knee-high ferns, laptops in hand, ants crawling up our calves, bald eagles circling above, waiting for the upload to complete. Progress is good, but then it halts at 85%. Come on, you can do it. A mosquito bit me. It's not all for naught, we got the latest weather report on Lynx, responded to a friend's text message, downloaded some emails, pushed changes to a few repositories.

We call them summer projects, because it seems like only the pace of long days without distractions allows for us to dedicate time to them. These projects have very little extrinsic motivation, it's just what we default to when we're left to our own devices. For Rek, it's drawing comics. For Devine, that takes the shape of linguistic experiments, for weeks now Devine has been expanding on the work of Giuseppe Peano who was fascinated by the idea of encoding logic for preservation and communication. Giuseppe dedicated lengths of his life to auxiliary languages to make his mathematical ideas accessible across language barriers; he might have been inspired by today's difficulty of replicating software proofs and crumbling toolchains. Devine has been trying to formalize into BNF what Peano left behind about [Latino Sine Flexione](https://wiki.xxiivv.com/site/latin_sin_flexion.html), and expanding on his experiments with the [axioms of natural numbers](https://wiki.xxiivv.com/site/rejoice.html).
