This article explains how real-time gameplay systems can be created in the language of Prolog, based on a set of advanced concepts such as time-binding of relations, decomposition of discrete events, and compound symbols. This model of technical game design will be especially helpful for constructing highly emergent systems, such as ones which comprise the backbone of scientific simulation games (e.g. The Sims, SimCity, SimEarth, SimLife, etc).
