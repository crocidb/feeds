After reading ["Atom is better than RSS, in ways that matter" rant](https://chrismorgan.info/atom>rss) and not really being too impressed, I still implemented atom support for this site, but not removing rss. I couldn't find a good case where RSS is not working for my kind of the content, but here the backward compatibility wins for me: I want my 3 subscribers not to lose the updates.
