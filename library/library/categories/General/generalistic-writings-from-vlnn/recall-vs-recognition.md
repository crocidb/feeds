**Recall vs Recognition** or **Implicit vs explicit** — the cognitive-psych foundation of the design\*.\*

Python's Zen say "*explicit is better than implicit*" Ruby has "a principle of least surprise."

Known example are **The Cathedral and the Bazaar** and **[Right Thing](right-thing-mit-approach.md) vs [Worse Is Better](worse-is-better-new-jersey-approach.md).** More specific example of this dichotomy is [which-key ≅ FORTH and embark ≅ (any syntactically sugared lang)](which-key-forth-and-embark-any-syntactically-sugared-lang.md)

Perhaps preferring nicely edited old book or fresh hypertext is another example of same dichotomy?
