There’s an old adage that you should never bring a knife to a gunfight. [My take on OpenAI’s response yesterday](https://daringfireball.net/2026/08/openai_apple_is_getting_this_wrong) was expressing incredulity that OpenAI was bringing, like, a box of chocolates to the gunfight. Apple is trying to kill io, and OpenAI’s public response is basically “*We love you guys, can’t we just be friends?*”

This thread on Hacker News though points out that their response doesn’t even explain what they’re responding to. There’s no context. They just assume the reader is up to date on the lawsuit Apple filed. If you didn’t know anything about the suit and started with this post, it would make no sense at all. I’ve heard from friends there that the OpenAI culture is “too online”, and this might be the best proof of it.
