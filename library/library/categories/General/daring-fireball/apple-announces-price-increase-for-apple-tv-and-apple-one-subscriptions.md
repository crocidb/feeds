Chance Miller, 9to5Mac:

>
>
> Here are the full details on the price increases:
>
>
>
> * Apple TV (monthly): $14.99 (up from $12.99)
> * Apple TV (annual): $119 (up from $99)
> * Apple One Individual: $21.95 (up from $19.95)
>
>
>
> Prices for other Apple One plans are unchanged. When Apple [increased](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/17/apple-raises-prices-for-apple-music-and-apple-one-subscriptions/) the price of Apple Music last month, it also raised the Apple One Family and Apple One Premier plans, but not the Individual plan.
>
>
>
> The higher prices go into effect today for new subscribers. Apple says existing subscribers will be notified of the price increase about a month before they are charged the new price.
>
>

Seems poetic that Tim Cook’s last action as CEO is a goose to services revenue.
