Regarding my [earlier complaint](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/08/06/stopthescript-is-a-dickover-killer) about it being a slog to open Safari’s preferences in the Settings app on iOS, a few readers pointed me to this tweet last year from Quinn Nelson, showing how to build (or [download from iCloud](https://www.icloud.com/shortcuts/5d69e334840d4493a32db5ab330c712b)) a simple Shortcuts shortcut that you can add to Control Center to open the current app’s preferences in Settings. Works great.
