 Government secret agents [have been persistently investigating](https://theintercept.com/2026/08/15/undercover-ice-agents-minneapolis-sunrise-movement/) the Sunrise Movement. They keep getting more and more evidence that it is committed to nonviolence (including nonviolent civil disobedience), but continue searching desperately for some violence in it somewhere.
