[Flock Reportedly Tried to Turn Uber Drivers Into Mobile Snitches](https://gizmodo.com/flock-reportedly-tried-to-turn-uber-drivers-into-mobile-snitches-2000796138). And Nexar, a manufacturer of dashcams, is already doing that to unwitting drivers whose cars carry those cameras.

Drivers should be allowed to install dashcams that record, as security cameras, but security cameras [must not be allowed](https://gnu.org/philosophy/surveillance-vs-democracy.html) to transmit these images anywhere without specific time-limited requests.

Don't do business with [Uber](/uber.html)!
