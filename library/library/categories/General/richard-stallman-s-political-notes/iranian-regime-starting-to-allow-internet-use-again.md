The Iranian regime is [starting to allow people to use the internet again](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/may/28/iran-internet-blackout-return-partial-connectivity), but people are worried that the shutdown was used to introduce new systems of surveillance and censorship that are not directly visible.
