 \*Court-appointed investigator finds [biggest deportation prison in California] [failed to comply](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/jul/28/california-ice-detention-healthcare-violation) with judge's order to provide adequate [medical care] [to the prisoners]\*.

This prison is privatized. A private prison company can increase its profits by skimping on medical care; thus, using privatized prisons tends to increase the illnesses and deaths among prisoners. This is one of the reasons why privatized prisons should be prohibited.
