 \*[The Intercept Sues](https://theintercept.com/2026/08/12/trump-media-truthsocial-premium-first-amendment/) [the [corrupter](/glossary.html#trump)] for Selling [early] Access to Truth Social Announcements.\*

This seems to be a plan to profit privately by selling the opportunity to cheat on bets to "predict" what the corrupter has already decided to do.

"Prediction" betting invites corruption; I think we ought to prohibit it.
