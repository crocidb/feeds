 An inspector general's report found that [most of the alleged fraud](https://theintercept.com/2026/07/31/immigrants-healthcare-medicare-fraud-scam/) in US government medical funding comes from corporations, not from the immigrants that [magats](/glossary.html#magat) try to blame.

 Experts on immigrant rights and healthcare say the administration is using immigrants as a scapegoat for systemic fraud in the healthcare system that can be attributed to corporate greed, while shielding actual bad actors who profit from a broken system.
