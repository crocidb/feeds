[Robert Reich: How Do We Protect Children from Becoming Addicted to [Anti]Social Media?](https://web.archive.org/web/20260326141848/https://robertreich.substack.com/p/how-do-we-protect-children-from-becoming)

 I agree with several of the proposed methods, but not entirely with one of them -- to ban people under 16 years old from using antisocial media. That sounds simple and harmless, but the methods that can tell who is under 16 years old tend to identify people, and that is a danger in its own right.

 I propose prohibing platforms from operating any recommendation engines or suggesting the use of any particular ones.
