Israel [attacked a notary office in Lebanon](https://theintercept.com/2026/06/14/lebanon-civil-records-israe/), destroying records of land ownership for up to a quarter of a million people.

 This seems to be a way of preventing them from ever returning to their homes, or to the wreckage of their homes.
