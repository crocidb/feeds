Grok's supposed-intelligence service was in the habit of [copying a repository's entire contents](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/07/14/musk-promises-purge-after-grok-build-caught-sending-entire-repos-to-the-cloud/5271123) to a Google server, if a user said to operate on part of it. Where it may have been copied to from there, we can only guess.

 We recommend [being selective in using the term "artificial intelligence"](https://gnu.org/philosophy/words-to-avoid.html#ArtificialIntelligence) because most of the systems described that way do not qualify as "intelligence".

 We recommend avoiding the term ["the cloud"](https://gnu.org/philosophy/words-to-avoid.html#CloudComputing) because that term leads people to forget that each computer you can reach on the net belongs to some company (or person).
