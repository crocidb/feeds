Ralph Nader argues that an impeachment drive against [the corrupter](/glossary.html#corrupter) now, before the 2026 election, would be a [good way to induce splits](https://mailchi.mp/nader/an-impeachment-drive-can-lead-to-trumps-resignation?e=555e9be067) in the Republican Party and may be drive him to resign.

 His arguments may be valid, but I wonder how much less destructive and cruel Vance would be.
