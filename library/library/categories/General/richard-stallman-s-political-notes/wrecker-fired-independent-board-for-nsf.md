[The wrecker](/glossary.html#wrecker) [fired the independent board](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/jun/02/trump-administration-ocean-observatories-initiative) that oversees the National Science Foundation and replaced them with obedient saboteurs. Their first act of sabotage is to terminate the Ocean Observatories Initiative and reserve its monitoring equipment.

 Nothing can help the businesses that want to cause mayhem in the ocean like eliminating the equipment that can monitor their activities and the changes they cause.
