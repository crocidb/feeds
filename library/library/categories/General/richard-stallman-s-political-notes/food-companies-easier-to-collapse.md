 George Monbiot warns that the mergers of so many food companies have produced a business system that [is susceptible to economic collapse](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2026/mar/25/big-corporations-global-food-system-war-iran). The collapse could be triggered by large shocks of various kinds, perhaps political or environmental, but the collapse would make the consequences far worse.
