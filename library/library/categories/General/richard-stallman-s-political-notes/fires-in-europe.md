 Large fires in France and Spain [are approaching](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/jul/26/firefighters-in-france-battle-to-prevent-huge-blaze-from-approaching-bordeaux) major cities.

If we don't get serious about curbing [global heating](</glossary.html#global heating>), it will reach a point where major cities are lost. Keeping up with [global heating](</glossary.html#global heating>) is a losing game -- the only way to triumph over it is to stop feeding it.
