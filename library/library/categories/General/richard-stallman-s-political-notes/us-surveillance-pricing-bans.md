 Three US states [have banned](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/jul/26/new-jersey-surveillance-pricing-ban) surveillance pricing. The latest is New Jersey.

Since I buy anonymously and pay cash, I can't be touched by surveillance pricing -- with one exception: airline tickets, which can't be anonymous. I wonder, will New Jersey's law apply to flights from Newark Airport?
