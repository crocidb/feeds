[The wrecker](/glossary.html#wrecker) is going all-out to increase the use of fossil fuels, but is not convincing the American public that that is safe.

 \*Two-thirds of Americans say they are [worried about climate](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/jun/17/climate-change-public-opinion-trump-fossil-fuel) but level of media coverage does not reflect this.\*

 I don't think [the wrecker](/glossary.html#wrecker) cares about the danger of global climate disaster, only about rewarding the [planet roasters](/glossary.html#planet) to keep them in his corner. They all deserve to end up broke and experience living on however much support the US provides to the poor.
