It seems [the corrupter](/glossary.html#corrupter) has decided to [cut his losses](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/jun/17/trumps-iran-deal-is-result-of-unrealistic-ambitions-for-an-untenable-war) in the gratuitous (and unwinnable) war with Iran. For once he has approached a situation in a way that does his country a little good, though it is far less than the harm he has done in relations with Iran over the past year. Meanwhile, he found no way to fix the problem that he gratuitously created when he [cancelled](https://stallman.org/archives/2025-jul-oct.html#1_October_2025_(UN_sanctions_against_Iran_reactivated)) Obama's non-nuclear deal with Iran.

 He will salvage something from this idiocy, though -- some way to increase profits for some of his billionaire supporters.
