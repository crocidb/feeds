The [persecutor](/glossary.html#trump)'s men [arrested and jailed](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/jul/17/ice-arrests-human-rights-lawyer-wu-shaoping-fled-chinese-crackdown) Wu Shaoping, a Chinese human rights lawyer who fled to the US in 2020 and asked for asylum.

Since then he has remained lawfully in the US waiting for a decision on his asylum request, until being arrested for no known reason. Will [the persecutor](/glossary.html#trump) deport Wu to China, where he will surely face imprisonment after a bogus trial?

It is a damned shame that such an asylum decision can take so many years, but that is a different issue.
