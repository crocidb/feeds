"Appeasing bond markets" — [prioritizing keeping interest rates for government bonds low](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2026/jun/02/uk-right-bond-markets-instability-austerity-populism) — is supposed to bring "stability", but in the UK it means a steady decline in the productive economy.

 A government has a practical need for low interest rates if it regularly borrows money to spend. Clearly governments need to get less funds by borrowing and more by taxing the rich.
