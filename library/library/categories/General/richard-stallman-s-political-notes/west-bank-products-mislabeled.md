Shipments of products made in Israel's colonies in the West Bank are [systematically mislabeled as "Made in Israel"](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/jun/15/settler-products-from-occupied-palestine-sold-to-europe-as-israeli-investigation-finds) to take fraudulent advantage of trade preferences for exports of Israel's products.
