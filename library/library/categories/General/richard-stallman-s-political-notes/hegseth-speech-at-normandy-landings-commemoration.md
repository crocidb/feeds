Hegseth went to a commemoration of the Normandy landings of 1944 for two events, but his speech at the first one at Colleville-sur-Mer was so [racist and hateful](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/jun/14/normandy-village-legrune-sur-mer-protest-d-day-pete-hegseth) that people in the town of Langrune-sur-Mer (where Hegseth had planned to appear) posted their disgust. This showed how vile he is and reverberated around the world.
