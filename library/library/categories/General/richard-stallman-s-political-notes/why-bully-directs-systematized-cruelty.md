Why does [the bully](/glossary.html#bully) direct his agents to perform so much systematized cruelty on randomly chosen victims? One [possible explanation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System_justification_theory) is that imposing an inflexible system manipulates people to believe that the system is justified and correct.
