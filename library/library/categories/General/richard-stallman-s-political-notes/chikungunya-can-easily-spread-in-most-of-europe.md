Due to our greenhouse gas emissions, [chikungunya can now spread](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2026/feb/18/tropical-disease-chikungunya-transmitted-europe-study) in most of Europe.

 Many "tropical" diseases are, or will be in a few decades, able to spread in the formerly temperate zones.
