\* Decriminalizing the possession of cannabis or strictly regulating access to the drug do not appear to drive up usage, but when the drug is sold commercially the [number of users increases](https://www.theguardian.com/society/2026/jun/17/cannabis-commercialisation-not-decriminalisation-drives-up-usage-study) and more mental health problems are seen, a review has found.\*
