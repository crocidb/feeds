 The European heat wave and drought have [dried up rivers](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/aug/01/eastern-europe-dried-up-rivers-tourism-energy-mammoth), cutting off shipping of freight.

 In addition, nuclear power plants are shutting down for lack of cooling water. They are too expensive to be economically feasible.
