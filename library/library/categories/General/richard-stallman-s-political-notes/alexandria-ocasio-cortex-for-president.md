[Calling on Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez to run for president.](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2026/jul/21/alexandria-ocasio-cortez-for-president)

I will support her campaign. But I will point out to her that the quandary of choosing between «latino» and «latina» is limited to Spanish. In English we already have a gender-neutral word to use: "Latin". (When a noun, its plural is "Latins".)

For Spanish, [I have proposed](https://stallman.org/articles/castellano-sin-genero.html) a solution.
