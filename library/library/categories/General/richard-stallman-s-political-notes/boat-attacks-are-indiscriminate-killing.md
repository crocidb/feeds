Some of the boats that the US attacks are probably engaged in smuggling people as well as possibly drugs. But the US generally doesn't identify the people on the boat — so attacking the boat is [indiscriminate killing](https://theintercept.com/2026/06/10/trump-boat-strikes-human-trafficking-victims/).

 If the US government has reason to suspect the boat's crew of smuggling anything, the legitimate thing to do is capture them, question them, and then perhaps try them. Not murder them.
