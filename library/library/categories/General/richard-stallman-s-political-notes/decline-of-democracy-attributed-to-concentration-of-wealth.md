 Robert Reich [attributes the decline of democracy](https://web.archive.org/web/20260609140829/https://robertreich.substack.com/p/sunday-thought-its-still-capital) and the rise of mass poverty to the concentration of wealth, and the concentration of wealth to government's failure to resist growth of monopolies and the under-taxation of the rich. In that, he is straight on target. His willingness to attack root causes is why I basically support him.

 Where he uses the [term "AI"](https://gnu.org/philosophy/words-to-avoid.html#ArtificialIntelligance) he falls for the usual marketing hype campaign, perhaps not realizing that it is one.
