Today was a really nice and sunny day, so I went out with some friends from uni. We went to a Café (no shops open on International Workers' Day) and decided to go to the museum next to it because museums are free with our student ticket thing.

The main exhibit in the museum atm is "[WOMEN IN THE DARK](https://womeninthedark.org/)" by [Franziska Greber](https://franziskagreber.com/); an exhibit about misogyny and violence. Not very fun and whimsical, like the day upto then, but we liked it and we spent a few hours there looking at everything.

The exhibit consisted mostly of messages written by women around the world with red ink on white shirts, and there were interactive parts too, like a book of poems and quotes by participants. There were also videos of women telling stories about their experience with violence and discrimination.

At the top of the museum (it has 3ish floors) there was a guestbook for people who had attended the exhibit in various places. I liked this, as a way to see how people connected with the art.

One of the messages was written with a pencil (rather than the pen that lay next to the book) and it said:

>
>
> Why should I care?
>
>
