Woah. My ship didn't explode. Twice.

I've been roaming a bit of lowsec again in my hookbill. I managed to reach 6 killmarks today! So that's 6 kills in this one ship, which of 4 were today.

![Screenshot of my ship with 6 killmarks on it](/assets/images/Screenshot_20260506_003701.png)

Holy crap, my ships never stay running this long. I am flying back to base as I'm writing this, so I'm expecting it to blow up while writing, lol.

Anyway, I had two really close calls. One of them was with a Federation Navy Comet, that was really tough, because of the drones and it had also railguns. But I managed to keep my transversal high and avoid its shots.

![Screenshot of my ship burning](/assets/images/Screenshot_20260506_001831.png)

It was in very rough shape after the fight, I had like sliver of hull left. But I managed to win this time. I then undocked again and went for more roaming.

![My ship flying out of a hangar, with "Fly safe" holographic signs nearby](/assets/images/Screenshot_20260506_002003.png)

Fly safe indeed.

I found someone ratting around with a Catalyst Navy Issue and managed to catch it unaware, then just made it go boom. Simple enough, it could not fight back at all.

After that, I found a Cormorant Navy Issue that managed to also take me down to hull.. Another very close call. But I managed to fly under its guns and keep the transversal velocity really high again, so it couldn't track me anymore. I then just made sure to get rid of it, slowly but surely. But again my ship was on fire..!

I had such adrenaline rush today I had to just call it quits for now. Such an amazing feeling to win multiple fights with same ship.
