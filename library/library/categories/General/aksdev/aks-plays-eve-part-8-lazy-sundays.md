Not much happening this time. I spent some of the time this weekend doing the Warpath event, but it turned out to be a bit boring. As much as I enjoy a nice lil PVE event, my blood craves for adrenaline inducing fights against other players.. So I went back to hunting.

Also the event rewards, while nice, were being paid very unreliably. Sometimes I just got no rewards at all.. So I didn't bother wasting my time on it anymore.

Anyways as I was roaming back to my pirate home, I spotted 80 mil worth of items in my route inside random station. I docked just to see whats there and it was bunch of various crap, and some ships. I have no memory of them, they're just there, sitting idle.. And I don't feel like hauling them.

Over years in my EVE adventures, I have assets all around systems. Some are forever stuck in nullsec stations, some scattered around lowsec and highsec.. It's kinda fun to sometimes just stumble upon those assets and see what kind of story they tell. These specific assets just told me "this was my pirate homebase at some point." :D

I won a 2v1, but I think the person is a single player with two accounts (multiboxing) and didn't know how to fly. Besides, the ships were very cheap fits, which usually is sign of a newbie (or sometimes bot, though i don't think this is the case this time).
