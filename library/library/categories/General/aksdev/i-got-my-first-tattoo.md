I promised to share this here too, so here you go, I finally got a proper tattoo!

[![](https://akselmo.dev/processed_images/scale_tattoo.b3a8bd98988a7d96.png) ](https://akselmo.dev/assets/images/photos/scale_tattoo.png)

It's a tattoo of my skin being torn off and there's scales dark gray underneath.

It was made by [Ravi-Lassi (@ravilassi)](https://www.instagram.com/ravilassi/?hl=en).

I've wanted this kind of tattoo since I was 15 so I finally went for it. I'm glad I did! I'm really happy about it.

The experience was a bit painful (apparently this is a painful area). At first it was quite tolerable and okay, but when the last highlights were being added, it hurt *a lot!*

I'm already thinking of next ones. Maybe Quake logo and Morrowind cover-art somewhere, maybe Half-Life logo.. And I really want more of these scales, probably on my back next time.
