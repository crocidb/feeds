The dam of AI-written doc contributions might be about to break. It’s already cracking for code, with posts [wondering](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45744209) how to review a vibe-coded pull request consisting of nine thousand new lines of code. In the midst of what Tom Johnson [describes as acceleration](https://idratherbewriting.com/blog/ai-narrative-from-liberation-to-acceleration), docs-as-code writers wonder how to contain the seemingly inescapable wave that could bury their backlogs in AI slop. The answer could lie in taking a stance. This means crafting an AI policy for docs.
