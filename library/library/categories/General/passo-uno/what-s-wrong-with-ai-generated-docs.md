In what is tantamount to a [vulgar display of power](https://tante.cc/2025/03/28/vulgar-display-of-power/), social media has been flooded with AI-generated images that mimic the style of Hayao Miyazaki’s anime. Something similar happens daily with tech writing, folks happily throwing context at LLMs and thinking they can vibe write outstanding docs out of them, perhaps even surpassing human writers. Well, it’s time to draw a line. Don’t let AI influencers *studioghiblify* your work as if it were a matter of processing text.
