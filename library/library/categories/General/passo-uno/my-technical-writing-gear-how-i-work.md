Technical writing requires appropriate gear to be done in a way that’s both healthy and productive. While it’s true that communicating with subject-matter experts and writing documentation can be done on a tiny Chromebook, I would compare such an experience to driving all the way from Chicago to San Francisco on a [BMW Isetta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isetta#BMW_Isetta_(Germany)): feasible, though not very comfortable nor fast, and certainly not fun for your derrière.
