Beholding Stripe’s excellent documentation and wondering how they’ve built it is a modern technical writing trope. It’s no wonder, then, that when Stripe announced that they open sourced their documentation format and framework, [Markdoc](https://markdoc.io/docs/syntax), folks would get psyched. I, too, was startled by the sudden release. After some initial doubts, I came to love their approach.
