While chatting with [Sarah Deaton](https://sdeaton.com/) about the formats agents could use to talk to each other, we got to questions that might sound obvious: why should machines (say, LLMs) communicate with each other like we do? Do they need all the rhetoric we carry around? Or could they get by with a terser register, one that carries the same meaning but in far fewer tokens?
