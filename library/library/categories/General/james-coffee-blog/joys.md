Here are a few moments that have brought me joy and have written down while wandering through the world.

The Joys, of:

* Looking back on the year and seeing how many new things I have done.
* Seeing people start their day - of realising I, too, am starting my day.
* A friend pointing out an accessibility feature in the train station — of remembering that moment as I navigate my own environment in a different city. I think of the connections between where I am and where I have been; between places near and far.
* The barista who knows your name.
* Hearing live music at the Castle.
* Spending a day with a friend and introducing them to a city you think of as a second home.
* Seeing the hills in the evening.
* Seeing a lotus.
* Realising how much you appreciate the sky.
* Watching the rhythm of the world.
* Noticing the red flowers out the window.
* Having taken a journey hundreds of times and still being surprised and delighted when a castle comes into view.
