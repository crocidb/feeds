trees and canopies  
shelter for the fisherman  
stoic as a river: he is still  
in movement  
engaged like a painter  
watching the moment.

*This poem was inspired by Jules-Louis Dupré's "*[*Willows, with a Man Fishing*](https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/paintings/jules-louis-dupre-willows-with-a-man-fishing)*".*
