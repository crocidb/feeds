I have been saving a lot of links to things I have read and appreciated this month. And since it is Blaugust and many of my friends and other writers whose works I enjoy are blogging, I have been reading more blog posts too. I thought I would share an excerpt of what I have read and seen on the web this month here, both for future reference and in case you would like to explore some links.

This list is by no means complete, and I am limiting myself to one post per author.

Art and music
----------

* [Ville-d'Avray: Entrance to the Wood](https://www.nationalgalleries.org/art-and-artists/4776?location[36063]=36063&search_set_offset=39) by Jean-Baptiste Camille Corot (which I [shared with V.H. as part of a painting exchange)](https://vhbelvadi.com/entrance-to-the-wood)
* [The Lilac Sunbonnet](https://www.nationalgalleries.org/art-and-artists/261456?location[36063]=36063&page=4&search_set_offset=291) by Bessie MacNicol
* [Woman and Child in a Garden](https://www.nationalgalleries.org/art-and-artists/5209?subjects[30590]=30590&search_set_offset=7) by Berthe Morisot
* [The books that reveal hidden works of art along their gilded edges.](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/p0p422t8)
* I listened to [this playlist of Chinese pop music one morning](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BDYWDr8XZjo). I didn’t know the songs but it was relaxing.
* [That Feeling by Novembers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nqTOJU-Qxdw)
* [Interstate 8 by Modest Mouse](https://modestmouse.bandcamp.com/track/interstate-8-2)
* Taylor Swift’s Debut Album (I haven’t listened to it much, and so some of the songs still feel relatively new)
* The songs available on Paris Paloma’s upcoming album “The Fatal Flaw” are all incredible. The music videos, too. In particular: “[Miyazaki [Fairy Lyric Video]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BQ89uLrw7_Q)”, and “[Pre-Raphaelite](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kevNt8Wdpjg)”. For the latter video, I love Paris’ comment in the YouTube comments: “We made a living, breathing painting just for you.🌹I hope you enjoy x” For the former video: I won’t let you take it from me (go listen to the song).
* [P.D.L.I.F.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HfUwOuvNd0M) by Bon Iver. The whole album on which this song was published is amazing.

Videos
----------

* [S2E9: Paul Scheer & Jason Mantzoukas in "One Man's Trash"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nwi_kE0gy94) (Chris Gethard Show). This was *hilarious.*
* This is more a link from this year than only this month, but [Nena Shelby's Taylor Swift guitar tutorials](https://www.youtube.com/@NenaShelbyMusic/videos) have made learning the guitar so fun.

Technology
----------

* “All code is liability. Results are what matters. Write code to do what you need to do, try to minimize it in general, don’t over-index on it. Focus on results.” – [Ryan](https://snarfed.org/2026-08-16_57510)
* [CSF\_03](https://tantek.com/2026/226/t1/cybersecurity-friday-small-business), the latest in Tantek’s Cybersecurity Friday series
