making notes and reviewing quotes,  
collected from day and night:  
here, there are stories.

i revel in observing, and  
in place-  
making.  
where am i? how do i feel?  
what does this mean to me?

i think of the melody of words  
their cadence and shape and tense  
and from all, i feel:.  
from words, wonder;  
*with words, wonder.*
