[Naty is hosting a new Homebrew Website Club](https://burgeonlab.com/rsvps/2026/0715-1415/) event on Wednesday, August 5, 2026, at 5:30pm to 7:00pm Hong Kong Time (UTC+8). If there is interest, the event may turn become regular!

If the time works for you and you love chatting about websites, I recommend checking out the event. Homebrew Website Clubs are always fun, providing a place to chat about what people are building on their websites, the culture of the web, HTML/CSS, writing on the web, and more.

This event adds to several other online events, geared toward:

* [European timezones (HWC London/Europe)](https://events.indieweb.org/2026/07/homebrew-website-club-europe-london-C7hLAVUWvWxs) (which I host!)
* [The US East Coast (HWC Eastern)](https://events.indieweb.org/tag/eastern)
* [The US West Coast (HWC Pacific)](https://events.indieweb.org/tag/pacific)

Furthermore, there are in-person events that happen in several cities listed on the [IndieWeb Events community page](https://events.indieweb.org/), and the [IndieWebClub Bangalore website](https://blr.indiewebclub.org/), all of which are run to provide a place to chat for people passionate about websites.
