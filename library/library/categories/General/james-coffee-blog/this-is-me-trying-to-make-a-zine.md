After the [IndieWeb Zine Pop Up](https://jamesg.blog/2026/05/24/joining-the-indieweb-zine-pop-up) in May, I decided that I wanted to make a zine. I had already made a zine before, Quiet Edinburgh, but the zine was more of a booklet. After the meetup, I started feeling inspired. I wanted to make a tiny paper zine, one that I could fit in my pocket. The idea lay in the back of my mind for a few months. Occasionally, I would think to myself *I should make a zine.*

Earlier this week, I had an idea for one: a collection of Taylor Swift quotes that I like. I let the idea sit on my whiteboard for a few days. When an idea rests on my whiteboard, it becomes an idea with which I am living: written on a place in my living space, ready to come to life at any point. This afternoon, I finally decided to make a zine. I called it “this is me trying (a new zine)”.

The title comes from the Taylor Swift song, but ended up going in a different direction from my original idea of being an index of quotes (as, indeed, many creative projects end up going in new directions; therein, one of the many joys of making things). I started writing what I wanted to accomplish, and then brought in two practical exercises for the reader. I am delighted with what I made, even if it is a bit rough around the edges.

I used a folding chart by [Smolghost on Itch](https://smolghost.itch.io/zine-resources) to help me fold the paper. This was invaluable. I am not sure if I followed all the instructions correctly, but I did end up with a tiny booklet I could hold.
