Do you remember the moment when you first heard your favourite song performed live? Or a moment when a friend told you that a song you love was a cover and that there were many more versions to hear? Do you remember a particular street performer – a guitarist, a pianist, or even a jazz band – who made you smile? Or have a memory of playing an instrument yourself that sticks in your memory?

This month I am hosting the Bear Blog Carnival, which is on the topic of “Music memories.”

I invite you to write a blog post on either:

1. A memory you have related to music, or:
2. A memory you want to make related to music.

To participate, you are invited to publish a post on your website related to the theme. Once you have published your post, [send me an email](https://jamesg.blog/email) with a link. I will add all contributions to a list at the bottom of this post. At the end of the month I hope this page will become a home for a constellation of musical memories for us all to read. I recommend that you submit your post by September 30th, although I will accept late entries too.

I look forward to reading what you write!

*At this point in the post, you might be thinking: “James, you don’t use Bear Blog!” That is true. The folks at the* [*Grizzly Gazette*](https://grizzlygazette.bearblog.dev/carnival/)*, who steward the Carnival, have kindly offered to let me host this month as there was no host assigned for the month. On that note: **everyone is welcome to participate in the Carnival**. You don't need to have a Bear Blog. If you run a Bear Blog, you should volunteer to host the next one! You can find* [*contact information for the Gazette*](https://grizzlygazette.bearblog.dev/) *on their website.*
