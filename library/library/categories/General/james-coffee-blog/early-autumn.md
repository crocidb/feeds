I look out to the trees around and I see a sea of green. The air was warm today; summer is still here. With that said, there are ever more signs that indicate the beginning of the forthcoming season. Every so often, I notice a tree whose leaves are yellowing. This morning, I saw a tree on which the leaves on one branch had started to turn a vibrant red. The hills were being kissed by a light mist. The occasional leaf falls. In the park today, I walked among rain-soaked leaves that fell with the day’s breeze.

The leaves in a particularly dense grove flowed from the trees like a light flurry of snowflakes might fall from the sky: every one unique, travelling at their own pace; signals of the seasons. I reached out and caught a leaf, a browning leaf, between two fingers. The leaf was wet from the night and morning rains that had since stopped, the sky now showing signs of blue among the still-lingering clouds. I held onto the leaf for some time, and the awe I felt that I caught a leaf flying through the air. I held the beginnings of a new season in my hand. I felt the beginnings of the new season in my heart.

⁂ ⁂ ⁂

My days of late have been characterised by colour and music: by the colours of morning, evening, and night – of golds and ambers and pinks and blues – and the music of moments: of singing birds, of being at a concert, of playing my favourite songs on my guitar and electric keyboard. I hold onto music and colour wherever I go: to the sounds of singers bringing music that brings joy to people in the city, to the greens and yellows and reds and browns of leaves from trees that are changing with the seasons.

When I woke up this morning, I appreciated the sounds of the early morning rain as I looked up and out the window to see the first glimpses of the new day. I appreciated the colour of the grey clouds and of the green trees. Later on, while out and about, I heard the town clocktower chime, a grey clocktower in the heart of a colourful town. I paid attention to every chime, a sound I have always loved. Why do bells and clock towers stand out so much? My first thought is that they were designed to stand out. My next thought is that the sound is so beautiful.

⁂ ⁂ ⁂

Summer is still in the air, but I always enjoy the moments of transition between seasons – of the [first moments](https://jamesg.blog/2026/08/24/first-moments) that show that one season is ending, and another is beginning.
