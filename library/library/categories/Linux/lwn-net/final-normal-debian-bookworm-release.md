 Debian has [announced](https://lwn.net/ml/all/e0718a5433efa5ba4a71fd3b671aab06f1441a07.camel@debian.org) the final normal update for Debian 12 ("bookworm"). Long-term-support updates will [continue until 2028](https://lwn.net/ml/all/a27c47d0f34f36e497d2596a9adfbcb662cd4439.camel@debian.org/). As may be expected from a stable version, the update is mostly limited to security fixes. Still, it may be time for Debian users to look into upgrading to a more recent version. Conveniently, Debian 13 ("trixie") also [received an update](https://lwn.net/ml/all/baf38e8be7c03f43ace7fb06433aed0aac8196de.camel@debian.org) this weekend, with many of the same security fixes.
