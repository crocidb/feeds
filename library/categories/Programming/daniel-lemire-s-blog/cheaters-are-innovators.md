If you seek approval above all else, you are unlikely to innovate outside the rigid bounds of the current system. But if you are willing to cheat, you can innovate.

* You do not convince traditional media or academic publishers to loosen up. You start your own site or a Substack, you build the audience directly.
* You do not convince NASA to iterate faster or accept higher risk. You build SpaceX and make the previous way of doing things look slow and expensive.
* You do not wait for management to green-light AI. You use it.

Changing a system from the inside is inefficient. Make the old system less necessary. Test the idea yourself. Listen to objections, then decide. Work inside a company or university if you must, but do not assume that playing by the existing rules will produce the change you want.

How are you planning to cheat this year?
