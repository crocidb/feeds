> As a child, I was never a fan of any actor or singer. David Bowie is the closest thing to a “music idol” I’ve ever had. I never had a poster of him on my wall, or a T-shirt with his face on it, but he was a constant presence in my playlists, to the point where Spotify calls him my favourite artist.

11 years ago, on 19 November 2015, David Bowie releases [“Blackstar”](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackstar_(song)) (as a single). Early the following year, on 8 January, [the album of the same name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackstar_(album)) is released. Oh, what a great album, if not the greatest of them all. It’s certainly one of my top five favourite (concept) albums, maybe even top three.
