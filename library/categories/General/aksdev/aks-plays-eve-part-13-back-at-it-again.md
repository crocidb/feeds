Space pewpew continues!

Been a while. Since I got my Akademy trip coming and it's making me super nervous, I wanted to play a "comfort food" game, which for me happens to be EVE Online.

Not much to report, except that I lost two Kestrels to some mistakes. I think some of the matchups were impossible either way, but hey, it was good way to get my feet wet again.

However, I decided to do One More Run before stopping for the night, so I took the trustworthy Caldari Navy Hookbill out in the space.

![My cool caldari navy hookbill](/assets/images/Screenshot_20260913_234531.png)

The keen eyed may notice that there is two killmarks on this thing! I managed to win alone against two destroyers:

* [https://zkillboard.com/kill/138426666/](https://zkillboard.com/kill/138426666/)
* [https://zkillboard.com/kill/138426693/](https://zkillboard.com/kill/138426693/)

Honestly I was surprised how well it went. I trusted my instincts to do just manual piloting and keep them away from me in about 8km distance. I could hit them, but they couldn't hit me.

Good fight! And good night!
