*Another Akademy attended, here's some photos!*

Hello hello! It was that time of the year where I go to [KDE Akademy](https://akademy.kde.org/), talk to fellow nerds, almost lose my voice due to socializing and eat a lot of nice food.

This time Akademy was in Graz, Austria. I had never been in Austria before. This time I also had to travel alone due to my wife being busy with studying.

Traveling alone was nervewracking for sure, but somehow I managed to do it. Unless I am dreaming and wake up under a bench in Frankfurt airport or something.

Now I do not have a lot of photos, but I tried. I am bad at photos, many of them are super shakey or bad. But maybe these are interesting anyway.

![My lizard fursona making an smile face.](/assets/images/emotes/smile.png)

Let me know if you want me to blur your face, I tried my best avoid those who had red lanyards!!!

[Traveling to Graz](#traveling-to-graz)
----------

I spent first 6h in a midnight train from home to Helsinki and could not sleep. Then I went from Helsinki to Frankfurt on a plane, I slept like two hours maybe. And from Frankfurt I went to Graz on a plane, also managed to sleep a bit.

At airport I met fellow KDE people and we managed to figure out our way to the hotel together.

The moment I arrived there, there was a welcome event on top of some building with bunch of food and drink. I had good time though I was super tired and the place was fully packed so I had barely any energy or will to try to properly socialize.

[Second day of speeches and goals](#second-day-of-speeches-and-goals)
----------

During day two I spent most of the time at the venue as well, and the new KDE Goals were announced: I am one of the KDE Goal champions now! Yay!

The goal I am champion of is [Next Generation Styling for KDE](https://invent.kde.org/teams/goals/goal-setting/2026/-/work_items/3).

See the youtube video of the goals, our little speech timestamped: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kwv4SQSvx9g&t=2559](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kwv4SQSvx9g&t=2559)

(I am the guy in full black with the Xenia shirt.)

So yes, make Union bug reports! :)

After the talks and goal stuff, we had a KDE birthday party! KDE is 30 years old now, so we had food and cake.

[BoF days](#bof-days)
----------

No photos from these, as I spent all my time in them. BoFs are tiny group hacking/discussion/etc sessions about stuff. I attended Union related BoFs but I also held my own: Desktop icons!

There will be it's own blogpost about the desktop icon BoF soon:tm:.

[Daytrip: Chocolate](#daytrip-chocolate)
----------

We went to a chocolate factory!!!

I've never been in one but it was very fun. And chocolatey.

There was like 300 tasting things for different processing phases of chocolate, from the chocolate beans to the full product.

I tried to taste it all. I really, really tried. At the end of it though I felt rather sick and did not want any more lol.
