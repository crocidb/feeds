*This is personal ramble yet again and it touches the AI topic again. Sorry.*

I will be 33 years old this month. Depending who you ask, I'm either "youngster" or "unc." What I've noticed I've began to mellow a bit. Perhaps it's something that just happens with age.. Or with all the years of therapy I have gone to. Or both.

This mellowing has caused me go over the "good" ol' contentious "AI" topic. No, I'm not turning into "AI" booster and having a psychosis about my argonian "AI" girlfriend. Don't worry.

What I actually have been thinking about, is that I *could* find an use for some of this: For research purposes. **But as of now, I will not bother with any of it.** It is still [a weapon](https://akselmo.dev/posts/it-is-just-a-weapon/) in my eyes, and nothing has changed that view yet.

**The ONLY WAY I would ever consider using it is it fulfills the following criteria**:

* The whole stack is free and open source software, from models to tools around it.
* It is trained with text that it only has rights to use: Opt-in, public domain.
* It does not talk to me, but gives me data I can read and understand.
* It cites every damn line with a source.
* It does not generate anything, but shows information.
* [It does not harm anyone.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deaths_linked_to_chatbots)
* It is not designed to be addictive.
* It is deterministic and returns same data with same input each and every time.

In short, it should be something like old Google was, but with natural language interpreter that I can ask a question with human terms, and it gives me the data and that's it. No "You're absolutely rights" or other sycophantic bullshit. Just fucking data. I don't want to chat with my computer, I want to ask it a question and it gives me data.

I would never use it to generate anything: That would mean I'm not in control, and I can't stand that sort of loss of control. I don't really drink alcohol because of the loss of control bothers me that much.

(I do like making procedural generation systems in games though, for example for levels.)

[I still feel the creative anger](#i-still-feel-the-creative-anger)
----------

Despite my above statements, I am still rather angry about it all. I buy a lot of art. I love commissioning people. I see programming as an artform. And artistic folk have been shafted by all the megacorps that have made these weapons against them. I am fucking livid about it to this day.

So, I try to aim that anger towards the megacorps. I don't like being angry towards other people and I find it accomplishes nothing. That's my view on it, anyway, you do you.

As I said above:

>
>
> But as of now, I will not bother with any of it.
>
>

The reason I have to do this disclaimer is that the moment I even try to make any sense openly around this stuff.. Both "AI" boosters and haters want me to jump off a bridge.

Your anger is valid emotion. But if you are aiming it towards someone who does not even do the things you dislike, what the fuck? You're only making your cause look bad.

[Positive about programming](#positive-about-programming)
----------

I will end this post with a positive note.

I've been working a lot on the [KDE Union](https://invent.kde.org/plasma/union) stuff. The QtWidgets side of it is no joke. The QStyle system is not well documented and it can get very difficult.

But by Akatosh it feels incredible to get it all working. There's no "AI" to copy paste code from, but just "I" and that's my own small brain doing things.

It has felt so empowering for me to get things working on that front. To see my work actually taking shape. My thoughts turning into code turning into results. Learning so many things every day I work on it.

I won't lie, and this may sound rough, but I have hated myself for most of my life due to how I have been treated in the past. I think I am slowly getting over it.. So that's why it has all felt even more so empowering to get things working myself. Games, KDE code, whatever it may be. It's my handiwork and it may not be the prettiest, it may not be perfect, but it's mine and it works! It can even create joy to others.

If I just replaced all that part with "AI" I don't think I would be very happy anymore.

Another thing is that I've ended up refactoring the code a lot. Sure I could use "AI" to make it fast and easy for me, but because I have gone through the codebase so many times now, I can pinpoint problems and changes much easier. Someone spots a bug, I can pretty quickly decipher where the bug might be, because my mental model of the codebase is so strong. And that also feels amazing. I can pretty much run the code in my head.

[Notes for juniors](#notes-for-juniors)
----------

So this makes me think about the juniors who are heavily relying on "AI" tools these days, either they are taught to do so, or just feel they're obligated to do so.

In my opinion, juniors should never use them for creation at least. Preferably not even for learning.

I am the type that learns best by doing. So that's why I avoid copy pasting anything from online when it comes to programming, but I look at the examples and make them work in my case. I *write* it all myself. Because that's when it attaches to my brain, it turns into knowledge. And then I can utilize that knowledge no matter what codebase I am working in. And I take notes, so they stay around with me.

So juniors, please, educate yourselves. Your brains will love that. You will have better time that way.

[Final thoughts](#final-thoughts)
----------

I think I will be still very critical cynic towards "AI" stuff. Maybe in 2036 they're something I can think about seriously to add to my toolbelt. Maybe at that point we have a tool that matches my criteria above. Or maybe it's gone by wayside and we look back at this time like wtf.

But I don't want to keep feeding more anger to the world anymore.

Being constantly angry is in my opinion the way how we all will lose..

And I only have so much hair left.
