*I'm back in geminispace*

Hey there, just small update that I've been doing a separate tiny blog in [geminispace](http://geminiquickst.art/).

You can find it in here if you also use gemini protocol: [gemini://akselmo.dev/](gemini://akselmo.dev/)

I also have a tinylog there that is basically gemini version of fedi.

We'll see how long that stays around, I just wanted a place to experiment in, as this blog feels very rigid to do so in.

On the other hand, I may just say "who cares" and experiment more in here too, as it's my blog! :P But I do like keeping things more stable in here.

I also enjoy the gemini posting way more, as posting there is literally modifying text files and pushing to git. I have similar setup for my blog, but it's a bit more convoluted as there's static site building etc in the middle.

Anyway I ramble about the above more in gemini space, if you're interested. :)
