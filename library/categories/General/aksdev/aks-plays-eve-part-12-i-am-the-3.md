Really short and spooky one this time.

I was roaming about lowsec in my lil Hookbill as usual, and found a Firetail in a complex. So I warped in.

They neutralized my capacitor completely (in short: i ran out of energy) so I was having real scary time keeping my repairing and modules running. I tried to disrupt their turret range and keep distance, but eventually my ship was just so completely out of capacitor that I just floated around in very slow speed.

Luckily my rockets still kept firing, so I could go down fighting.

Except.. I survived.

![My ship with only 3% hull left, basically exploded](/assets/images/Screenshot_20260522_011712.png)

I was on the edge of my seat, though I had already kind of accepted defeat. But then I noticed I was alive and their ship exploded instead.

* [https://zkillboard.com/kill/135654072/](https://zkillboard.com/kill/135654072/)

What a fight. What a glorious, absolutely fun fight. I'm still riding the adrenaline high.

I kept roaming a bit more after but it's getting late, so more next time. But this game keeps giving me moments like these. They're delightful!
