Not much to share, but I had two nice fights. And I saw cool things in the game.

Was just roaming about with my Hookbill as usual, until I spot a Catalyst Navy Issue. Ended up being rather tight fight, but I managed to stay in right amount of range to eventually win the fight: They were using railguns, so using the tracking disruption here was really the key.

* [https://zkillboard.com/kill/135446231/](https://zkillboard.com/kill/135446231/)

My ship was in bad shape afterwards..

![My ship, flying, on fire](/assets/images/Screenshot_20260512_225610.png)

So I took it out to repairs. I had never seen this before though!

![Little drones repairing my ship!](/assets/images/Screenshot_20260512_225719.png)

Look at em! Cute little drones! So they repaired my ship and I kept on roaming. Too bad all their work was for naught, since I spotted a Thrasher Fleet Issue. They got me down pretty quick, I should have used range tracking disruption instead of tracking speed, I think.

* [https://zkillboard.com/kill/135446604/](https://zkillboard.com/kill/135446604/)

Well, enough excitement for tonight, I'm super tired so I'm surprised I managed to even get that first kill.

More later, whenever!
