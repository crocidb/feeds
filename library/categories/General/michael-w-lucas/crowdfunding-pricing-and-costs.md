I’m preparing to launch the [Twisted Presents Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/mwlucas/twisted-presents/) at last. The US economy has gone wrong lately, for obvious reasons. Costs for printing and shipping have increased. We’re not quite in a [capital strike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_strike), but it’s dang near. I needed to revisit costs and prices for this book.

I took notes as I did so. If you want to do crowdfunding, here’s my approach. This, other stuff from that site, tidbits from my blog, and assorted other detritus will probably form part of a long-requested “How I Make A Living Writing” book.

Those notes are on the [Tilted Windmill Press Knowledge Base](https://kb.tiltedwindmillpress.com/doku.php?id=pricing_crowdfunding).

The hardest lesson I had to learn when I started writing professionally? “If someone puts a dollar in your hand, take it.”
