Me, a few days ago: Hey, Penguicon has some great Guests of Honor lined up! Not a dud in the bunch.

[Then they posted this.](https://www.facebook.com/permalink.php?story_fbid=pfbid0Db2sMc8bhGG1iaSxbxApXk8djYfzmQoJmqWF2GHYDD5tA87bUYF5Atgi9VdoCaiEl&id=61569305640468)

Looks like I’m going to be a Guest of Honor at [Penguicon 2027](https://2027.penguicon.org/). I was previously a Penguicon GoH in 2013, so obviously they didn’t learn their lesson. The con runs April 8-11 2027 so if you’re in Detroit, do sign up. Other guests include Dr Rob Pyatt, [Jennifer Brozek](https://jenniferbrozek.com), [John Chu](https://www.johnchu.net/), [Phenderson Djéli Clark](https://pdjeliclark.com/), and [Seanan McGuire](https://www.seananmcguire.com/). With heavy hitters like this, I’m not sure why they need me?

Perhaps it’s just that if you give too many talks at Penguicon, they make you sit at the front of the class.
