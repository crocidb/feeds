I wrote a guest post for [The Sprout](https://calgaryvegansociety.com/the-sprout/), the blog of the [Calgary Vegan Society](https://calgaryvegansociety.com/), about navigating social eating situations as a vegan. The post covers:

* Private vs. social eating challenges
* The social importance of shared meals
* Being firm and confident in your dietary choices
* Practical strategies for restaurants, family dinners, office potlucks, and travel
* How to maintain your values while staying connected to community

You can read the full post on [The Sprout](https://calgaryvegansociety.com/2026/08/07/a-social-guide-to-being-vegan-in-a-meat-eaters-world/).
