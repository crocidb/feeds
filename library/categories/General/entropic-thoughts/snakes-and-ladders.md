 It is common for promotional pamphlets aimed at children to contain a themed variant of *snakes and ladders*. This is the “game” where the players toss a die, move as many places as the die says, and then some spots on the board have an event that send the player forward, or back, or give them an extra toss or whatever.

 The theme is typically related to whatever the promoter wants to promote, but styled for children. I get the appeal – for children. They can probably imagine they’re really travelling along the path! In the variant pictured below, there’s forest and water and tunnels and dogs and everything!
