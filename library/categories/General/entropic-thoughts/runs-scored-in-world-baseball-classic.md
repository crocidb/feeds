 In the Spring 2026 edition of the Metaculus Cup one question concerned the number of runs in the knockout stage of the World Baseball Classic (WBC) in 2026.

 The community predicted a mean of just under 70 runs, with a spread of 60–80. My prediction was 60 runs, with a spread of 42–80. The actual number ended up being 59 runs – nearly exactly what I predicted. Let’s see how that was done.

 First, there’s a sloppy way of making a quick guess. It’s not very interesting, but it’s a good baseline. Once we have that, we’ll dig into modeling counts on a more granular level, and that’s when it gets interesting. Those techniques can be used for all sorts of counts, not just sports prediction!

 I don’t know anything about baseball. I don’t even know what a run is, so I had my research assistant count how many runs were in a few of the previous WBCs.
