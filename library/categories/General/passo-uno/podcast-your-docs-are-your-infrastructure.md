I recently was a guest on The Stack Overflow’s Podcast today, talking about docs (of course), the Vale prose linter, job titles, automation, LLMs, and much more. You can listen to the entire episode here or [in the SO blog](https://stackoverflow.blog/2024/11/26/your-docs-are-your-infrastructure/), or [read the transcript](https://the-stack-overflow-podcast.simplecast.com/episodes/your-docs-are-your-infrastructure/transcript). Getting docs closer to developers was one of my personal goals this year, and I think these kind of appearances might help. We need to be where developers are.
