[Code linters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lint_(software)) support developers by catching errors and stylistic issues in code, such as bad formatting or keywords in the wrong places. The term comes from [lint traps](https://greenlivingideas.com/2014/08/14/clean-dryers-lint-trap-duct-screen/) in dryer machines, which capture the tiny bits of fiber that separate from cloth.
