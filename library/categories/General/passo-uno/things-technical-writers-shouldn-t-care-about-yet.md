Strategy, Michael Porter [wrote](https://www.amazon.com/Strategy-Creating-Shared-Michael-Porter/dp/1633699161), is choosing what *not* to do. Now, the problem with knowledge work such as the one tech writers carry out is that it’s full of things that *seem* to require equally important, time-consuming decisions. While engaging in lengthy disquisitions might be alluring, endlessly combing the Zen garden of theory doesn’t solve the basic problem of the [docs hierarchy of needs](https://passo.uno/docs-hierarchy-of-needs/), which is *writing the damn docs* and making sure they’re accurate and useful.
