Docs are a product. Contributing to them is among the finest forms of product engagement. Bystanders can become builders and authors: They contribute a verse so that the powerful play can go on. They cease being the product to become the owners of the product narrative. And in this AI age, where docs [matter more than ever](https://passo.uno/from-tech-writers-to-ai-context-curators/), users who write can steer the future of products.
