Three years ago I published a [high fantasy map of technical writing](https://passo.uno/tech-writing-fantasy-map/). The world has moved quite a bit since then. AI happened, and with it a darkness has crept in from the north, one that feeds on the daily pessimism of writers that feel cornered.

I felt like an update was long overdue, so I sat down, fired up an editor and created a new version. It’s a map of contested territory, and we’re still on it, waiting for someone to check the advance of [natural stupidity](https://passo.uno/reconsider/). Can you find yourself in it?
