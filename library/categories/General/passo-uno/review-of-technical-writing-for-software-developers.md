Just around the time I was [complaining](https://passo.uno/best-tech-writing-books/) about the scarcity of books on technical writing, I got a copy of [Technical Writing for Software Developers](https://amzn.to/3XcyvaD) by Chris Chinchilla, a regular of the Write the Docs community. Delighted by the chance of reading a book from one of the sharpest pens in technical writing, I set aside some time to read through the nine chapters and write a review. The book taught me little I didn’t already know – Chris and I use the same [tools and methods](https://passo.uno/favorite-tech-writing-tools/). The biggest value I extracted from TWfSD is its reflection of the times: it spurred lots of thoughts on the future of our profession and how we promote it.
