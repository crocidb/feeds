Something I learned today from a coworker: if you turn your monitor sideways, [subpixel anti-aliasing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subpixel_rendering) gets completely broken. This isn’t as much of an issue on today’s high-dpi displays, but for anything lower than a 4k screen, the effect can be noticeable.
