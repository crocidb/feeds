On media being incompetent at depicting young people[#](#on-media-being-incompetent-at-depicting-young-people)
==========

Have you noticed that a lot of modern contemporary media is utterly incompetent at portraying a younger generation or even just the current realities of life?

In my current transient fixation on content I used to consume as a kid, I stumbled upon an old sitcom from 2007. One that was de-facto the most popular sitcom in Russia for a good couple of years, probably until 2012-2013. I remember it being genuinely enjoyable, relatable and fun. While looking back at it, like with all such things, there's a bit of that old media cringe factor and dated humour, but it's still surprisingly just a wholesome, nice piece of media. I'm much more media literate than I used to be as a kid, and I can recognize when something is good without letting nostalgia bias me too hard.

The thing that really strums my feeling strings is the fact that it was a brand-new original concept, as original as a sitcom could reasonably get. Simply because of that concept it was relatable to a *lot* of kids, teens and adults, alike. I think I could honestly call that era the golden age of entertainment in Russia, but rose-tinted nostalgia glasses do their job.

It doesn't necessarily matter, but I'll talk about the contents anyway. The protagonist is a middle-aged father of five daughters. His wife left him, his job is going to shit, the classics. The daughters are relatable to everyone growing up back then, and I mean *everyone* was watching this -- of course you would! When you don't have streaming, or an endless stream of hilariously diverse content to destroy our dwindling attention spans, you watch the telly.

I think you can see where I'm going with this - that sitcom is an absolute time capsule and it nails on the head the culture of growing up back then. It's just really good at staying true. And there were so many different pieces of media all around the world back then that were just really relatable. To an extent, it formed culture since teens would take parts of the characters' personalities on.

Recently, I discovered that it had been spun off. Relatively normal sitcom stuff, the teens grew up and one of them has kids of their own and whatnot. But... holy shit. It's all the same shit you see everywhere now. I didn't expect it to be a masterpiece, or even good, but it highlighted a really shitty pattern. Privately, I routinely say it's become hard to find and create new media, which is why we get a lot of sequels, reboots and nostalgia right now in all areas of creativity. I can confidently say there is nothing original about this reboot. There is nothing relatable about it to a young person, too. It's devoid of any even remotely interesting stories. Try and guess what the kids' defining character traits are? You could do it in just a couple attempts. TLDR: nothing original. Wannabe content creator, copycat of the goth from previous iteration, stereotypical "hyper-woke" vegan social justice warrior and/or a libertarian corpo-rat - the classic false archetypes designed to make fun of minorities.

For context, none of those archetypes really exist, I'm sure anyone reading this knows this already. They aren't well written because they're not based in reality *at all, not even a little*. Consider the fact that in 2007 when the initial sitcom came out, a lot of teens, indeed, were parts of those factions, as portrayed, minus the exaggerated comedy. In 2025, you can apply "wannabe content creator" to anyone because it's ubiquitous and doesn't really entail anything. It's not a defining character trait, it's a footnote. All of these character traits are, if you can even call them that.

Call me too old, ridicule me for having not lived in the country for a while now, but honestly, I'm genuinely not that geriatric - I'm only 21 (at the time of writing). This slop is written by old people who have no idea what they are talking about. It's on the same level as "Oohhhhh these kids and their YouToks and TikTubes!", it's *that* bad.

And this is just one example! It keeps happening! Have you noticed? No piece of media portrays any of these archetypes well, because they don't exist! When have you seen a show that shoehorns in a content creator trope that isn't complete garbage? Look at the latest Megamind, or... literally, have you seen any good film where one of the characters is a raging vegan not for the sake of making fun of vegans, or a tiktoker not for the sake of audience self-inserts? No! Because it's just plain *bad writing*! I cringe every time I see a phone in a film, it's *never* done well.

At a certain point, I have to start thinking about the fact that humanity as a whole has lost a lot of individual personality in the last 1.5 decades. I honestly struggle to come up with a genuine character trope that could be both as candid and as strong as they were in the past. Everyone has sort of dissolved into a sea of averageness, and it's hard to stand out when you're *constantly* exposed to so many other people also trying to stand out. It makes me sad thinking about this, since it's not hard to make conclusions as to what has caused this, and it's even easier to say that it will not get better.
