Microsoft’s iPhone app for OneDrive has long supported creating three new document types via a big “+” button right in its file viewer: Excel, Word, PowerPoint. It [recently](https://bsky.app/profile/jontatum.com/post/3mtknwk6vhc26) came to my attention that they’ve added a fourth document type, and they even gave it real Microsoft-style “[they just have no taste](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UiOzGI4MqSU)” file icon. [Just look at it](https://daringfireball.net/misc/2026/08/onedrive-fourth-horseman.png). The whole world’s coming to an end, Mallory.
