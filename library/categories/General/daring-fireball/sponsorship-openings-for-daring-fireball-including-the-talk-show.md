Sponsorships have continued selling briskly, [of late](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/03/06/daring-fireball-weekly-sponsorship-openings). Knock on wood. As of yesterday, the next opening [on the schedule](/feeds/sponsors/) wasn’t until the last week of December, and the Q1 2027 schedule is already filling up. ~~However, due to some schedule rejiggering, *next week* is now open.~~ *Update:* OK, next week has been sold, but Christmas week remains open. That’s a good week at DF. I keep posting and readers are often more bored than usual. Also a good week to promote games.

If you’ve got a product or service you think would be of interest to DF’s audience of people obsessed with high quality and good design, [just email me](<mailto:sponsors@daringfireball.net?subject=Weekly Feed Sponsorship>) — especially if you can act quick for next week’s opening. I’m also booking sponsorships for Q1 and Q2 2027, and over half of those weeks are already sold. The rate for the two remaining weeks in 2026 is $11,000, and the rate for open weeks in 2027 is $12,500.

The sponsorship schedule for The Talk Show also has openings, including for the next few episodes in November and December. The general rule of thumb is that sponsorship spots on The Talk Show cost one-third the rate for the weekly spots on DF. I’m happy to work out deals a little lower than that for first-time sponsors. If you’ve got a product or service you’d like to hear pitched on America’s favorite three-star podcast, [get in touch](<mailto:sponsors@daringfireball.net?subject=Sponsoring The Talk Show>).
