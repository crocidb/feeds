Andrew Webster, The Verge (gift link):

>
>
> How do you follow a game like *Grand Theft Auto V*? Thirteen years ago, Rockstar’s crime-fueled epic went on to redefine what an open-world game could be and became an enormous success, selling more than 230 million copies across three generations of game consoles. It sits just behind *Tetris* and *Minecraft* on the list of the best-selling games of all time, and [its lucrative online mode](https://www.afp.com/en/grand-theft-auto-online-top-video-games-roleplay-cash-cow) has helped it remain popular for well over a decade.
>
>
>
> That level of success has allowed Rockstar to take its time with a sequel, which has further increased the hype around the next *GTA*. A series of [major leaks](https://www.theverge.com/games/984959/gta-vi-leaks-rockstar-games-official-statement-heartbreaking) and [delays](https://www.theverge.com/news/816005/grand-theft-auto-vi-gta-g-delayed-november-2026) only exacerbated this. At a time when [much of the video game industry is struggling](https://www.theverge.com/column/1022402/new-zelda-and-gta-represent-a-games-industry-that-no-longer-exists), [the next edition of *GTA* stands alone](https://www.theverge.com/column/983410/grand-theft-auto-vi-exists-in-its-own-universe), a cultural moment so big ([almost](https://www.theverge.com/games/1025415/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-review-nintendo-switch-2)) [everybody has been forced to get out of the way](https://www.theverge.com/entertainment/944229/grand-theft-auto-6-release-calendar-summer-game-fest). The buildup to *Grand Theft Auto VI*’s launch is unlike anything the game industry has ever seen.
>
>
>
> Even more remarkable? The game was actually worth the wait.
>
>

The entire tech world is celebrating something today. On one side (avarice, hype) the Anthropic IPO. On the other (fun, mayhem) GTA 6. I briefly watched my son play last night after it came out at midnight. It looks incredible and fun as hell.
