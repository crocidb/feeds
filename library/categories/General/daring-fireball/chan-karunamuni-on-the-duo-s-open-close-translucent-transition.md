Chan Karunamuni, human interface designer at Apple, on X:

>
>
> iPhone Duo is here! Excited to have worked on the design of our open/close transition. It’s designed to make the two displays feel like one seamless experience by perceptually bending the focal plane, not the content, so that it feels like opening a window onto a singular view.
>
>

Karunamuni thanks a few engineers by name, which is always good (and for Apple employees, far too rare) to see.

[Johnnie Manzari](https://daringfireball.net/search/johnnie+manzari), HI designer formerly of Apple, now at Google DeepMind, [retweeting Karunamuni](https://x.com/johnnie/status/2113643318165082486):

>
>
> The second I saw that transition I thought “here’s the next ‘change the whole industry’ design from Chan”.
>
>

Previous such raise-the-bar designs that Karunamuni led or played a major hand in include [the Dynamic Island](https://x.com/chan_k/status/1570795751764295682) in [2022](https://x.com/chan_k/status/1571207749677219840), [the “shockwave moment”](https://x.com/chan_k/status/1703820400902431077) when you put two iPhones together to initiate AirDrop or NameDrop in 2023, and iOS 18’s [cool-ass Flashlight beam UI](https://x.com/chan_k/status/1835729051178451013) in 2024. He also gave Steve Lemay [this full-throated public endorsement](https://x.com/chan_k/status/1996825927561658854) upon his promotion to lead all of Apple HI design upon Alan Dye’s departure for Meta last December.
