Apple Newsroom:

>
>
> Apple today announced the opening of Apple Music Hall, a brand-new state-of-the-art live music venue in London’s storied Battersea Power Station, designed to connect artists and fans through bespoke, intimate performances unlike anywhere else.
>
>

Apple might be the most interesting architecture company in the world this century. They have a core look and feel they apply to their own facilities built from the ground up (e.g., Apple Park, [certain](https://www.apple.com/retail/michiganavenue/) flagship retail stores), but they also have [tremendous respect](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2018/11/apple-champs-elysees-to-open-on-famed-parisian-avenue-this-sunday/) for [historic buildings](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2019/05/apple-carnegie-library-opens-saturday-in-washington-dc/) they take over.

The balconies in Apple Music Hall are bronze, but the lighting (at least in Apple’s photos) is so warm that the balconies look a little like giant Mac Minis.
