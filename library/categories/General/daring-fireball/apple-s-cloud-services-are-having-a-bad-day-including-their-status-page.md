Juli Clover, MacRumors:

>
>
> Apple Accounts and [iCloud](https://www.macrumors.com/guide/icloud/) sign-ins appear to be experiencing issues, according to multiple social media reports and [Apple’s System Status page](https://www.apple.com/support/systemstatus/).
>
>

I’ve been seeing weird problems in multiple apps (Photos, Notes, and System Settings) on one of my Macs, on which I’m trying to install the 27.3 beta. Photos was reporting that it had just completed syncing, but it was missing a bunch of recent photos (but not in chronological order). Notes was missing changes from other devices. And System Settings wasn’t showing me the choice to install beta OS updates. It just wasn’t there, and tricks [like this](https://robservatory.com/how-to-enable-the-beta-updates-feature-in-macos-13-4/) didn’t fix it. I worried the whole machine had gone bad, because when I checked Apple’s [online Status page](https://www.apple.com/support/systemstatus/), everything was green across the board. Turns out the Status page wasn’t working either. It is now, and shows outages for App Store, Apple Music, AppleCare on Device, and iCloud Account & Sign In.

**Update:** [Siri AI is down for me too](https://daringfireball.net/misc/2026/11/siri-ai-not-available.png).

**Update 2:** Seems like most/all services are back up, but during the day, [even Apple Retail went down](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-17/apple-works-to-restore-systems-after-update-caused-outage?accessToken=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJzb3VyY2UiOiJTdWJzY3JpYmVyR2lmdGVkQXJ0aWNsZSIsImlhdCI6MTc5NDk0MzY3MSwiZXhwIjoxNzk1NTQ4NDcxLCJhcnRpY2xlSWQiOiJUT05VU0VLSkg2VjQwMCIsImJjb25uZWN0SWQiOiJDNEVEQ0FFMUZBMDU0MEJFQTI0QTlGMjExQzFFOTA4MCJ9.7VLB930QAB1IP1waOtG-o9MW1nu2HukjCXbXkm3p6VI).
