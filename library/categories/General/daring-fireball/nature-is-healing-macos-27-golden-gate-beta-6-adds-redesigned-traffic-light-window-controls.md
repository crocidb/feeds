Nice. But these changes can’t improve fast enough for me — I am impatient. The MacOS user interface had been in a slow, steady, sad, no-fun decline for years, and then, a year ago, the bottom fell out with the disaster of 26 Tahoe.

But *all* the changes in 27 Golden Gate are in the right direction. Golden Gate is not a mixed bag. Designers with taste and talent are clearly [back at the helm](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/08/12/nature-is-healing). And I love that they’re still pushing UI and [icon](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/08/10/ios-27-beta-5-icons/) changes in mid-August, entering [the home stretch](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/08/10/ios-27-beta-5) of the .0 beta cycle.
