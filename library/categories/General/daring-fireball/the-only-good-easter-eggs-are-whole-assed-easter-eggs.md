Marcin Wichary on the [original iPod easter egg](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/10/24/apple-music-ipod-404-page) on Apple Music’s website 404 page:

>
>
> I am usually not a fan of easter eggs — most of them are poorly made — but this might be the best iPod recreation I’ve seen, and it’s an interesting thing to study:
>
>
>
> * I think it generally covers all the modes etc.
> * (with small exceptions) the typography looks accurate,
> * it has the little “haptic” beeps,
> * it has proper acceleration and the scrollbar,
> * it has a little music library with various playlists,
> * I have only noticed a few small bugs.
>
>

It looks real, sounds real, feels real. Don’t bother with your easter egg idea if you’re going to half-ass it.

**Update:** The original iPod itself had a great easter egg: a hidden version of the classic game Breakout. Apple’s new easter egg web-page iPod [includes the hardware iPod easter egg](https://www.digitaltrends.com/phones/apple-music-is-hiding-a-playable-ipod-easter-egg-and-brick-game/). Just go to Settings → About and hold down the center button for 5 seconds. An easter egg in the easter egg. That’s how you do it.
