Federico Viticci:

>
>
> Don’t like that the iPhone Duo doesn’t auto-lock when you close it?
>
>
>
> You can fix it with this simple Shortcuts automation.
>
>

Two steps:

1. A conditional for when the iPhone is `Closed`.
2. Lock the screen.

I [mentioned in my review](<https://daringfireball.net/2026/10/the_iphone_duo#:~:text=done with a book>) that I kept closing the Duo expecting it to lock, like when closing a book (or a laptop, for that matter). And it didn’t occur to me that I could simply add this feature via Shortcuts. That’s cool. But I also mentioned in my review that I wouldn’t want that to happen every time I closed it. Sometimes you do want to just switch from the wonderful inner display to the miserable little outer display, and the last thing you want when you do that is to need to re-authenticate for the hundredth time of the day with Touch ID. It was just a wrong instinct I had, and I broke myself of that instinct after about a week or so of full-time Duo use. I stopped expecting it to lock on close. So while I think this shortcut is worth pointing out — and I especially think it’s a good reminder to think about Shortcuts when you find yourself wishing for features that aren’t in Settings — I recommend trying to break yourself of this habit if you have a Duo and find yourself with the same wrong close-to-lock instinct I initially had.

**Update:** [Stephen Robles posted a clever version](https://mastodon.social/@stephenrobles/117502280150643367) that only locks the Duo on close if it’s on the home screen; if any app is open, it stays unlocked. That’s much more tempting to try. (He includes that one in [a whole video full of Duo tips](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GVt5_g4gE-o) on YouTube.)
