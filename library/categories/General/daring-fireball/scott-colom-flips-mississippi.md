Emine Yücel, reporting for TPM:

>
>
> Mississippi prosecutor Scott Colom officially defeated incumbent Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith (R-MS) Wednesday night. Colom will be the first Democrat to represent Mississippi in the Senate since 1982 and he will also become the state’s first Black senator in nearly 150 years.
>
>

150 years is, needless to say, a long time. The crazy part about that is that [Mississippi has the highest proportion of black citizens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Americans_in_Mississippi) in the entire country (38 percent).

Colom, by all accounts, ran a fantastic campaign, with little to no support from the national Democratic Party because almost no one imagined Mississippi — the deepest of the deep South — might turn blue. [This commercial Colom ran](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nga2Q207-58) is one of the best I’ve ever seen. Not just best *political* commercials. I mean it’s one of the best 30-second spots ever made. It’s perfect. I just keep watching it over and over.

**Update:** I stand corrected on the national support. [The WSJ ran a story back in September](https://www.wsj.com/politics/elections/mississippi-election-democrats-fundraising-ec77cb8a?st=KixCU4) — six weeks before the election — on Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer raising funds for Colom amongst big Democratic donors. Schumer smelled a winner.
