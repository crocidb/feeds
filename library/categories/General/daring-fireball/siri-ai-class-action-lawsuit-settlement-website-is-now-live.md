Joe Rossignol, MacRumors:

>
>
> In May, Apple [agreed to pay $250 million](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/05/apple-class-action-siri-lawsuit-settlement/) to settle a [U.S. class action lawsuit](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/03/20/apple-intelligence-siri-lawsuit/) over Siri AI’s delayed launch, and the [settlement website](https://www.smartphoneaisettlement.com/) is now live.
>
>
>
> Apple will pay an estimated $25 per eligible iPhone to eligible customers, who can submit a claim via the settlement website between September 21 and December 21 of this year. The exact payment may be higher or lower than $25 depending on the total number of valid claims submitted and other factors, with the maximum amount set at $95.
>
>
>
> Apple denies all of the allegations made in the lawsuit. Apple agreed to the settlement to avoid the costs and risks of a trial.
>
>

I went ahead and [filled it out](https://www.smartphoneaisettlement.com/) for the iPhone 16 Pro I purchased in September 2024. I’d call it borderline whether it was worth my time for $25. It was a pain in the ass every step of the way.

For the eventual payment, your choices to receive the dough are PayPal, Venmo, ACH (bank transfer), or check. I tried to get it via PayPal and it just wouldn’t complete the connection to my PayPal account. After wasting time on a few tries, I tried ACH next, and the web form had pasting disabled on the fields for the routing and account numbers. Ridiculous. I was able to get around that to paste the numbers in, because I use [StopTheMadness](https://underpassapp.com/StopTheMadness/). But most people don’t. The only reason I can think of that they try to block pasting on those fields is to make it difficult.
