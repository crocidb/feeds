Steven Nelson, writing for The New York Post:

>
>
> President Trump claimed Monday that Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang could have become a leader in China’s artificial intelligence industry if he hadn’t been reinstated to the US firm — telling CNBC in a phone interview that the 63-year-old has “got a little blood there” despite being born in Taiwan.
>
>
>
> “If we didn’t do this, I could see China coming in and saying to Jensen, ‘Hey, how would you like to come to China?’ — He’s got a little [Chinese] blood in him. He’s got a little blood there — ‘How would you like to come to China and head up Huawei?’” Trump told CNBC in a phone interview, during which he called Huang, 63, a “national treasure” despite his affair with an alleged Chinese spy.
>
>
>
> “If Jensen was angry enough, if he was treated badly, maybe he could do that.”
>
>

This was during Trump’s [50-minute phoner on CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/trump-nvidia-jensen-huang.html). [As I noted](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/11/23/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang) yesterday, on his Truth Social blog, [Trump wrote](https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/117666141823845971):

>
>
> On a personal note, I have gotten to know Jensen, and he is a Great Patriot and National Treasure.
>
>

Think about how perverse Trump’s worldview is that he simultaneously declares that Jensen Huang is a “Great Patriot and National Treasure” *and* that Huang would be justified in moving to China to help the United States’ geopolitical archrival in the AI arms race “if he was treated badly”.
