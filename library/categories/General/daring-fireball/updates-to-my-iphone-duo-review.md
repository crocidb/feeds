Nothing major, but since publishing at 7:00pm last night, I’ve made a few additions and changes to my iPhone Duo review. I’m calling them out here, so that if you’ve already read it, you can see the changes. If I make more, I’ll add them to the top.

Regarding the camera, added and/or clarified the following (in **bold**):

>
>
> As for the camera itself, it’s terrific. It’s clearly not of the same caliber as the iPhone 18 Pro, but it’s good. The technical specs of the Duo’s main 1×/2× camera are nearly identical to those of the year-ago iPhone Air, and [I love the iPhone Air’s camera](https://daringfireball.net/2026/09/the_iphone_air_and_iphone_17_pro). If anything, the Duo’s camera should be better, because it gets the year-better A20 Pro imaging pipeline. Which, notably, brings support for the same new Photographic Styles, including textures like film grain, that were [introduced with the iPhone 18 Pro](https://daringfireball.net/2026/09/the_iphone_air_and_iphone_17_pro). **However, the Duo is not “Pro” — it does not get camera features like “Pro controls” (variable lens aperture, shutter speed, white balance, histogram), [Apple Reference Image](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/09/15/apple-reference-image), spatial video, ProRES (video or RAW), or Apple Log. Apple’s “shot with iPhone” keynotes will continue to use iPhones Pro.**
>
>
>
> Also, notably different from the Air, the Duo has an ultrawide 0.5× second camera lens. This brings both the ultrawide perspective *and* macro photography. Like the Air, the Duo’s main 1× camera has a minimum focus distance of 15cm. That’s closer than the 17 and 18 Pro cameras (20cm), but there are a lot of times you want to snap photos of receipts or documents closer than 15cm. **With the Air, you can’t focus closer than 15cm, because it only has that one camera. With the Duo, you can focus on subjects down to just 2cm away, using the 0.5× lens or the 1× lens in “macro” mode (which switches to the ultrawide lens but sensor-crops to the 1× field of view).**
>
>

Regarding the security of “Unlock with Apple Watch”, I added this note:

>
>
> (It’s worth noting that the wrist tap on your watch for this gives you a button to tap to lock your iPhone immediately — it’s not just a notification that it happened, it presents you with an action to undo it.)

Added this note (in **bold**) about it occasionally rotating (uncomfortably) in pocket, because it happened to me again twice today:

>
>
> It does not go into or out of my jeans pocket smoothly. **Sometimes, in my pocket, it rotates 90 degrees, which is uncomfortable.** Folded up it never stops feeling heavy. It *is* heavy, compared to other iPhones.
