Charlie Warzel, writing for The Atlantic (gift link):

>
>
> The rationale for this rally is that Huang is the central figure of the AI boom — that Nvidia, which powers the AI economy and props it up with the company’s investments, is basically an exoskeleton around Huang. With him, AI progress and U.S. tech leadership can continue apace; without him, the music stops, investor confidence falters, and the AI bubble pops. On November 9, I [wrote](https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/2026/11/jensen-huang-chinese-spy/689357/) that, in the eyes of Silicon Valley and Trump, Huang was too big to fail. For 11 days — the window between Huang’s departure and his return — it looked like I was wrong and the shameless era of *lol nothing matters* in American politics and technology was winding down. Nope!
>
>
>
> The professed logic of those calling for Huang’s return was [nonsensical](https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/2026/11/silicon-valley-model-collapse/689505/). The venture capitalists and shareholders argued that American dominance in the AI race against China depended on Huang’s ability to do whatever he pleased. This impunity included allowing Nvidia leeway to potentially skirt U.S. export controls and get chips into China, and sleeping with an alleged Chinese intelligence asset. You might be forgiven for missing the genius of the argument.
>
>

The other side of this coin is that Huang’s personal dishonesty — not just to his family but [to the entire Nvidia company](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/11/08/huang-betrayed-nvidia) (which, I guess, [includes his family](https://www.businessinsider.com/madison-huang-growing-influence-at-nvidia-jensen-daughter-2026-11)) — and disregard for U.S. national security policy *do not matter*. It’s one thing to believe (as I do) that a romantic indiscretion shouldn’t necessarily be cause for firing. It’s another thing entirely to argue that integrity doesn’t matter.

Even just putting aside the fact that Donald Trump is singularly responsible — in an objectively unprecedented way — for Nvidia’s board reinstating Huang, the whole thing is part and parcel with Trumpism itself. Should a single extramarital affair rule someone out from elected office? No. But does a [lifetime of lecherous behavior](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2020/09/06/trump-cohen-the-act), an entire [string](https://www.nickiswift.com/913028/the-real-reason-marla-maples-and-donald-trump-divorced/) of [failed](https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/was-donald-trump-accused-of-sexual-assault-prior-to-the-2016-election/) marriages, and a [documented long-term friendship with the world’s most infamous pedophile](https://www.wsj.com/us-news/law/trump-jeffrey-epstein-letter-birthday-book-analysis-0bbeeaf6?st=i3v3VX) suggest a man might be fundamentally unfit for leadership?
