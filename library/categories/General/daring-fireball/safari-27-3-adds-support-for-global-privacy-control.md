> There are a whole bunch of features and fixes coming to Safari with Apple’s OS 27.3 update. One that is not in the [beta release notes](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/safari-release-notes/safari-27_3-release-notes) but appears nevertheless is support for [Global Privacy Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Privacy_Control#Legal_status). This new standard is effectively a [replacement for Do Not Track](https://pxlnv.com/linklog/rip-dnt-firefox/) with legal muscle that [should](https://pxlnv.com/linklog/webxray-opt-out-audit/) permit you to indicate your tracking preference one time and then, theoretically, never again see a cookie consent banner.
