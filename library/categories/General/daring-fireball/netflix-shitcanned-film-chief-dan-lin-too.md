Anthony D’Alessandro, reporting for Deadline:

>
>
> Dan Lin is out as film boss at Netflix in a move that has been largely speculated for weeks. Insiders at the streamer have denied that Lin and even Bela Bajaria were on the chopping block, but here we are.
>
>
>
> Lin is meeting with his feature staff Monday morning for a post-mortem after the news. Per sources close to the situation, a successor to Lin won’t be named until the arrival of [Peter Rice, who is taking over Netflix’s Chief Content Officer role](https://deadline.com/2026/10/netflix-peter-rice-chief-content-officer-1237158809/) from Bajaria.
>
>
