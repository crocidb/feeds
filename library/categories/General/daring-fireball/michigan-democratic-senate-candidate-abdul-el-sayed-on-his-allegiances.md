And he posted it on X, where it’s garnered 9 million views. [He posted it on Bluesky too](https://bsky.app/profile/abdulelsayed.bsky.social/post/3my4gocauzs2x), if you’d prefer that. That this dope Thomas Teepell was a Republican operative whose stunt blew up in his face, Wile E. Coyote style, exemplifies that.
