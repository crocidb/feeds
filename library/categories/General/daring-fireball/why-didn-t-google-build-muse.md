MG Siegler, writing at Spyglass:

>
>
> The number one response I’ve gotten to [my thoughts on Meta’s Muse personal assistant agent](https://spyglass.org/metas-amusing-muse/) is some variation of “I will never trust Meta with my data.” But the number two response is arguably more interesting (and related): “Why didn’t Google build this?”
>
>
>
> The short answer to this is undoubtedly: they’re working on it. But the longer answer is probably more interesting here too.
>
>

Siegler’s take is basically that Google lacks focus. They’ve got multiple AI agent products, none of which are hitting — which as he points out [has been their decades-long problem with messaging apps](https://www.theverge.com/2021/6/21/22538240/google-chat-allo-hangouts-talk-messaging-mess-timeline). Say what you want about Meta as a company and Mark Zuckerberg as a leader, but they (and he) have focus. [I make fun of them](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2025/04/28/om-malik-facebook-rename) (and him) for going all-in on “the metaverse” only to lose all interest in it just a few years later, but to play devil’s advocate, that’s how focus works.
