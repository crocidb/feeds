Filipe Espósito, reporting for Engadget (after [a long stint](https://daringfireball.net/search/Filipe+Espósito) writing for 9to5Mac):

>
>
> The current iPhone 17e doesn’t have a camera bump — instead, the lens has a small protrusion and sits flush against the back of the phone, with the microphone and LED flash also embedded in cutouts directly in the rear glass. But according to CAD images seen by Engadget, Apple appears to be considering changing that with the iPhone 18e. The renders show an almost square camera bump measuring about 28 mm by 35 mm that houses the single rear camera, the LED flash and the rear microphone. It’s somewhat similar to the camera module on the M5 iPad Pro (which you can see in the image below).
>
>

The lens-only protrusion is one of the nicest things about the 16e and 17e — which [still take excellent photos](https://daringfireball.net/2026/03/the_iphone_17e). But the bump comes for all iPhone models eventually.
