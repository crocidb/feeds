Greg Joswiak on X:

>
>
> Over 200,000 apps are now optimized for iPhone Duo! That number DOUBLED in just over a week.
>
>

I’m back on my Duo review unit to test the new face unlock feature, and I’ve already noticed more apps are Duo optimized. Oddly enough, the first one I checked that still isn’t Duo-optimized is Apple’s own [Apple Store app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/apple-store/id375380948), which was last updated 3 months ago. Weird.

**Update:** The very next day, the Apple Store app was updated with full optimization for the Duo. But then the store’s [back end went down for several hours](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/11/17/apples-cloud-services-are-having-a-bad-day).
