In the wake of [Nitter/XCancel’s demise](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/08/25/xcancel-cease-and-desist), here’s a delightful new Safari extension from Zhenyi Tan (of And a Dinosaur fame):

>
>
> But sometimes people still post X content. Maybe you want to see [Gruber roast Google Design](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/08/12/google-design-pants-pissed). Maybe you want to see posts by [Federico Viticci](https://ticci.craft.me/explanation) who recently returned to X. So I made Litterbox, a Safari extension that opens x.com links in a popup. The idea is you open it, take a look, gag a little, then close the lid. [...]
>
>
>
> Litterbox doesn’t send your cookies when you open those popups, because of course. It uses the same API X uses for its website embeds. I don’t think they’ll kill the website embed feature. But if they do, it will be very funny.
>
>

I love it. I’ve got it installed even though I don’t avoid visiting X.com. It’s just faster and nicer to view tweets in Litterbox’s popup. And if I *do* want to proceed to X.com, there’s a link at the bottom of the popup. Perfect.
