Very fun. Alas, this easter egg isn’t present in MacOS 27, only iOS 27. But the MacOS 27 version of the Liquid Glass settings panel does have a drawing glitch [that makes the magnifying glass in the fake search field flicker terribly](https://daringfireball.net/misc/2026/09/no-moof-for-mac.mov), so Golden Gate does have that going for it.

**Update:** A bunch of readers have kindly reported that the Clarus easter egg is present in MacOS 27 Golden Gate, [illustrated on X by Andreas Storm](https://x.com/avstorm/status/2097787965552406756) and [at 512 Pixels by Stephen Hackett](https://512pixels.net/2026/09/liquid-glass-dogcow/). I still can’t get it to work on the MacBook Neo I’m running the latest beta of Golden Gate on though. One of the readers who sent a note says they don’t see the magnifying glass flicker, either.
