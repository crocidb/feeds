>
> Paul Crowley, a cybersecurity researcher at Anthropic, is listed as a manager of Red Means No LLC, which says that its parties are “designed to be spaces to … ignore the ‘nos’ of others.” Participants can use a safe word, “red,” to end an encounter but give “default consent” to “spanking,” “wrestling,” and “light pain” by entering the orgy, according to Red Means No’s [website](https://www.redmeansno.com/). > The orgies have been known to attract a disproportionate number of employees at Anthropic and OpenAI, a dynamic critics say creates professional pressure for women in AI to attend. The criticism spilled into public view last month after an independent AI researcher, John Wentworth, said that he “absolutely [does] upgrade” his assessment of AI safety experts “if I find out they go to [Red Means No],” adding that attendance was a “strong signal” of free thought.
