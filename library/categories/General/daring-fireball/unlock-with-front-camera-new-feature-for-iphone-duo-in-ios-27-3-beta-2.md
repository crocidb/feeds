Juli Clover, MacRumors:

>
>
> Apple today added a new option for unlocking the iPhone Duo using the front-facing camera, giving device users multiple ways to access their content. The feature is available in the second iOS 27.3 beta.
>
>
>
> The option to unlock with facial recognition is only available on the Lock Screen, and authentication via Touch ID is still needed for Apple Pay purchases, adding passwords and logins, and more. Unlocking with the camera requires a recent Touch ID or passcode authentication, and Touch ID is required again after about 45 minutes of inactivity.
>
>

This is a much-needed compromise for the lack of Face ID. Good to see Apple adapting quickly to real-world feedback.

There’s also another new Duo feature in 27.3 beta 2: Settings → Display & Brightness → Close to Lock. Turn that on and the Duo locks when you fold it — like, as I wrote [in my review](https://daringfireball.net/2026/10/the_iphone_duo), putting down a book.
