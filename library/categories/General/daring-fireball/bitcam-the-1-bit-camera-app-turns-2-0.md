10 years ago [I wrote about BitCam 1.0](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2016/06/10/bitcam):

>
>
> Gorgeous, unbelievably faithful one-bit camera app for iPhone done in the style of the original Mac. A lovely tribute to [Bill Atkinson’s remarkable dithering algorithm](https://www.google.com/search?q=bill+atkinson+dithering+algorithm). “Catnip for old-school Mac users,” [says John Siracusa](https://twitter.com/siracusa/status/740989106268831744). Check out [the fun recent-hire-at-Apple Chris Espinosa is having with it](https://twitter.com/cdespinosa/with_replies).
>
>

Originally a creation of The Iconfactory, BitCam is now in the good hands of [Héliographe](https://heliographe.studio/) (coiners of [the best icon-design zinger](https://www.threads.com/@heliographe.studio/post/DTeOwAykwQ1) ever). Version 2.0 has everything great about BitCam 1, and so much more. Just a wonderful ode to the original Mac, with [so much](https://mastodon.social/@heliographe_studio/117139385118413865) [extraordinary](https://mastodon.social/@heliographe_studio/117150743301554818) [attention](https://mastodon.social/@heliographe_studio/117149403714856951) to detail. Great fun on the iPhone (especially as a camera), and great fun on the Mac (especially to explore the details of its OG-style UI). Free to download on the App Store, and a one-time purchase of just $10 to unlock the full feature set.

If BitCam doesn’t speak to you, you’re not hooked up right.
