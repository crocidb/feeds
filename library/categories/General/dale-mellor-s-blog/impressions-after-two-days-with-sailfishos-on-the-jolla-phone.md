Impressions After Two Days With SailfishOS on the Jolla Phone
==========

Hot pros and cons of the new Jolla phone
----------

Dale Mellor [ https://khleedril.org/dale-mellor ](https://khleedril.org/dale-mellor)

 19th August, 2026

Iʼve been happily daily-driving my new Jolla phone for two days now, and here are my immediate thoughts.

This article is succeeded by the [two week](https://khleedril.org/blog/2026-09-05--jolla-impressions.html) and [two month](https://khleedril.org/blog/2026-10-22--jolla-impressions.html) impressions.

* + Signal totally works.
* - When the phone rings cold, it is hard to quickly pick up the call.
* + It takes a day to get used to the user experience, but it is really good.
  * Be aware of application menus hidden at the top of the screen.
  * Sometimes they may also be hidden at the bottom.
  * Swiping from the right does NOT go back in the history; swiping *to* the right (but not from the left edge!) kind of does that.
  * You close applications by swiping down from a top corner.

* - Folders on the pull-up application launcher need to show little icons of their contents.
* - The way running Android apps are shown on the home screen is a waste of space.
* + Puzzle games in the Jolla store are addictive for a while.
* - It is a bit Ubuntu-ish in that there is not much scope for personalisation.
* + The screen is fantastic.
