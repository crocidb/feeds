Impressions After Two Months With SailfishOS on the Jolla Phone
==========

 khleedril.org/2026-10-22--jolla-impressions

Dale Mellor [ https://khleedril.org/dale-mellor ](https://khleedril.org/dale-mellor)

 22nd October, 2026

Iʼve been happily daily-driving (this is my only phone nowadays) my Jolla Sailfish phone for two months, and here are my thoughts at this time.

This article follows the [two day](https://khleedril.org/blog/2026-08-19--jolla-impressions.html) and [two week](https://khleedril.org/blog/2026-09-05--jolla-impressions.html) impressions.

* #### As a phone/communicator ####

  * Its great strength is as a broadband router/gateway/access point/VPN end-point.

    With all the goodness of plain *ip*, *iptables* (unfortunately no *nft*) and *wg* commands, networking is infinitely configurable. I donʼt need any other network devices in my house when I have this; it does it all. (SSH access to the device is, of course, a cinch.)

  * It is a joy to use as a mobile phone.

    With the single exception of incoming *Signal* or *WhatsApp* calls, everything feels like a single swipe-tap away.

  * I still prefer the *events* page rather than the *running apps* page as the main home screen.
  * * I have one very niche Android application which doesnʼt like the Bluetooth stack (and one very niche app that does; it negotiates connection with, and controls, my hearing aids).
    * The *HereWeGo* navigation app sometimes suffers long unresponsive pauses when you are setting navigation up.
    * Otherwise everything works perfectly.

* #### As a portable computer ####

  * I havenʼt had time to explore this at all yet. Maybe Iʼll have something to say in the next impressions…
