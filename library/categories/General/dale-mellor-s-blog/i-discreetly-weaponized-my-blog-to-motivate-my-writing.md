I Discreetly Weaponized My Blog to Motivate My Writing
==========

A monthly word-count tracker hidden in plain sight
----------

 https://khleedril.org/blog/2026-11-06--weaponized-blog.html

 khleedril.org/2026-11-06--weaponized-blog

Dale Mellor [ https://khleedril.org/dale-mellor ](https://khleedril.org/dale-mellor)

 6th November, 2026

Have you spotted it yet?

I find that the only thing which motivates me to write is to constantly count words. The only targets meaningful to me when I sit down to write are when I tell myself that I am going to produce 100 or 1,000 words or even sometimes—when Iʼm desperate for progress—3,000 words. Any other kinds of targets, such as completion of chapters, deadlines, or actual page inches, do not give me the same level of impetus.

Since writing this blog in its current form (from the beginning of this year), I have come to realize that I read its pages very frequently, a bit like the speedometer in a car. It mostly amounts to procrastination, but the ostensible reason is to observe how the quality of my writing changes over time, and to look over old prose with a strangerʼs eyes and see if it is still clear and makes sense to someone who is not me. And it reassures me that I did not, God forbid, make any spelling mistakes or grammatical faux pas. I have to admit that I also get a deal of pleasure seeing that there is something (almost) tangible that I have built, that other people will read.

So, inspired by Axxuyʼs<sup><a href="#axxuy">[1]</a></sup> article *November Writing Month*<sup><a href="#nowrimo">[2]</a></sup>—wherein they use the *WritingMonth*<sup><a href="#writingmonth">[5]</a></sup> website, made by Amin<sup><a href="#amin">[6]</a></sup>—I decided I too really needed a writing progress bar in a prominent location to constantly remind me how I am doing this month, and thence to inspire me to write more.

I am not too interested in committing to a service which might go away at some point in the near future: lock-in to a proprietary system. It is not only a ‘big corp.’ problem, but small independents and individuals are equally likely to get bored of running something and close it down. So, as with everything on this web site, as much as possible I build from scratch. And I incorporate this new functionality right into this blog.

So, have you spotted it yet?

Did you notice that, where there used to be a simple and understated horizontal rule under the navigation icons, there is now a subtle ruler with bars at the head of this text? Iʼll let you into a little secret: the ruler is marked with the number of days in this month, the red bar indicates the number of days that have passed already, and the green bar shows my word-count for the month as a fraction of the target—the magnitude of which is for me to know and you to guess, i.e., MYOB: mind your own business.

The word count comes not only from the articles I write for this blog, but for progress on the *Broken Spark*<sup><a href="#spark">[3]</a></sup> novel and some other writings which are also MYOB.

Thus I see, at a glance, every time I look at any part of this blog, how my writing is progressing with respect to the progress of the calendar month.

It is something of a peeve of mine that so much in the independent blog space is given to articles about the art and craft of blogging, and this article is as guilty as any, but it does allow me to conclude with a serendipitous bit of meta-meta-writing: for the first time in history, thanks to the contents of this article and the innovation they report, the green progress bar has extended (at least momentarily) past the red bar!
