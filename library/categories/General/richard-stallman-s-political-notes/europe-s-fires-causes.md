 The main cause of Europe's record-breaking fires [is global heating](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/jul/30/fire-weather-eu-wildfires-data), due mainly to burning too much fossil fuel.

Scientists have identified a secondary cause: people's abandoning many of the old farms, which have since been overgrown by wild vegetation.

If [[the saboteur in chief](/glossary.html#trump)]'s war on the climate is not met with strong resistance, [things will only get worse](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2026/jul/30/europe-burning-trump-war-climate-sanctions-white-house).

The EU could, if it insists, impose fuel use taxes on both ships and planes traveling between the EU and elsewhere. For ships, the goal would be to increase their efficiency. For planes, partly to discourage their use.
