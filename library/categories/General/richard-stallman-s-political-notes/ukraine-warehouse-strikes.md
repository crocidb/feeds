 Ukraine is bombing [warehouses of the online store Wildberries](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/jul/24/ukraine-warehouse-strikes-wildberries-war-closer-ordinary-russians), which contain mostly civilian merchandise, but also some military gear and drone parts. Destroying them ruins the small businesses that are selling throught that company.

Wildberries' involvement in military logistics justifies attacking it, but it would be better to direct Russians' ire at Putin, not at Ukraine.

I suggest that Ukraine declare a moratorium on attacking Wildberries warehouses, with a deadline for sellers of non-military products to retrieve their property from them, after which Ukraine would resume bombing them.
