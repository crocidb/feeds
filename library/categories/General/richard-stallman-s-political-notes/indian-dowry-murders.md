Dowry murders in India [have become](https://www.theguardian.com/global-development/2026/jul/07/dowry-murders-in-india-no-longer-spark-public-anger-or-debate-study-finds) more frequent, but arouse much less public anger.

 This may be due to Modi's repression of any political activity on behalf of people who are poor, weak, or exploited.
