The builders of Israeli "settlements" (in occupied Palestinian territory) [hold events around the world](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2026/jun/13/uk-london-real-estate-west-bank-israel-settlements) to sell apartments in them. One such event being held in London has triggered objections supported by 100 members of the houses of Parliament, who call for prohibiting the sale of land that was taken from Palestinians in violation of international law.
