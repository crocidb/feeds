Scott Pelley condemned the [magat](/glossary.html#magat) management of CBS for "murdering 60 Minutes", and [they fired him for that](https://www.theguardian.com/media/2026/jun/03/60-minutes-scott-pelley-cbs-accusations), as he must have expected. Since then, he has reported that they had tried to command him to insert lies into the show.
