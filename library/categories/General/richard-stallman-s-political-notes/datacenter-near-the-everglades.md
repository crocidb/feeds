 A planned datacenter near the Everglades [threatens to damage](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/12/datacenter-everglades-risk) it with waste heat.

There is a bright side: as data centers boost the combustion of fossil fuels, they will speed the inundation of the data center along with the Everglades and South Florida. And the White House.

But I don't think that will make up for all the things that the world would lose as civilization falls.
