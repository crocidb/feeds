US citizens: call on Congress to [pass the Find Our Families Act](https://actionnetwork.org/forms/become-a-grassroots-co-sponsor-of-rashida-tlaibs-find-our-families-act), which would require the deportation [thugs](/glossary.html#thug) to give their prisoners' relatives accurate information about where the prisoners are, and about their medical emergencies.

 It would also ban torturing the prisoners and their relatives by keeping the one from communicating with the other.

 US citizens: Join with this campaign to address this issue.

 To phone your congresscritter about this, the main switchboard is [+1-202-224-3121](tel:+12022243121).

 Please spread the word.
