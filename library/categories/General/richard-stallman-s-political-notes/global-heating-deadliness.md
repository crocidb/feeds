Due to [global heating](</glossary.html#global heating>), Texas prison cells meant to make prisoners suffer now [can kill them](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/jul/10/texas-crisis-brutal-heat-prison-cells).

The deadliness of global heat [is not limited to prisons](https://www.stallman.org/archives/2026-may-aug.html#29_June_2026_(Extreme_heat_will_double_US_hospitalizations)), but overhot places outside prison where people live and work are generally overhot because no one wants to spend money to do anything about that. Prisons can be overhot by intention.
