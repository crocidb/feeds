[The bullshitter](/glossary.html#bullshitter) has [acknowledged important points](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/jun/16/us-iran-deal-israel-relationship), such as that Israel wages war in ways that gratuitously kill civilians, and that Netanyahu's wars are unjust and bad for US interests.

 Does this mean [the bullshitter](/glossary.html#bullshitter) has had a change of heart? I doubt it; he has gone so long without a heart that he surely doesn't have one now. I think he is simply applying to Israel and Netanyahu the approach he has used with so many others: to contradict himself frequently and change positions so fast that negotiators accustomed to serious negotiations can't grasp what is happening.

 I am not sure this will confuse Netanyahu, though. He seems to practice a similar approach.

 Iran now [demands that Israel withdraw its army from Lebanon](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/jun/16/irans-top-envoy-says-peace-deal-with-the-us-dependent-on-israels-withdrawal-from-lebanon) or there will be no peace deal.

 Maybe the new maximum leader believes he dominates [the wrecker](/glossary.html#wrecker) so much that he can demand anything whatsoever and get it. Or maybe he is bluffing.

 With so much readiness to bluff, it will be hard for them to reach an agreement even if there is an agreement to be reached.
