 The [bully](/glossary.html#trump)'s henchmen are rushing the deportation of people who have been trafficked, and [minors who fled abuse by their parents](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/05/trump-immigration-children-deportation-push), depriving them full and proper judgment of their cases.

Sometimes they get deported before the hearing that will decide whether they are entitled to stay in the US. That is Kafkaesque.

The persecutors are mainly focusing on minors, but "minors" does not imply "children". A 17-year old is not a child and we must not call per one. Can't you be concerned for someone's rights without calling per a "kid"? I am.

But the reason I am concerned about that manipulative exaggeration is not just that it is soppy. It can be dangerous too, to those very people. Calling a teenager a "child" can lead to disrespect for per rights.
