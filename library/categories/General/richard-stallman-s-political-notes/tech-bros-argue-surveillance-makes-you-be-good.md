A number of tech bros argue that [total surveillance will make everyone "be good"](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/26/xprize-founder-says-humans-behave-better-when-theyre-being-watched/). The implicit assumption is that the people that can use the data to punish you are good, and will always and only punish bad acts. The reality of surveillance is the opposite of that. Massive surveillance helps deportation [thugs](/glossary.html#thug) find immigrants and attack them, sometimes lawfully and sometimes not. But it doesn't deter the deportation [thugs](/glossary.html#thug) from murdering people[[1]](/archives/2025-nov-feb.html#12_January_2026_(Masked_deportation_thug_murdered_Renee_Nicole_Good)) [[2]](/archives/2025-nov-feb.html#29_January_2026_(Alex_Pretti_shot_dead_by_deportation_thugs)) on the street. The trumpets who control the surveillance don't allow access to the surveillance data to catch a killer who is on their side.
