 Kevin T Baker explains how the pressure to automate and accelerate US military target selection (ultimately using a Possible Intelligence system called "Maven") [has made it easier](https://www.theguardian.com/news/2026/mar/26/ai-got-the-blame-for-the-iran-school-bombing-the-truth-is-far-more-worrying) to make mistakes, and harder to notice and prevent a possible mistake.

The bombing of the Iranian girls' school was decided by that process.
