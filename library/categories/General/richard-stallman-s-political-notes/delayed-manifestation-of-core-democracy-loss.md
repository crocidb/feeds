 The US lost the core of its democracy in the 1970s-80s. After that, inertia kept parts of the democratic system sort of working. What we are seeing now is the [delayed manifestation](https://www.theguardian.com/global/commentisfree/2026/mar/08/trump-democracy-oligarchy-policy) of the full consequences of that loss.
