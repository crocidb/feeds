Ford fired experienced engineers and replaced them with automation, which harmed the quality of production. The company was compelled to [rehire many of the engineers](https://www.the-independent.com/tech/ford-ai-automation-human-workers-b3003787.html).

 I wonder if Ford will fire them again if it gets the automation to work better.
