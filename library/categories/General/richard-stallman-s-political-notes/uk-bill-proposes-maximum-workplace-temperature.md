A bill in the UK [proposes to set a maximum temperature](https://www.theguardian.com/politics/2026/jul/12/green-mp-hannah-spencer-aims-to-pass-law-on-maximum-workplace-temperatures) for workplaces, as a safety standard.

 This is needed, in the age of [global heating](</glossary.html#global heating>) disaster, to protect the lives of workers.
