\* Humanity can raise living standards, reduce inequality and keep global heating within a 2°C rise, [according to a sweeping vision](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/jun/04/world-inequality-lab-equality-academics-planetary-survival) for planetary survival.\*

 The plan includes redistributing a large part of rich people's wealth. I don't have the time or background to validate the details, but it sounds plausible, if we can overcome the power of the rich.

 The authors of the plan [describe it](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2026/jun/04/a-good-life-for-the-99-isnt-a-pipe-dream-it-can-be-done-heres-how).

 It is worth a try, since our current course is [taking us to disaster](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/jun/04/happiness-is-not-just-about-gdp-ambitious-plan-or-utopia).
