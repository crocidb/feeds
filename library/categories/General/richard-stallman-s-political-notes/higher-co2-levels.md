 Slow, natural global heating, about 56 million years ago, [devastated the Earth's forests](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/13/forests-carbon-dioxide-emissions) once it passed a certain level of CO2 in the air.

Higher CO2 levels damaged plants, both physiologically and by encouraging massive plant-killing fires, and by causing plant-killing droughts.

Since 2000, the CO2 we have added has been bad for forests.
