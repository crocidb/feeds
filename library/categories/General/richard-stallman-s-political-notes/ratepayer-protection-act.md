The "Ratepayer Protection Act" [is supposedly intended](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/jul/05/ratepayer-protection-act-datacenters) to prevent datacenters from driving up electric rates on ordinary people, but experts say it is too weak and too easily bypassed, and is a figleaf rather than a solution.
