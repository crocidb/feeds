[The bullshitter](/glossary.html#bullshitter) and Iran say they are [coming closer to a peace deal](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/jun/14/trump-news-at-a-glance-president-claims-iran-no-longer-want-a-nuclear-weapon-amid-peace-deal-hopes).

 We can't presume that [the bullshitter](/glossary.html#bullshitter) is telling the truth about any of this. Even if some parts are true, other parts may be bullshit.

 But even if they do make an agreement, [the bullshitter](/glossary.html#bullshitter) might break it at any time. Iran too might break the agreement.
