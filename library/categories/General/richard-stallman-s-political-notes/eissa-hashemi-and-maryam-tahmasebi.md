 Eissa Hashemi and Maryam Tahmasebi, a married couple of professors who were permanent residents in the US, [are in deportation prison](https://www.thenation.com/article/society/maryam-tahmasebi-iran-ice-essay/) because Hashemi's mother, [Masoumeh Ebtekar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Masoumeh_Ebtekar), is a supporter of the regime and participated in the occupation of the US embassy.

It is not impossible that they are somehow working with Ebtekar on a nefarious plot, but the persecutor's henchmen have not made such charges -- they have simply cancelled the family's residence permits arbitrarily and (according to Tahmasebi) aim to keep them in deportation prison for life.
