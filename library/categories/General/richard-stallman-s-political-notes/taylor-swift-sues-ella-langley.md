 (satire) \*[Taylor Swift Sues Ella Langley For Bangs Infringement](https://theonion.com/taylor-swift-sues-ella-langley-for-bangs-infringement/).\*

The serious point of this news parody is that the system of copyright, transformed by massive duplication technology into a system of oppressive extraction, inspires attempts to put legal monopolies on every aspect of human behavior.
