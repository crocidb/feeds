Robert Reich: \*[No one can be honest in a regime based on big lies](https://web.archive.org/web/20260720033410/https://robertreich.substack.com/p/sunday-thought-integrity). As the poet, philosopher, and statesman Václav Havel put it, "If the main pillar of the system is living a lie, then it is not surprising that the fundamental threat to it is living in truth." So a choice must be made — personal integrity or loyalty to [[the corrupter](/glossary.html#corrupter)].\*
