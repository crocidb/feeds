 Of 2500 articles about Britain's June heat wave, 3/4 of them [did not mention](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/jul/14/most-coverage-june-heatwave-did-not-mention-climate-crisis) global heating at all. And under 5% of them mentioned "net zero" policies to avoid making the heat get worse and worse.
