Scientists predict that the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (a loop of currents, some at the surface and some deep) [will collapse due to global heating](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2026/jun/14/amoc-collapse-europe-climate). They do not know whether that collapse will spread over many years, or happen with shocking speed. The activities to monitor the change have been defunded.
