 The Tory government of the UK was convinced to decide to spend over 250 billion pounds on [new fossil fuel generators](https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2026/jul/08/carbon-capture-con-andy-burnham-fossil-fuels-renewables), justifying this based on the claim that they will emit somewhat less greenhouse gas pollution than already existing fossil fuel generators.

 That is a very stupid justification, given that it would be cheaper to build renewable generators as replacements, and eliminate all of their greenhouse gas emissions.
