Various groups are estimating that [Israel has killed around 600,000 people](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/9/14/foreign-doctors-say-israel-systematically-targeting-gazas-children-report) in Gaza, which would be 1/4 of the population. Ralph Nader contends that both HAMAS and Israel have reasons to accept the underestimate.
