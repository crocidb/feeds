[The wrecker's](/glossary.html#wrecker) henchmen plan to [break up and wreck](https://abcnews.com/Politics/trump-administration-moves-dismantle-prominent-us-weather-climate/story?id=128498427) the National Center for Atmospheric Research, stating [global heating](</glossary.html#global heating>) denialism as their motive.

 A court [blocked one attack](https://arstechnica.com/science/2026/06/judge-blocks-part-of-trump-admins-effort-to-hurt-colorado-research-center/), ruling that it was "arbitrary and capricious".

 If they succeed, the blow to climate modeling may indirectly kill millions more people, perhaps hundreds of millions more, but it will be hard to estimate how many of additional deaths were due to the sabotage of NCAR. In 20 years or so, their successors will deny that [the saboteurs](/glossary.html#saboteur) of today had anything to do with the world-wide disasters, but people who reject their lies will understand who is responsible.
