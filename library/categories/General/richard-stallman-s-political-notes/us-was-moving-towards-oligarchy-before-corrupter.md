Robert Reich argues that the US was [steadily moving towards oligarchy](https://robertreich.substack.com/p/we-needed-trump) before [the corrupter](/glossary.html#corrupter) pushed it quickly to the extreme.

 Therefore, if we do overcome [the corrupter](/glossary.html#corrupter) and his followers, they may turn out to have taught us a crucial lesson about defending democracy, justice, and honesty.
