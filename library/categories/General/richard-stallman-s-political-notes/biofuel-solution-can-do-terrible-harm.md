The biofuel "solution" to [global heating](</glossary.html#global heating>) [can do terrible harm](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/jun/05/biofuel-alternatives-oil-price-rises-world-food-crisis) if it substitutes for increased solar and wind electric generation. \*Experts say increased use of crops for fuel is "dangerous game" that could send food price inflation soaring.\*
