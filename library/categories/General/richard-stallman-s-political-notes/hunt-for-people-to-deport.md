That backfired against them in several cities, [including Minneapolis](https://www.stallman.org/archives/2026-jan-apr.html#17_January_2026_(ice_minneapolis_protests_renee_good)) and [Los Angeles](https://www.stallman.org/archives/2026-jan-apr.html#18_January_2026_(Keith_Porter_shot_by_deportation_thug_for_celebratory_shot)), with the effect of galvanizing Americans against him. So now their plan is to collect ever-more surveillance data so they can find lots of [unauthorized immigrants](https://www.stallman.org/archives/2026-jan-apr.html#28_March_2026_(ICE_to_TSA_positions)), as well as many authorized immigrants that they can falsely accuse of being unauthorized, or target for fabricated accusations of political crime.
