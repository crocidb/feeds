 The ambassador to Britain appointed by President Aung San Suu Kyi defied the military government's order to vacate the ambassadorial house in London. [The UK is denying the moral doubts](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2026/aug/12/ex-myanmar-ambassador-uk-kyaw-zwar-minn-trial-trespassing-diplomatic-residence) about the situation by prosecuting him for "trespassing" in a diplomatic residence.
