A new way of designing gameplay systems, based on a force-exchange network and functional force-vector transformation logic. This was inspired by the two-vector event model of Peter Gärdenfors (Professor at Lund University), as well as the emergent system architecture concepts invented by Glenn Puchtel (Principal software architect at GRUBBRR).
