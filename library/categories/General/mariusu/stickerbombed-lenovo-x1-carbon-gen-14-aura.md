Especially with laptops being an attractive target for thieves at airports and touristic areas, I not only enjoy telling a story through the appearance of my devices, but I also find customizing the appearance a good way to deceive snatchers into mistaking these items for something of lower and potentially uninteresting value.

Unlike the average *tech bro* that collects stickers of their favorite tech corporations and stacks, only to stick them onto their MacBooks in an uninspiring way, I prefer to carefully plan my [stickerbombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sticker_art) and create a well-crafted collage that tells a story. For that, I use stickers that I collect throughout my [travels](/travel/), and also order specific stickers, which I then cut into new motifs. Instead of Silicon Valley logos, my works usually contain imagery that is reflective of personal life events, social topics, and sometimes satire.

If you like this sort of art and are looking for further inspiration for your own projects, you might enjoy the books by [Stickerbombworld](https://www.stickerbombworld.com), as well as the [c/stickerbomb](https://lemmy.sdf.org/c/stickerbomb) and [r/stickerbomb](https://www.reddit.com/r/stickerbomb/) communities.
