"Campaign" (originally: Burning Artemis) is the title of an unproduced Doctor Who novel, originally meant to feature in the BBC's Past Doctor Adventures range. The author, Jim Mortimore, self-published the story in 2000, with the proceeds going to charity. [A revised PDF version of the book can be found here](https://doctorwho.org.nz/archive/pdf/campaign.pdf) and read for free! The story features [the first Doctor](https://tardis.wiki/wiki/First_Doctor), [Susan](https://tardis.wiki/wiki/Susan_Foreman), [Ian](https://tardis.wiki/wiki/Ian_Chesterton) and [Barbara](https://tardis.wiki/wiki/Barbara_Wright).
