Share of [Doctor Who fanzine: In All But Name](https://archive.org/details/inallbutname)

Last year for Doctor Who's birthday (23.11.1963, for the uninitiated) I uploaded a fanzine I'd organized to the internet archive. The PDF is in DIN A4 format and has 100 pages, with fanworks from 30ish people. The journey there began almost a year before, in December 2024.

I have to give a *huge* shoutout to the other people who helped me organize the zine, but specifically to [ivq](https://www.instagram.com/ivqks159/) for giving me so much info on planning and communication!!
