*Today there was a rainbow.*

⁂ ⁂ ⁂

A few minutes into my walk, a shower flashed from the grey sky. Looking at all the falling water that surrounded me, I took cover under a nearby, dense tree, one through which comparatively few raindrops were falling. *I can wait here for a moment.* While I was not dry, I was not soaked, either. I thought about whether to turn back, but something kept me going. I wanted to go on my walk. A minute or two later, the rain turned from a downpour to a drizzle. I came out from under the trees and continued walking.

A minute or two ahead on the path. I saw a family taking cover under a tree, too. We had the same instincts: to take a break while the rain fell, rain that would get progressively lighter as time went by.

Turning a corner, the grey sky was filled with an arc of colour. A rainbow beamed from one place to another. Perhaps the bottom of the rainbows were between two trees who had been soaking in all the water. *I have to write this down to bear witness to the fact that today there was a rainbow.* The colourful sky lightened my heart and my step. I saw the rainbow for only a moment – maybe a minute or two – but that was enough to be left with both the joy of rainbows and a reminder that rainbows happen.

The rain calmed down. The sun emerged (albeit through the continuing grey sky). Up ahead, there were trees of yellows and rich oranges – autumn, in bloom. Concrete paths were coated with crunchy leaves. I kept walking, the occasional light raindrop falling on my coat, enjoying the day.
