*I don’t know exactly how the trees are changing, the details being so minute and complex at once, but I know that they are. I can see the change in the colours.*

⁂ ⁂ ⁂

Carrying the weight of summer, the trees shed with colour and become anew, carrying messages of the present and tomorrow. Amid my thoughts on the changes in the trees – the beginnings of a new season – I started to look at the reds and oranges and greens and greys that stretched from the ground to the sky in a new way. On my walk, leaves became dreams and dreams became leaves.

Slowing my pace, I started to assign my old dreams to leaves – things I wished for that never happened, and things I accomplished that did happen. I walked around the past and felt the lingering colour of all those moments, meandering through the joys of reaching a goal and the melancholies mixed with recognition that come from things that were never meant to be. *What if autumn was the season of falling dreams, making way, with colour, for new ones?*

Thinking about this question, I looked up to the distance and smiled as I considered not my dreams at present, but the very fact there are more to make. I then looked around. The colourful fates of past dreams were the red leaves, grounded to Present. Those same leaves reminded me that the changes in autumn make space for new buds in the forthcoming season. I too have been making space lately.

On my walk I imagined myself letting go of old dreams as they stayed where they were while I walked ahead, and then I started to think of new ones. I noticed that the dreams I came up with in the moment have been in my heart for a while – of making new friends, of being with the friends I have, of seeing new colours. I was already carrying visions of new seasons in my heart, just as I was carrying memories of old ones too – memories full of colour, memories that make the melody of me. Simultaneously, I recognised some dreams are evergreen – those of being with Nature, those of friendship, those of being where I am.

Of all the dreams I imagined, one stood out. I called out to myself, in my inner monologue, “All I want is to be me.”

These words became in autumn, fuelled by the colours of Nature and the song of the heart.
