I wrote this a few days ago, inspired by [Things I Want to Remember](https://agracefull-life.com/2026/07/13/things-i-want-to-remember-its-been-awhile/) by A Graceful Life. I planned to add more to the list but I didn't end up finishing it. With that said, I do want to publish what I have, as a little snippet of four things that caught my eye on one evening last week.

*Ironically, I cannot remember on which day I wrote the things I want to remember, but it would have been either Thursday, Friday or Saturday.*

⁂ ⁂ ⁂

*Things I want to remember from today:*

1. *The way tiny flies, so small they were almost invisible, danced through the skies as I looked at the sunset; how the smallest things can be the most beautiful.*
2. *The candy floss colours on the periphery of the sky at sunset, fading by the moment.*
3. *The way hearing friends laugh makes me feel.*
4. *How the sunlight cast a golden glow behind my guitar, making me think about the joys of music — how music lets us become ourselves, together.*

⁂ ⁂ ⁂

Yesterday, I also noted down these moments of joy:

* The joy of the starry night.
* The joy of the first moments outside in the morning.
* The joy of first moments — in the coffee shop, looking at all the pastries, of the first moments of music, of daylight.
* The joy of seeing Nature in the reflection of the window
* The joy of the blue skies and green trees.
* Leaves that look like stars.

The articulation "first moments" inspired me to write "[First moments](https://jamesg.blog/2026/08/24/first-moments)" this morning, with my observations from today.
