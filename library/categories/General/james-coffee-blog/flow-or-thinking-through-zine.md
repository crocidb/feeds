After writing *autumn (essence, of)* today, I worked on another zine: *flow (or, thinking through)*.

When writing *flow (or, thinking through)* I started with no particular topic in mind other than a vague interest in exploring the concept of flow. To get started, I consulted my notes, bringing in and expanding upon ideas I have written about, and then I let myself explore whatever flowed in the moment.
