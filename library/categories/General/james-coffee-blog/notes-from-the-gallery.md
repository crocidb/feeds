On Tuesday I took a few notes while visiting the National Gallery of Scotland. Usually I develop these into a full post, but since the notes have been in my drafts for a few days I thought I would publish them verbatim.

⁂ ⁂ ⁂

*How the light of a museum changes the painting — the golden frame feels warm, contrasting with the cool silver sky. Like how this morning the warm colours contrasted with the cool air — how the world is framed; how the story is framed. Painting presence and proximity and distance. Wondering for how long the work will be here. Did Turner wonder for how long the ruins of the castle would be there? Did Turner feel melancholy, even though the painting exudes grace and stillness? One animal — alone?*

*The joy of hearing someone whisper “oh wow!” as they walk between rooms in the gallery.*

*The joy of hearing bagpipes after leaving the gallery for the day — of staying until almost closing time. Of seeing an autumn leaf blow past the concrete plaza, propelled by the strong wind of the day.*
