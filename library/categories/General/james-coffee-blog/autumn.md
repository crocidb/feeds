*The joy of the cool morning air and the seeing singing birds fly between trees. There is a light haze in the air this morning, the haze of an autumn morning. My cheeks are chilly right now — probably pink too. Light shines through the windows and casts a long glow on the floor inside and the leaves outside — the crisp green glow of the sunlit plants. The day is so beautiful.*

*There are more trees changing colour — the sight of the trees in the sky outside the coffee shop was breathtaking. Everywhere I look morning welcomes me into this Sunday — the air is as cool as I love. I am listening to Lorde’s music, which takes me back to her concert a few weeks ago — the energy of the light in the darkness, the impact of the unity of voices, the joy and passion and depths of the songs. I was a writer in the dark and a writer in the light that day — every second a new experience, every second new observations that were and became a story.*

*The feeling of wanting to hold onto every note of a song — to hold onto every part of a moment.*

⁂ ⁂ ⁂

Just as the sky was clear enough last night to see the stars, so too was the sky clear this morning, except for the light haze that graced the day. I went for a walk and visited a coffee shop, one of my favourite ways to start a morning. At one point on my walk, I noticed the leaves on one particular weeping branch on a tree were turning orange and red. With every passing week I notice the changes in the seasons to a greater extent. As I write, I look out onto a tree in the distance that has been changing colour for the last week; its orange colours standing out amid the sea of green on the horizon.

While sitting in the coffee shop, I read a blog post and noticed the author, a writerly friend half-way across the world, had mentioned me and my most recent post on autumn. My writing on an autumn leaf here in Scotland carried its way to another world where I think it will be around Spring now. Moments like this are what make the web special.

⁂ ⁂ ⁂

Writing this post, I carry with me the music that I listened to on my journey, and look forward to playing the next song.
