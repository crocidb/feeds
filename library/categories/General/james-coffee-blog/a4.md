“You injured yourself again? What was it this time?”  
“I have taken up type-writing. Unfortunately there are holes between the keys.”  
“Did your finger get stuck?”  
“I wish. I dropped a quarter of a digestive biscuit into the keyboard while I was writing a letter to my friend. I decided to use this as an opportunity to learn typewriter maintenance.”  
“Ouch. You scratched yourself with a screwdriver then?”  
“No, you’re looking at an A4 injury.”  
“What’s that? A category? Sounds minor?”  
“Let’s just say instruction manuals are their own hazards.”
