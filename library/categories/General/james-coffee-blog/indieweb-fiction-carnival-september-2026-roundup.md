In September I hosted the IndieWeb Fiction Carnival on the topic of “[Moonlight](https://jamesg.blog/2026/09/01/iwfc-moonlight)”. I received four stories. I also wrote a story for the Carnival.

To everyone who participated – thank you for your stories! They were all a delight to read. And if you are reading this and thinking “I wish I had participated!”, I am still accepting contributions. If you write a story on the theme, feel free to send me an email and I will add it to the list of contributions.

Below you can read my summary of all the contributions I received for last month’s Carnival. I will try my best not to include spoilers. Please enjoy.

*This month’s IndieWeb Fiction Carnival is being [hosted by Marisabel on the theme “Sunflowers in the Sky”](https://marisabel.nl/public/blog.php).*

Contributions
----------

### Many moons from now ###

Ruben started the month with his story “[Many moons from now](https://kedara.eu/many-moons/)”. Under the moonlight and in a power cut, the main character falls to the ground and enters a dream-like state. *Will the main character wake up?*

### Marble Memories ###

My contribution was “[Marble Memories](https://jamesg.blog/2026/09/01/marble-memories)”. An older man loved walking through the glistening fields at moonlight. One night, he sees something strange: a block of marble. He takes notes diligently, every night. *What could the marble block be?*

### a kind of lunacy ###

In a story that made me imagine new worlds – in the very best way – Marisabel wrote “[a kind of lunacy](https://marisabel.nl/public/blog/IndieWeb_Fiction_Carnival_September_2026_Moonlight)”, where the main character, Mani, yearns to “reside in perpetual moonlight”, eventually embarking on a new, mysterious journey, “run[ning] towards her light, tripping with each step”. *Will Mani reside in perpetual moonlight?*

### The Power of Moonlight ###

In "[The Power of Moonlight](https://sarajaksa.eu/2026/09/indieweb-fiction-carnival-september-2026-the-power-of-moonlight-original-fiction/)", Sara takes us to moonlight passing through leaves and trees where a man lying on the ground is discovered by a police officer. “The person groaned, and turned around. They looked like they were in pain.” *What happened?*

### We are always three ###

Finally, in "[We are always three](https://joelchrono.xyz/blog/iwfc-moonlight/)", Joel brings the reader into the archives of the main character’s family, exploring what is to be found. In one, a man “wearing some weird suit” lets a “cup spill on the mat”. *Who is the man? What is he doing?*
