This year I have been drinking more tea than coffee. I have around three or four coffees a week, usually all decaf, and I don’t visit speciality coffee shops as much as I once did (in large part because I am never sure whether there will be decaf available). I think the last time I had a speciality coffee must have been a month ago. Yesterday I went on a wander and saw a coffee shop that opened a few years ago that I had never visited. I walked past the shop but, shortly thereafter, decided to turn around and try a cup of coffee from there.

Served in a small orange paper cup – sized appropriately for a flat white – the coffee I had was *delicious*: nutty, creamy, oaty. The cup being orange brought even more joy because orange is my favourite colour. Although the cup was small, it took me some time to drink the coffee: I savoured every sip. I slowed the pace of my walk and eventually stopped for a while to appreciate some architecture.

The coffee reminded me of so many speciality coffees I have had in the past. The flavour was distinct. The coffee was strong. The baristas are clearly experts in their craft. The cafe had an energy that reminded me oddly of London. Or perhaps that is me yearning to visit there once again. In any case, I am happy with drinking tea on most days, but the occasional speciality coffee is nice to have, especially while out and about.
