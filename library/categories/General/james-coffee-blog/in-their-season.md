The leaves are in their “flying season".  
 Coating the skies in colour, brilliantly.

The trees are in their “wintering season".  
 Branches bearing and silvering, beautifully.

The sunrises are in their “November season".  
 Pink comes later in the day, unpredictably.

Leaves, trees, sunrises and all, perpetually  
 Delighting day, cyclically.
