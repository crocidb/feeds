The low clouds glowed with shades of lavender as I opened the window to see the first colours of Day. A field in the distance, yellow from harvest and the drier weather, is coated in a blanket of cool fog; in front of the trees are an almost-but-not-quite transparent haze, making the trees, full of autumn life, too, feel cool. In the foreground and all around there are yellow leaves. The occasional tree carries red leaves. All around there is colour, from deep and sky blue to countryside green to vivid red.

High in the sky, I see the moon. Yesterday the whole moon was visible; today, almost the whole moon is visible; the visibility of the moon ebbs through phases just as Nature moves between phases. I can see thin clouds passing in front of the moon, so light that they are almost transparent,. The clouds are moving fast with the morning breeze.

Here, indoors, I feel the chill of the metal on my laptop and a breeze coming from the window. One edge of the window shows signs of condensation: the contrast between the relative warmth indoors and the chill outside, where the blanket of fog continues to coat the fields. Outside, the birds sing the song of Morning, the moon shows its full but fading light before disappearing until night, and blue, cloud-soaked sky, contrasts beautifully with the warm autumnal colours below.

*Good morning, world. Happy Tuesday.*
