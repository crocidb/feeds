*What made you happy today?* asks [Catch a Bird](https://catchabird.com). I *love* this question, and the website. For the last five minutes or so, I have been both answering the aforementioned question, and trying my best to make some people smile. Here are a selection of my answers to the question:

* Trying 100% dark chocolate for the first time.
* Playing the guitar.
* Singing my heart out.
* The joy of autumn.
* Have a wonderful day :)
* Taylor Swift!
* Seeing two cats while wandering the neighbourhood.
* You’re an amazing human :)

I love technology that can highlight the best of us; technology that brings us together.

To you, dear reader, I hope you have a wonderful day.
