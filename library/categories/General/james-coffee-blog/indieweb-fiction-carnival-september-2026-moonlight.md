When you close your eyes and think of moonlight, what do you see? An author typing a story on an antique typewriter while burning the midnight oil? A musician who is perpetually unaware that there is moonlight outside because they are playing in an underground club? A person too tired to sleep, but who finds beauty in the moonlight?

I am hosting this month’s [IndieWeb Fiction Carnival](https://indieweb.org/IndieWeb_Fiction_Carnival). The topic is “Moonlight.”

For your entry, you can write about whatever comes to mind when you hear “moonlight.” Moonlight might be in the foreground of your story. The moon could be a main character. Or moonlight could be in the background of the story, a soft presence like the moon itself. If you like Sci-Fi, you might even think about moonlight on other planets.

There are no recommendations for the length of your piece: your story can be as short or as long as you want. You don’t need to have any experience writing fiction to participate in the Carnival. I’m still new to writing fiction!

Please send your entries to me [via email](https://jamesg.blog/email) by September 30th. I will list all the entries on this page in the order I receive them. At the beginning of October, I will post a round-up summarising all of the entries that were received.
