Frances post [Highlights of my week](https://francescrossley.com/highlights-of-my-week/) has inspired me to write a list of things I love and that bring me joy. [I have done this before](https://jamesg.blog/2024/11/22/things-that-bring-me-joy) but I thought it would be fun to write another list. I encourage you to write one, as well. You can keep it private if you want – it’s the writing that matters.

⁂ ⁂ ⁂

I love:

* The sound of falling water – water falls and water fountains, peaceful streams and flowing rivers.
* Saying good morning to all my friends.
* Seeing someone smile, and hearing people laugh.
* Hearing people perform music that brings out their passion.
* Art in public spaces.
* Learning new things.
* Writing, prose and poems and stories and dreams.
* Watching the world go by in Princes Street Gardens.
* Visiting bookshops and convincing myself that I should read the books I have at home before I purchase more.
* Listening to songs from artists I love.
* Learning about new artists and songs that, over time, become the soundtrack to my life.
* Walking quickly among the morning bustle.
* Walking slowly in the afternoon.
* Bringing joy to places where it is harder to find.
* Making people laugh.
* When someone says just the right thing that fills you with joy.
* Doing the dishes (it’s my favourite chore).
* People watching.
* Thursday evenings.
* All evenings.
* Watching the sun set.
* Feeling the autumn breeze on my cheeks.
* Thinking about colour.
* Thinking about art.
* Imagining.
* Playing.
* And so much more.
