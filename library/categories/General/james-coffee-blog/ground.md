> Rain may exist, but so too do clear skies, and the possibility for something to start serendipitously, like a raindrop falling from a sky once dry or a wave from a friend you never expected to see. *([In Romantics, VI](https://jamesg.blog/romantics-vi)*)
>
>

I wave with words. I weave and paint with words -- with pinks (the morning sunrise in autumn), golds (the sun itself), blue (the skies), greens (the trees). Colours paint. I start walking.

I carry the songs of prior days just as I know that there are the songs of new days to be written. I think about the songs of Nature. Does Nature sing? If so, is her song in the sound of the leaves dancing across the air, and in the leaves being blown by the wind, and in the sound of the three birds perched on branches surveying their world?

Or does Nature also sing with time, just as we, too, are in dialogue with time. Trees change with the seasons. Or do trees turn time into colour? Both? We, meanwhile, turn time into stories – the rain may fall as a drop from a cloud, or a tear from the eyes with which we see the world.

I think about materials; the solidity of concrete. I learned to think about the materials on which I stand from a book about museums. The writer remarked that the marbled floors were hard to walk on for long periods of time. In contrast, I remember when I touched grass earlier this year and felt the softness that the Earth holds. One book opened my world; the ground, transformed.

The brisk walk leaves my cheeks chilly and my heart warmed, beating faster from the wind I have braced and the passion that sounds from the songs I have been singing, songs I know well, songs that soak my emotions like the grass absorbs the rain; songs that bring me into memory and, then, today.
