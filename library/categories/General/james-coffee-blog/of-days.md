I have thought about winter through all the seasons this year. The season has become something of a frame of reference – of times when days are shorter and the air is colder, and of times where the brilliant blue skies come alive in the stillness of the season. I spend less time walking and more time inside. As I write, I realise it is hard for me to write about the season without being among it – the day itself is so often the anchor in my works.

What I do know is that, at this time of year, I can see the stars for longer because the sun sets sooner. So too can I see more of the stars in winter.

⁂ ⁂ ⁂

Earlier this month I attended a workshop on memoir writing. While I was not especially interested in writing a memoir, I was interested in the genre. What did it mean to write a memoir? I left with pages of notes. Although, what stood out more in the workshop was something I said in our workshop chat when we were all asked why we attended. I said “I want to figure out what my story is.” On reflection, I think, like Whitman said, we contain multitudes – we have many stories. However, it was not the exact words but rather the fluency with which I said them that caught me off-guard.

As with many topics, I don’t know what to say next, and so I sit with what I have, and wonder that maybe one day I will come back to this note and think “I know what this means now.”

⁂ ⁂ ⁂

The coffee shop was almost empty this morning – the chairs and tables lined up against the back wall were all in full order. A single person sat with a laptop at one table. Another table by the counter had a single, large empty mug. The counter itself was clean; there were no cups or other orders being readied.

Maybe the coffee shop was quieter because the seasons are changing – the sun rises later, and the air is getting cooler. Or perhaps today was an aberration.

I like quiet places but I sometimes like busy places too.

⁂ ⁂ ⁂

An older man wears a jumper with the text “you are enough.”

Every time I see jumpers like this I think about how one person can make a difference. Today I additionally thought about how many people may smile on a day because someone chose to wear an item of clothing with a kind message.

⁂ ⁂ ⁂

This afternoon I watched an American Football game that is part of the NFL London series. The Eagles were playing the Jaguars. Did you know American Football is one of my favourite sports to watch? I saw the first three quarters and loved every minute. The opening of the game was a surprise – so many flags on the pitch, and a touchdown that was not to be. [CBS summarises the opening well](https://www.cbssports.com/nfl/news/jaguars-vs-eagles-live-updates-score-highlights/live/):

I didn’t see most of the last quarter, but what I did see was a good game.

⁂ ⁂ ⁂

There are still many words used by the commentators that I don’t know, but it’s nice to watch and learn – to build knowledge by combining what I hear and what I see. I think I now sort-of understand pass interference. Maybe.
