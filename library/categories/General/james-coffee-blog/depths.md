be my invisible mirror  
 that opens when i write.  
 with words, wonder; hope, dreaming.

be my literary pillow;  
 bear the brunt of the i (eyes)  
 the questions, of self.

be the messy middle of my mind  
 on paper, that speaks of life.  
 blank, until it is not.

*Inspired by* [*Tim Kiely's prompt*](https://poetrysociety.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NPC25_Prompt_2.pdf)*.*
