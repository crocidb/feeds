A browning leaf fell from the tree with grace  
 “I had no idea leaves could be so tiny.”  
 A large yellow leaf fell into my embrace  
 “This is my dream – ever colourful and shiny.”  

Stacks of leaves lie at the feet of their past  
 Layers of reds and yellows – veins of time  
 Ever maturing with life ever vast  
 From sunrise to evenfall – may Day shine.
