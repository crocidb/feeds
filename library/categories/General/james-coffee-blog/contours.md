Spectra of coherence — ground, sky; all presents. // Deconstruct.  
 Where do I find the word that embodies a step?  
 — elevation, propulsion, placing; in cycles, forward, forward.  
 A mirror of irreducibility.  
 How can a word reflect my cold nose, dripping with winter air,  
 Breathing in fast, deeply.  
 Stories, of moments —   
 Collapsed colour, stark senses, every expression:  
 Poem, no contours; possible?  
 (But in contours definition exists.)
