I have never considered myself an especially arty person when it comes to drawing. I *love* studying art, but I have always had a bit of a block when it comes to drawing myself. Recently, I have wanted to try and sketch something, and so, with my sketchbook sitting with a light layer of dust, I decided to pick it up and start in an area that interests me most: anime and manga characters.

For the last few days I have been sketching. I have taken a trick from studying and leave my sketchbook open and so, as I walk around the house, I might see it and pick it up. (My study trick is to leave my textbook in a prominent place, usually on the table next to my armchair, as a visual reminder to pick it up and read.) This trick has worked, and I have had a lot of fun sketching. I am sketching by reference from manga that I have lying around: I want to sketch all my favourite characters, and try different poses and outfits.

Leaving my sketchbook open has had an interesting side effect: I have felt good about my drawing, even if it is by reference. (In contrast, I tried sketching a photo of my which went… not so well. But I learned a lot!)

This lunchtime, I was trying to sketch Mao-Mao from *The Apothecary Diaries*, whose manga I have just started reading (and thoroughly enjoyed <sup class="footnote-reference" id="f-1"><a href="https://jamesg.blog/longform-feed#1">1</a></sup>). Here is one of my sketches, from one of Mao-Mao's poses in the first manga:

I plan to keep up my sketching, trying to do my best :)
