colours shimmering,  
sun lowering.

leaves leaving,  
seasons deepening.

trees lightening:  
branches preparing.

winds cooling,  
time wintering.

*I wrote this poem with reference to a series of key words in the first edition of* [*Planet Persephone's Go Offline zine*](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1794798589/go-offline-zine-print-at-home-booklet)*. I used "lightening", "time", "shimmer", "colour", "deep", "leaves", "wind", and "winter".*
