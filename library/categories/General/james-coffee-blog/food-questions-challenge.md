*There is maybe a fun blogging challenge in writing about our favourite snacks from where we live...*

After a discussion with [Joel](https://jamesg.blog/https;/joelchrono.xyz) and [Zachary](https://zacharykai.net), I started writing a few questions about food to which I’d love to hear others’ answers. This is in the same vein as the “[Bear Blog Questions Challenge](https://blog.avas.space/bear-blog-challenge/)” and other similar challenges. To participate, copy and paste the questions below and answer them on your own website:

* What was your favourite food growing up?
* What is your favourite food that is made locally? (In your region/country.)
* What is your favourite food generally?
* What is one food you have had in the last year that you remember warmly?
* What food do you really want to try?
* Do you take photos of your meals?
* Savoury or sweet?
* Spicy or no?
* Tea or coffee? (Both? Neither?)

Once you have answered the questions, feel free to send me an email with a link to your post. I will add a link to your writing here.

My responses
----------

Below are my responses to get us started.

### What was your favourite food growing up? ###

It has to be my grandmothers’ jam, especially the blackcurrant and gooseberry (or apple) jelly. Blackcurrants are my favourite fruit and my grandfather gardened a few bushes of them. Granny would use as much of the fruit from the garden as possible to make jam; every summer’s harvest was enough to make a year’s supply of jam, with leftovers. I would only have the jam when visiting them, so it was always a special treat. Blackcurrants to this day are my favourite fruit, although I haven’t had them in a while.

I also loved eating scones growing up. I didn’t have them very often; only when visiting cafes. But they were – and still are – so delicious.

### What is your favourite food that is made locally? ###

I had a colleague who once called me, affectionately, Mr. Oats because I liked porridge and other oats products. Here in Scotland we make a food called an “[oatcake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oatcake)” which is essentially a thin slab of oats. It is ideally served with cheese on top, but I have in the past enjoyed eating them plain. (I like plain foods as snacks.)

I also enjoy [Tunnocks' confectionaries](https://www.tunnock.co.uk/products/caramel-wafer/), in particular the caramel wafter.

### What is your favourite food generally? ###

I like lots of foods, but I think pasta and fish are my favourite food categories. (Although as the days go by I think more and more about what it would take to reduce the amount of fish I eat.) My favourite dessert is a small, well-made tiramisu (or, really, any dessert, so long as it: (i) isn’t too sweet, and; (ii) isn’t chocolate cake).

### What is one food you have had in the last year that you remember warmly? ###

I have a lot of good memories with food this year. One that stands out is a delicious vegan schnitzel I had in Nürnberg, Germany. The lingonberry sauce that accompanied the dish was delicious; my first time, I think, trying lingonberry. What made the meal was that I was surrounded by friends. Food is almost always better with conversation.

Going back a bit further to a few years ago, one meal that stands out was a I had a pizza with capers on top that I had in Rome, on my first trip abroad. The weather was warm, almost 40 degrees. I had never experienced a climate like it before. I didn’t expect how salty the pizza would be. With that said, it was delicious. That day I remember drinking several bottles of water, too.

### What food do you really want to try? ###

I love trying new foods (especially vegan/vegetarian). The one food I have been wanting to try for a few weeks was 100% dark chocolate, which I was able to taste yesterday. I thoroughly enjoyed the experience.

### Do you take photos of your meals? ###

Occasionally, but usually no. I don't mind if others' take photos of their food, though; we all make memories in our own ways.

### Savoury or sweet? ###

Sweet. 100%.

### Spicy or no? ###

I like spicy foods, and do enjoy challenging myself to see what spice I can tolerate. With that said I wouldn’t say I seek out spicy foods.

### Tea or coffee? ###

Both! I like tea most of the time, although I do enjoy the occasional cup of coffee, especially when I am out and about. When I am travelling, I like to seek out new coffee shops to try; I walk a lot while travelling so I need all the energy I can get!
