*The voice of the romantic midnight harkens; its calm tones echo through the day with the help of words. Is midnight romantic?*

I have been thinking about moments between: the transitions from one state to another. Yesterday, as I left the cafe from which I had purchased a coffee, I noticed how the sounds of the cafe receded into the background with every step I took, as the sounds of Nature and all that was around in my new environment took over. The echoes of new orders – lattes; morning fuels – receded as I admired the trees and the stone and the world.

I thought about the between moments when I woke up this morning and spent the first twenty minutes of the day lying in bed, enjoying the cool surface of the covers and the warmth of being under them. In that time, I had a few moments where all that was on my mind was waking up – transitioning from being in the state where I’d like an extra hour in bed to the one where I was up and saying good morning to the world; a moment as consequential as any other in my day: the beginning.

This essay exists as a between moment – between my first asking “is midnight romantic?” as my weary eyes began to weigh such that I knew it was time for sleep, and now, when I am considering the question. I exist in the moment when the question occupies my mind – not yet the desire for an answer, an answer that could be a poem, but the question.

I sit with the thought of how when the world quietens down, new dimensions of my inner world open. At night, I can use the energy left of the day to write down my remaining thoughts – questions and ideas – that may one day become something more. *There are beginnings all through the day.*
