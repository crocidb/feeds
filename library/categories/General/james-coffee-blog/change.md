The sun set an hour earlier today. I felt – feel – the fewer daylight hours already, the clocks having changed only last evening. When I played my guitar there was darkness outside and a roaring wind. The sound of the storm was abated, temporarily, by A minor and Fsus2. Wind is expected for another hour, before forecast to tame.

I reached for a song I knew, before, shortly thereafter, turning to songs I haven’t played in a while – the day was changing and so was I.

I reached for familiar, but not entirely familiar, music, with chords I know but whose order I do not hold in my muscles or, if I do, less deeply than the songs to which I reach every day, the changing air perhaps silently encouraging me to play a new song. With every passing chord I found my stride. I found the moment. I started playing more familiar music again, rejoicing through repetitions of lyrics that I keep in my heart along with passion and hope.

Lately I have been thinking about how I can’t write about winter until it comes. The image of winter in my mind was ice and blue skies. This morning I remembered the sun is seen less and less in winter; blue skies are one of many scenes in the season. I felt the cold for the first time since the cold snap a week or two ago. Feeling said cold, and holding onto it -- for the cold makes me cold but also connects me to the moment -- when possible I went to where it is warm: music and memories and the kitchen while I cooked peas in the pan that sent steam that heated up the space.

[Frances](https://francescrossley.com/autumn-cosiness/)’ post today on autumn reminded me of the hazy boundaries between the seasons; about the confluence of factors that come together to create a feeling, to create a season, to create moments. Just as, too, I have seen the seasons in new lights today. I balanced between the familiar and the less familiar and the novel, amid the maelstrom of flying leaves under gale force winds. To stay occupied while the storm passed -- carrying with it wisps of autumn leaves but the air of the forthcoming season -- I sat with music, held friendship, and started writing.
