This week James invited me, MJ, the site mascot and meowditor in chief, to contribute a post to *with words, wonder.* The first idea on my list – fish I love – was “vetoed” (at first I thought this had something to do with *toes*, but, alas, English seems to be enigmatic at times). My second idea, however, was approved: a piece inspired by James’ ongoing weeknotes series. I promised I would leave out the list of fish I love, but, in order for me to contribute, I knew I had to mention one. Without further ado, I’m a big fan of vegan salmon. Yes, cats can be vegan too.

The last few weeks have been all-go. Today I started my petition to James to bring back [Kittyscript](https://jamesg.blog/kittyscript). There is no way I am going to learn to write code in a language called Python. Snakes and cats don’t really get along. And don’t get me started on Go. A cat and a gopher? This is starting to sound like *Caddyshack*. To the extent I remember there were no (or not enough) cats in *Caddyshack,* though – that, indeed, being the greatest downfall of an otherwise amusing film.

I have also been hard at work practicing meditation. A cat’s life is demanding. A proper meditation practice is necessary. I have been trying to look out the window for as long as I can and watch my thoughts blow through the wind. Unfortunately, this viewpoint has been thus-far ill fated because occasionally birds fly past and disturb my meditation. *Of course I’m going to jump back a little bit when a bird flies past. They’re huge and they fly!* I have tried starting at the bookshelf. The colours sooth me. Why James has so many books he hasn’t yet read, however, is beyond me! *[Editor’s note: Hey! I might need them!]*

My meditative practice is usually followed by my current pet project: a musical instrument for cats. Pianos are functional, but the keys are too small, especially the black keys. James can attest to troubles I have been having with pianos. It’s hard to communicate “I meant to play a C#, not a Eb” when you are a cat. He doesn’t mind, though. Apparently we’re all always learning so it is okay to make mistakes, as long as you keep playing. Nevertheless, a piano specifically designed for cats would be nice. Having the pedals a bit closer to the keyboard would be ideal, too. I can’t sustain my notes at present. And don’t get me started on singing.

The changing seasons have been good for me. I much prefer to stay closer to home and so James’ writing about the seasons is what lets me really experience them. Sitting in my role as meowditor in chief suits me well. It helps with my English. My singing, however, leaves much to be desired. Perhaps I shall try to sing my next blog post. I’ll have to run it past James.

That’s all I have time for at the moment. The sun is starting to get in my eyes and I’d like to spend a bit of time staring at the bookcase and thinking about my piano project before preparing for dinner. This has been MJ. May you have a wonderful day.

\~ MJ
