Hey everyone!

This is the list of all the changes we've done to our projects during the month of May.

<img src="../media/content/videos/may_2026.jpg" alt="a hand drawn ink illustration of 2 bears dancing together, with others watching in the background, an hommage to William Holbrook Beard's Dancing Bears, painted in 1865" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="679">

* **Wiki**, documented the installation of Pino's [water tank](https://100r.ca/site/water_tank.html), added [thermostat](https://100r.ca/site/engine_care.html#thermostat) to [engine care](https://100r.ca/site/engine_care.html), and updated [woodstove installation](https://100r.ca/site/woodstove_installation.html#may2026).
* **[Grimgrains](https://100r.ca/site/grimgrains.html)**, added a [solar-roasted kinako](https://grimgrains.com/site/solar_roasted_kinako.html) recipe.
* **[Uxn](https://100r.ca/site/uxn.html)**, added instructions for validating builds during the [bootstrap process](https://wiki.xxiivv.com/site/uxntal_bootstrap).
* **[No Bears None](https://100r.ca/site/no_bears_none.html)**, we released a book! Nearly everything else was pushed aside so that we could complete it.

In the last few days before our departure, we finished rebuilding our galley cabinet and installed the new [water tank](https://100r.ca/site/water_tank.html). As per usual, we took our bikes apart, filled our bins with fresh produce, stowed away everything, and finally, on May 17th, propelled by a strong tide and a desire to be in a [quiet place](https://rabbits.srht.site/days/2026/05/23.html), Pino flew away from the crowds of Victoria.

These days, every morning at 0400, we are awakened by purple martins perched on our lifelines singing their dawn song, after coffee we write, draw, row, wander ashore, and we try to roast new things in the sun, like [soy beans](https://grimgrains.com/site/solar_roasted_kinako.html).

On the 25th, we finally released the first edition of [No Bears None](https://100r.ca/site/no_bears_none.html) for e-readers, a very detailed logbook about our sail from Victoria to Sitka. It's a heavily edited version of the original https://100r.ca/site/victoria\_to\_sitka\_logbook.html"\>Victoria to Sitka logbook, with a ton of extra stuff like a 100 drawings, 17 topical chapters and 13 recipes. A big thank you to everyone who has already got their copy! We plan to release a paperback version, either late this year or beginning of 2027.

Rek designed the [shirt](https://kokorobot.ca/media/content/iwp9_shirt.jpg) for this year's [International Workshop on Plan 9](https://iwp9.org/) that happened in Victoria.

Devine implemented a [vector renderer](https://wiki.xxiivv.com/site/hershey), which allows us to do beautiful typesetting directly in https://100r.ca/site/uxn.html"\>Uxn. We used that to release two zines this month, one for helping folks who want to break into [Interactions Nets](https://wiki.xxiivv.com/site/pocket_nets), and another one called [Fractions Are Everything](https://wiki.xxiivv.com/site/pocket_fractions) that invite readers to look at fractions differently.

**Book Club:** We are reading *Sailing Back in Time* by Maria Coffey, Emily Wilson's translation of *The Odyssey* and *Heart Of Darkness* by Joseph Conrad.
