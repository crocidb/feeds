Hey everyone!

This is the list of all the changes we've done to our projects during the month of September.

<img src="../media/content/videos/september_2026.jpg" alt="characters from the Donsol game all standing inside their own cards, all of the monsters are looking at the hero character with a shield and sword who has just slain an elemental" loading="lazy" width="1200" height="679">

* **Wiki**, added [portland island](https://100r.ca/site/portland_island.html), and updated [alcohol stove](https://100r.ca/site/alcohol_stove.html).
* **[Donsol](https://100r.ca/site/donsol.html)**, released on [Itch.io](https://hundredrabbits.itch.io/donsol)!
* **[Legs](https://hundredrabbits.itch.io/legs)**, we made a last minute gizmo for Catjam 2026.
* **[Rabbit Waves](https://100r.ca/site/rabbit_waves.html)**, finished a page about [One-time Pads](https://rabbitwaves.ca/site/one_time_pads.html).
* **[Rejoice](https://wiki.xxiivv.com/site/rejoice.html)**, discovered [infinity](https://wiki.xxiivv.com/site/rejoice.html#infinity).
* **[Tote](https://100r.ca/site/tote.html)**, added support for I/O.
* **[Korokobot](https://kokorobot.ca)**, added a page on [Processing Analog Art](https://kokorobot.ca/site/processing_analog_artwork.html).

We're disheartened to see a lot of the organization for direct action done on platforms that spy on its users, we've often talked about how it would be worthwhile to offer basic cryptography know-how to our friends, and so for a second month now, we're releasing a new [Rabbit Waves](https://100r.ca/site/rabbit_waves.html) page on how to encrypt and decrypt messages, this time using [One-Time Pads](https://rabbitwaves.ca/site/one_time_pads.html).

Ten years after the original release of [Donsol](https://100r.ca/site/donsol.html), we decided to make a new version with animated sprites and a proper in-game manual as a definitive release, and it's finally out and available directly [in the browser](https://hundredrabbits.itch.io/donsol) or your favourite Uxn emulator!

Devine has been invited to [Oredev 2026](https://oredev.org/) in Sweden to talk about software archival, because of it this month was spent making a lot of slides and talk notes. As usual, we'll share the full transcription after the conference.

**Book Club:** We read *To Kill a Mockingbird* by Harper Lee, *Madame Bovary* by Gustave Flaubert, and we are still re-reading *Dracula* by Bram Stoker.
