I've discussed my experience with [Beurer TL 100](https://vlnn.dev/daylight-lamp-first-day.org). Of course, things with the light sources got much worse very quickly when the war started -- 10 hours blackouts were norm for months -- and so new market emerged, cheap chinese LED lamps with batteries. Most of them are crazy bad in the context of light temperature and blinking, but they did their job perfectly, showing us the way in the time of darkness.
