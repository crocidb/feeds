A k-d tree (k-dimensional tree) is a [binary search tree](binary-search-tree.md) for points in k-dimensional space. It generalizes the BST idea: instead of comparing whole keys, each level of the tree compares points along **one axis**, cycling through axes as you descend. ![](../static/brain/k-d-tree/be4796db-4a91-4afa-9300-4961d762bb9d.png)
