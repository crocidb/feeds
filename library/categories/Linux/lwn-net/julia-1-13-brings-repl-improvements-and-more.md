Version 1.13 of [Julia](https://julialang.org/) was [released](https://julialang.org/blog/2026/09/julia-1.13-highlights/index.html) on September 10, 2026. Julia is a general-purpose language especially well suited to high-performance numerical computing. Its expressiveness, speed, and [composability](https://arstechnica.com/science/2020/10/the-unreasonable-effectiveness-of-the-julia-programming-language/) have made it increasingly popular with scientists and mathematicians. We last looked at Julia [one year ago](https://lwn.net/Articles/1044280/), in a review of the 1.12 release. The newest version features an enhanced read-eval-print loop (REPL), a GUI for installation, a smoother developer experience, and an assortment of performance improvements.
