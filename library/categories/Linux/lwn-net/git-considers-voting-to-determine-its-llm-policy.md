One of the dominant themes of 2026 has been the debate within development projects about the terms under which LLM-written contributions should be accepted — if, indeed, they should be accepted at all. The [Git project](https://git-scm.com/) has not been immune to this trend; the topic was [discussed](https://lwn.net/ml/all/summit-2026.94e33e9ddf234334.06@ttaylorr.com) at the recent Git Contributor's Summit. In this case, the path to a resolution may be somewhat circuitous; the project, which has never been governed as a democracy, is considering adopting a voting process as a way of bringing the discussion to an acceptable conclusion.
