The [Netdev 0x1A](https://netdevconf.info/0x1A/) conference was held in Rome, Italy from July 13 through July 16. Conference organizer Jami Hadi Salim has let us know that the videos and slides for [all sessions](https://netdevconf.info/0x1A/pages/sessions.html) are now available. Topics include Linux QUIC, shared memory socket transport, eBPF-based DDoS protection, and more.
