[Version 45](https://universal-blue.discourse.group/t/bazzite-45-is-here/12702) of [Bazzite](https://bazzite.gg/), the image-based gaming distribution based on Fedora Linux, has been released. Notable changes in this release include a rebase to Fedora 45, updated [Open Gaming Collective](https://opengamingcollective.org/) (OGC) kernels, support for Logitech TrueWheel devices, and more.

LWN [covered](https://lwn.net/Articles/1046228/) Bazzite in December 2025.
