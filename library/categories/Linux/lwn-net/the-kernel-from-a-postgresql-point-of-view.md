Andres Freund has [a few claims to fame](https://lwn.net/Articles/967866/), but chief among them is his many years of work to improve the performance of the [PostgreSQL](https://postgresql.org/) relational database management system. That work requires working with — or around — many Linux kernel features and behaviors. He put in an appearance at the 2026 edition of [Kernel Recipes](https://kernel-recipes.org/en/2026/) to talk about his experience working with the kernel project, how the kernel could better support applications like PostgreSQL, and some interesting developments in the PostgreSQL world.
