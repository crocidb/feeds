Mike Rapoport has [announced](https://lpc.events/blog/current/index.php/2026/10/19/lpc-2026-video-recordings-are-available/) the release of the video recordings from the [2026 Linux Plumbers Conference](https://lpc.events/event/20/) (LPC), which was held in Prague from October 5 through October 7. Find the complete set via the [YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLd3Gjl51NPg0) or look for the video links in the [schedule](https://lpc.events/event/20/timetable/#all). See also LWN's [coverage](https://lwn.net/Archives/ConferenceByYear/#2026-Linux_Plumbers_Conference) of talks from LPC.
