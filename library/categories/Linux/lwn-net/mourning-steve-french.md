From Jeremy Allison we have [the sad news](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7497016255946731520/) of the passing of Steve French. He was the maintainer of the kernel's SMB filesystem code for many years, having only [dropped that role](https://lwn.net/ml/all/20260818140738.314254-1-pc@manguebit.org/) due to health issues in the last week. > I've known Steve for over 20 years. He was a legend in the community, and a really good friend. He will be greatly missed. Farewell Steve.
