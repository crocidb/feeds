[Early in the 7.4 merge window](https://lwn.net/Articles/1101211/), Greg Kroah-Hartman applied a [patch](https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/commit/?id=fcbfaffee51a452cc8efcbaa3b88ec4595678424) that caused the kernel to be marked as tainted whenever user space manually binds or unbinds a device driver to a device. This change was meant to be a defense against a flood of spurious bug reports generated by fuzzers, but it also turned out to get in the way of some real use cases. After some discussion, the patch has been reverted, but this situation hints at an area where better kernel interfaces are needed.
