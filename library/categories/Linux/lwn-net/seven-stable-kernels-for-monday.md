Greg Kroah-Hartman has announced the [7.3.6](https://lwn.net/Articles/1108910/), [6.18.65](https://lwn.net/Articles/1108911/), [6.12.119](https://lwn.net/Articles/1108912/), [6.6.163](https://lwn.net/Articles/1108913/), [6.1.193](https://lwn.net/Articles/1108914/), [5.15.226](https://lwn.net/Articles/1109274/), and [5.10.275](https://lwn.net/Articles/1108915/) stable kernels. Each of these contains quite a few patches throughout the tree—6.12.119 has the fewest with more than 1,700 patches, while 6.6.163 has more than 3,300. Users are advised to upgrade.
