The KDE Project announced, in 2025, that [Plasma 6.7](https://kde.org/announcements/plasma/6/6.7.0/) would be the last version of the desktop environment that would support X11 sessions. Those who keep up with KDE's announcements no doubt expected it when [Plasma 6.8](https://kde.org/announcements/plasma/6/6.8.0/) dropped X11 support as promised. What seems to have come as a surprise, at least to a number of [Fedora KDE Plasma Desktop](https://fedoraproject.org/kde/) users, is that the Fedora 44 release—which shipped in April with [Plasma 6.6](https://kde.org/announcements/plasma/6/6.6.0/)—received an update to Plasma 6.8 that swept away the X11 session without much warning. Despite the surprise, it is gone for good: the [Fedora Engineering Steering Committee](https://docs.fedoraproject.org/en-US/fesco/) (FESCo) has [voted against](https://forge.fedoraproject.org/fesco/tickets/issues/3707#issuecomment-1371370) a request to revert the update for Fedora 44 and the just-released Fedora 45.
