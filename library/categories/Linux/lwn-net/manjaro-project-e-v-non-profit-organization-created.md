Frank Vandermeiren has [announced](https://forum.manjaro.org/t/the-manjaro-project-e-v-is-now-official/191197) the creation of the Manjaro Project e.V., a nonprofit organization in Germany that is independent of the Manjaro GmbH & Co KG company. This is following a [governance struggle](https://lwn.net/Articles/1063717/) that took place earlier this year between a number of [Manjaro Linux](https://manjaro.org/) contributors and Philip Müller, project lead and CEO of the Manjaro company.

>
>
> At the moment, we are still working on optimizing the new development infrastructure, which was partly necessitated by the ongoing (A.I.) bot attacks against our old infrastructure.
>
>
>
> However, as you may have noticed, the last [Testing Updates](https://forum.manjaro.org/c/announcements/testing-updates/13) and [Stable Updates](https://forum.manjaro.org/c/announcements/stable-updates/12) were initiated by the Manjaro Team without supervision from Philip Müller — with special thanks to @Yochanan for his hard work!
>
>

Dennis ten Hoove has [indicated](https://forum.manjaro.org/t/the-manjaro-project-e-v-is-now-official/191197/3) that "
> a transfer of company assets to the e.V. has not yet taken place

", and the group's next step is to ready the nonprofit for the transition and "
> reach out and engage in dialogue with the company

".
