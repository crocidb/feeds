The [7.4-rc1](https://lwn.net/Articles/1104653/) kernel prepatch is out, and the merge window is closed for this development cycle.

>  This is a \*BIG\* merge window. I think the reason is the by now obvious one, but I'm actually taking it as a good sign: I'm \*much\* happier with the flood of AI triggered work going into the merge window than it showing up mainly as extra fixes during the -rc period. So I'm hoping that this merge window being larger than usual is a sign that the AI fixes are becoming more part of the "normal development" than a flood of fixes that go into the whole stabilization phase. I think that's a much healthier model.

 There were 18,591 non-merge changesets brought in during this merge window — this is not just a record, but it nearly matches the patch volume during the entire (record-setting) 7.3 development cycle. So far, 2,678 developers have contributed to 7.4; 625 of them are first-time kernel contributors. Subscribers can find more details in [the KSDB 7.4 page](https://lwn.net/ksdb/releases/v7.4/).
