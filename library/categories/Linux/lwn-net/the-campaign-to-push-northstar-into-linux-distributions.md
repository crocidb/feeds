There is not a broad selection of mature, open-source web-browser engines for Linux users. Most of the browsers available for Linux are based on one of just three projects: Chromium's [Blink](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blink_(browser_engine)), Firefox's [Gecko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gecko_(software)), or [WebKit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WebKit). A new entrant would generally be seen as a welcome alternative, but the recent campaign to push one of the LLM-written [Nordstjernen](https://nordstjernen.org/) browsers into [Debian](https://debian.org/) and [Arch Linux](https://archlinux.org/) has raised some questions about the project's development and security processes.
