[OpenBSD 8.0](https://www.openbsd.org/80.html) has been released. There are many new features, including the addition of hibernate support for arm64, an increase in the kernel virtual address space for amd64 to 512GB, quite a few improvements for the [qwx](https://man.openbsd.org/qwx.4) driver that supports Qualcomm WiFi 6 devices, and much more. See [the full changelog](https://www.openbsd.org/plus80.html) for details.
