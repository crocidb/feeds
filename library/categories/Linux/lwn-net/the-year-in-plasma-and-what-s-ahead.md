A lot has happened in the [KDE Plasma desktop environment](https://kde.org/plasma-desktop/) in the last year. Marco Martin, a KDE contributor who spends most of his time working on Plasma, took the stage at [Akademy 2026](https://akademy.kde.org/2026/) in Graz, Austria to give an update on Plasma's major new features, some of the minor-but-interesting ones, and a preview of what's coming soon. The biggest upcoming change, dropping X11 support from Plasma, has been well-advertised; but there are also plans afoot to further improve remote-desktop support and more.
