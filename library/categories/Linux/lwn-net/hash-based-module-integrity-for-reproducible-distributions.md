We last looked in on [hash-based integrity checking for kernel modules](https://lwn.net/Articles/1012946/) just over a year and half ago. Since that time, the [patch set](https://lwn.net/ml/all/20260505-module-hashes-v5-0-e174a5a49fce@weissschuh.net/) has undergone multiple revisions. At the 2026 [Linux Plumbers Conference](https://lpc.events/) (LPC), Thomas Weißschuh described the need for kernel-module integrity guarantees while still providing reproducible builds, how his patches implement that, and some ideas for possible further work.
