After teasing the new System76 Thelio Prime last month, this week officially launched their redesigned Thelio Prime mini desktop PC. This AMD Ryzen 9000 series powered creation is quite small with an internal volume of just 9.78 liters but quite capable with up to the Ryzen 9 9900X being supported, AMD or NVIDIA discrete graphics options, up to 96GB of RAM, and up to 16TB of NVMe storage. I have been testing out the new Thelio Prime for the past few days so far and it's been stacking up as well as one of their best compact PCs yet.
