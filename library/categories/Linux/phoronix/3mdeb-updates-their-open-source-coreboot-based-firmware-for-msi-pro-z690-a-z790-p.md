Four years ago the 3mdeb firmware consulting firm managed an incredible feat in bringing Coreboot to a modern, retail desktop motherboard with the current Intel Core processors of the time. This was a port of their Coreboot-powered Dasharo firmware to the MSI PRO Z690-A motherboard and then to the newer MSI PRO Z790-P motherboard. Today they released updated firmware builds for those still making use of the Intel Alder Lake and Raptor Lake era hardware...
