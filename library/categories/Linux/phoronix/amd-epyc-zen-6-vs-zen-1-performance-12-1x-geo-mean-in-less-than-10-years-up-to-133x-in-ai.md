This month brought the availability of the new EPYC 9006 "Venice" SP7 Zen 6 server processors. We have seen excellent generational gains with the likes of the EPYC 9686F and EPYC 9996 over Zen 5 Turin CPUs as well as sharply outperforming the competition from Intel and NVIDIA. With the first-generation EPYC "Naples" processors having launched nearly ten years ago, I was curious to quantify AMD's server performance gains over the past decade. So up today is a look at how the EPYC 9996 and EPYC 9686F Zen 6 processors compare to the original EPYC Zen 1 flagship with the EPYC 7601 across real-world workloads for performance as well as power efficiency for some pretty epic gains.
