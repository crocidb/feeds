The [Sinclair](/space/com/sinclair) ZX81 was the first computer I ever owned:

<img src="/space/com/sinclair/zx81/sinclair-zx81.jpg?v=14df95970e1f" alt="Sinclair ZX81" width="480" height="360">Sinclair ZX81

<img src="/space/com/sinclair/zx81/sinclair-zx81-large.jpg?v=e63f0e725c53" alt="Sinclair ZX81 (large)" width="480" height="664">Sinclair ZX81 (large)

With 1KB RAM and a wobbly 16KB external expansion, it was around three times as dumb as your modern digital watch.
