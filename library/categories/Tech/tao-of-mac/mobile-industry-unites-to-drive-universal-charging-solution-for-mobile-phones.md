*Finally*, after over a decade, manufacturers come to their senses. I [have been ranting about this](/space/blog/2004/10/12) ([time](/space/blog/2005/07/09) and [time](/space/blog/2007/07/12) [again](blog/2007/07/21/1312)) for *years*

  Interestingly enough, I seem to have lost a link where, only a few days ago, some manufacturers (in response to an unusually lucid proposal from someone at the [EU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com), of all places) actually claimed this would be impossible to do - which, is, of course, bullshit. [Micro-USB](/space/hw/usb) can provide all the power and data connectivity required for today’s phones.
