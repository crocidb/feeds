I do not identify or profile visitors. The site does use a few third-party services for advertising, security and operational telemetry–the latter solely to detect abuse, failures and rendering problems:

* I use [EthicalAds](https://www.ethicalads.io?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) to serve advertisements. This is a privacy-focused ad network that does not track you, and I have no access to any data it may collect other than aggregate statistics about the ads served.
* I use [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com), which may use cookies to provide security and performance enhancements. Since it performs caching and other optimizations, it effectively breaks most tracking mechanisms, and I have no access to any data it may collect other than raw bandwidth and visitor statistics.
* I use operational telemetry (which I change from time to time as I test new things) to detect abuse, failures, broken pages and rendering problems. I only look at aggregated diagnostic information and do not use it to identify or profile visitors.
* Finally, external links to other sites may use `UTM` tracking parameters, but *only* when I explicitly link to them and solely because with the advent of the fully `HTTPS` web referral tracking has become impossible, and it is a nice way to let other people know I am linking to them if they use standard analytics software.
* I currently have the following `UTM` parameters set:
  * `utm_source: taoofmac.com`
  * `utm_medium: web`
  * `utm_campaign: unsolicited_traffic`
  * `utm_content: external_link`
