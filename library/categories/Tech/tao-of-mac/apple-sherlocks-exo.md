Well, it looks like Apple has decided to “Sherlock” [`exo`](https://github.com/exo-explore/exo?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com), which has been doing this for a good while. Given the popularity of M-series hardware for inference, I think this makes some sense, but also given the way that inference engines have evolved *very* quickly into optimised MoE streaming, I have my doubts it’s going to be necessary.

It does help sell more Macs, but, as usual, I don’t know if Apple will have the staying power to maintain this over time… Either way, I probably should have finished [`go-exotic`](https://rcarmo.github.io/projects/go-exotic/?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) – I’m kind of annoyed I had that idea back in Spring and never really got around to it.
