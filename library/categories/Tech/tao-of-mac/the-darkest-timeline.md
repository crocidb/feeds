It is times like [these](/space/links/2026/11/23/1724#jensen-huang-returns-as-nvidia-ceo) that test men’s resolve, they said, back when time was something you could actually enjoy rather than plough through at an ever-accelerating pace.

Now, I [fancy myself](/space/blog/2015/04/06/1940#a-short-rant-on-metaphysics) somewhat of a stoic. Not in the philosophical sense, mind. [Zeno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeno_of_Citium?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) and [Marcus Aurelius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcus_Aurelius?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) were, frankly, somewhat dreary and uninteresting reads, and my ability to quote either has thankfully faded away over the decades (quoting either was never popular at parties).

But I occasionally think of myself as such because throughout my career I have always been at the bleeding edge, which invariably means going through [some occasional rough patches](/space/blog/2024/06/08/1200#the-after) or even placing some fundamentally counter-intuitive [bets](/space/blog/2015/10/14/2230#moving-to-microsoft) that I *knew* would require me to plough through various [challenges](/space/blog/2017/11/05/2130#the-smallest-azure-iot-hub-client-ever).

It should be obvious by now that whatever I do, I typically do for the long run–this site hasn’t been around for nearly 25 years by accident.

>
>
> And, unironically, when [Apple](/space/com/apple) told me I would have to shutter it to join them some five years ago, I was *really* stuck on having to give it up–that wasn’t why I didn’t, and I don’t think I will ever write about the whys because that’s not the [Apple](/space/com/apple) way, but I quite liked the people I talked to at the time and there was a lot of (human, philosophical) alignment there, so that experience was a definite plus.
>
>

But even a stoic has limits. Stoicism used to be a blend of logic, physics and ethics, but to me it has always been a way to centre myself amidst constant change.

Now, in the space of a few months (and, [in my particular case today](/space/site/disclaimer), a few hours), [logic went out the window](/space/links/2026/11/18/1200#anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering), ethics is in an [extremely dicey position](/space/links/2026/11/23/1724#jensen-huang-returns-as-nvidia-ceo), and plain physics says I should take better care of my back: instead of slaving away at a job (and industry-related hobbies) that demand far too much of my time, I should probably just quit and become a carpenter.

Or something similar that lets me take in fresh air occasionally (or is in and of itself a breath of fresh air). After today, I might well do just that–I’ll need to sleep on it, at the very least.

The keen-eyed among you will have noticed I have not mentioned [AI](/space/ai#artificial-intelligence) until now–that is largely because it has been nothing but self-congruent (and increasingly cogent) throughout the year.

It’s clearly people (and tech industry culture/politics) that are the problem.
