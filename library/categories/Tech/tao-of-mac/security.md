One of the things I seldom talk about, both due to my [Disclaimer](/space/site/disclaimer) and to my ingrained dislike of the “it’s got to have a half-dozen firewalls to be secure” school of thought.

[

Resources
----------

](/space/security#resources)

These will be mostly non-obvious or intriguing ones - there are far too many people posting links to `bugtraq` and suchlike, although [Schneier](http://www.schneier.com/blog/?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) is mandatory reading if you actually want to use your *brain* to deal with security…

* [The Devil’s Infosec Dictionary](http://www.csoonline.com/read/080105/debrief.html?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com)

* [mod\_security](http://www.modsecurity.org/?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) - [Apache](/space/cli/apache) paranoia enhancer.

* The [NSA Lock-down guide](http://www.nsa.gov/snac/downloads_macX.cfm?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) for [Mac OS X](/space/com/apple/macos) [Panther](/space/com/apple/macos/panther) Client.

* [Bastille](http://www.bastille-linux.org/?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) - Lock-down helper for [Linux](/space/os/linux), [Mac OS X](/space/com/apple/macos) and [HP-UX](os/hp-ux)

* [Samsung TV root](https://github.com/chris-ritsen/samsung-tv-root?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) — Host-side root and control toolkit for Samsung Tizen TVs, with remote input, diagnostics and native TV controls; tested results are model- and firmware-specific, and root is volatile (Filed 2026-10-25.)

* [own-your-glass](https://github.com/JulioFerrero/own-your-glass?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) — POSIX shell toolkit and webOS app for hardening rooted LG TVs; mitigates telemetry, microphone and screen-capture leaks and ad tracking. Requires root and is validated only on webOS 10 (webOS 25) (Filed 2026-10-25.)

* [Stupid Security](http://www.stupidsecurity.com/?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com)

* [Tao Security](http://taosecurity.blogspot.com/?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) - pragmatists. I like pragmatists.
