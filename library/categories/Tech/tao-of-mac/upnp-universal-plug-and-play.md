[Universal Plug and Play](http://www.upnp.org/?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com), an [Intel-centric](Intel) norm that is used for things like dynamical location and configuration of network devices (it is quite different from [Rendezvous](Rendezvous) in the sense that it covers both service discovery and interaction).

It can used for remote control of [Windows](/space/com/microsoft/windows) Internet Sharing, punching holes in [UPNP-enabled](/space/protocols/upnp) firewalls ([MSN](MSN) and most [Bittorrent](Bittorrent) clients use it for precisely that purpose), etc., but most recently for configuration and media sharing among entertainment devices under DLNA.

Of course, [Apple](/space/com/apple) seem to keep wanting to do their own thing, and their own thing only, so there is no native [UPnP](/space/protocols/upnp) support in [Mac OS X](/space/com/apple/macos) - nor is it likely to ever happen, thanks to [AirPlay](/space/com/apple/airplay) and the like.
