The `.NET` framework is [Microsoft](/space/com/microsoft)’s take on [Java](/space/dev/java) - a single [VM](VM) for multiple platforms (essentially multiple versions of [Windows](Windows), until [Mono](Mono) came along) that can be targeted by several different language environments.

Damn impressive piece of engineering, and slightly better (architecturally) than [Java](/space/dev/java).
