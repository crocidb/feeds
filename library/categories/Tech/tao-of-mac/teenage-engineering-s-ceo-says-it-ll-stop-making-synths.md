This was not on my bingo card for… well, this decade, given the relative success of their more recent gear like the [KO II](https://teenage.engineering/products/ep-133?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com).

Sure, there are some really strange things in the line-up, like the [medieval flavour](/space/links/2024/08/07/0831#ep-1320-medieval), and their UX can stray well beyond quirky into cuckoo land. But they’ve always been a source of whimsy and musical creativity–[my OP-1](/space/blog/2021/04/17/1610#the-op-1) being a case in point.

Then again, things like the [M-VAVE FM-1](/space/com/m-vave/fm-1#m-vave-fm-1) are vastly cheaper these days (and arguably have better hardware features). They’re hackable, too, with an active ecosystem–which I suspect will dictate the success of future music hardware.
