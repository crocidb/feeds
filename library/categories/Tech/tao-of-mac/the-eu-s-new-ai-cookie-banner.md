This is going to become the new cookie banner, only spectacularly less useful–everyone will ignore the labels, and anyone trying to deceive people will simply remove them.

I asked my AI for its take on this, and it thinks machine-readable provenance is the potentially useful part: it gives platforms and editors something to inspect, but only says that a co-operating tool marked a file. Its absence proves nothing, and screenshots or re-encoding can strip metadata and defeat some marks (although robust watermarking can sometimes survive transformations). [I was already worried in 2023](/space/links/2023/03/21/1911) that people and media were not ready for convincing deepfakes, and training everyone to ignore another mandatory badge will not help. ![AI-generated](https://taoofmac.com/static/img/ai-label.svg)
