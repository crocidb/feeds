BeOS was an operating system developed by Be Inc. It was conceived for the company’s BeBox personal computer which was released in 1995, and it was great at multitasking, with a very characteristic graphical user interface. It lives on as [Haiku OS](https://www.haiku-os.org/?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com), an open-source reimplementation, which has been in development… forever.

[

Historical Notes
----------

](/space/os/beos#historical-notes)

Originally created by [Be](http://www.be.com?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) (which went out of business and was bought by [Palm](/space/com/palm)), BeOS was a very fast operating system (it could *boot* in 20 seconds), lightweight and efficient.

It failed partly for lack of *hardware* support and partly because of some irritating incompatibilities with the [POSIX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/POSIX?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) standard (and the resulting lack of *software*). It is now known as the successor to [AmigaOS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AmigaOS?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) in the “operating system that never dies” category.

BeMD2
