Google Reader was [Google](/space/com/google)’s [RSS](/space/markup/rss) and [Atom](/space/standards/ietf/atom) feed aggregator, with support for [OPML](/space/markup/opml) import.

It had passable support for mobile devices (it even worked on the [Wii](/space/com/nintendo/wii), and the usual [Google](/space/com/google) `KISS` design, Ajaxy bits and decent usability (including [`vim`](/space/cli/vim) keybindings for the geek crowd).

Sadly, it did not support any kind of automated filtering (or classification) whatsoever, and was killed by Google in 2013.
