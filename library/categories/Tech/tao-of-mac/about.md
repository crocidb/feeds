Tao of Mac is my personal wiki. It has been online since 2002 and contains articles, weekly notes, reviews and reference pages about technology, products, books, photography and my other interests.

I only publish information that is already public and useful to me as a reference. I do not write about ongoing work, projects, customers or suppliers, and everything here is my own opinion. See the [disclaimer](/space/site/disclaimer) for details.

My [profile](https://carmo.io/?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) is at `carmo.io`, and my software projects are listed under [Open-source work](https://rcarmo.github.io/?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com).
