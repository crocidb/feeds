Google acknowledging that some Android apps don’t run well on Intel Googlebooks is about what I’d expected. One of Android’s hidden catches is that Google has been neglecting it on Intel for a long time, and there isn’t a polished ARM-to-x86 translation layer to make up for it.

The main reason the [Lenovo Chromebook I tested](/space/reviews/2026/10/04/1800#android-apps) had such a seamless Android app experience was its MediaTek processor, and I really think the best Googlebooks are going to be the ARM ones.
