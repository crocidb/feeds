[Textream](https://textream.net/?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) is an open-source teleprompter with word tracking, constant-speed scrolling and voice-activated scrolling. On the Mac, it can display scripts in a notch overlay, a floating window or fullscreen. It requires macOS 15 Sequoia or newer and supports both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs; an iPhone and iPad companion is also available.

My [Swift Smart Prompter](https://rcarmo.github.io/projects/swift-smart-prompter/?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) uses a live transcript to provide meeting-agenda cues, whereas Textream follows a prepared script.

The [source code](https://github.com/f/textream?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) is available on GitHub.
