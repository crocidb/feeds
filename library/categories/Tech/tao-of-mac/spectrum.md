The [Sinclair](/space/com/sinclair) ZX Spectrum was one of the first widely sold home computers (way back in the 80s – I actually started out with a [ZX81](/space/com/sinclair/zx81)).

It is also one of the most widely emulated (I won’t even try to list all the emulators here, only the ones that stand out in one way or another).

I used to own a plain 48K:

…and later, a 128K “+3″:
