And this lands just as the AI industry’s [“teaser period”](https://www.groundbrkr.com/p/the-teaser-period-why-the-ai-boom?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) is supposed to end–when vast take-or-pay compute commitments become actual bills and utilisation starts to matter. Nothing says “winning the AI race” quite like making the entire stack more expensive just before the economics get real…
