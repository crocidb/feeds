[**XScreenSaver**](http://www.jwz.org/xscreensaver/?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) is the time-honored [X11](/space/apps/x11) modular screen saver suite written by [Jamie Zawinski](http://www.jwz.org?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) (he of [Netscape](/space/com/netscape) fame), which is now available as a set of [macOS](/space/com/apple/macos) screen saver modules.

Nothing like a bouncing cow or a swarm of [OpenGL](dev/opengl) flying toasters to spruce up a [UNIX](/space/os/unix) box, be it a [Mac](/space/com/apple/mac) or anything else…
