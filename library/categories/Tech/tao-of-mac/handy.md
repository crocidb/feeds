[Handy](https://handy.computer?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) is an open-source, cross-platform dictation app that transcribes speech locally using Whisper or Parakeet models and pastes the result into the active application.

Its optional text post-processing can use either local or cloud models: Apple Intelligence on supported Macs, a custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint (including a local server), or providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter and Groq. Speech recognition remains local, but a cloud post-processing provider receives the transcribed text.

The built-in “Improve Transcriptions” prompt removes fillers, fixes spelling and punctuation, and converts spoken numbers and punctuation while preserving the original language and meaning. The result is cleaned-up dictation, much like Rambler for Android. Post-processing is optional and disabled by default, and its prompts can be customised.

See also [TypeWhisper](/space/apps/typewhisper) and the [speech recognition catalogue](/space/ai/speech#ai-speech-technologies). The [source code](https://github.com/cjpais/Handy?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) is available under the MIT license.
