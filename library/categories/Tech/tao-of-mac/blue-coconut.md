[Blue Coconut](http://husk.org/apps/blue_coconut/index.html?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) is a [Mac OS X](/space/com/apple/macos) application that enables you to download individual tracks from shared [iTunes](/space/apps/itunes) music libraries.

The underlying [Perl](/space/dev/perl) libraries might be very useful for anyone looking for a simple [DAAP](/space/com/apple/daap) client implementation.
