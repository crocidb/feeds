[MacPacker](https://macpacker.app?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) is an open-source macOS archive manager that lets you browse nested archives without extracting them first and extract individual files. It supports more than 40 formats, Finder Quick Look previews, encrypted archives and ZIP editing.

It requires macOS 14.6 or newer. The [source code](https://github.com/sarensw/MacPacker?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) is available under GPLv3.
