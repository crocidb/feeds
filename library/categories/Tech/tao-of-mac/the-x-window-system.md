Before [X.org](http://www.x.org?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com) came along, I made a note of this:

>
>
> *The last wide-spread functional enhancement exported by the X server might well be the Shape extension, designed (in the hot tub) at the 1989 Winter Usenix in San Diego.* (quoted from [here](http://www.xfree86.org/~keithp/talks?utm_campaign=unsolicited_traffic&utm_medium=web&utm_source=taoofmac.com)
>
>

These days most people use (or are only aware of alternatives like [Terminal Services](/space/protocols/rdp), [VNC](/space/protocols/vnc), or [NX](apps/nx), but the ancient lore is still valuable.
