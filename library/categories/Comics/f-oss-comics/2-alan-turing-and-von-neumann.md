Alan Turing described an abstract machine that clarified what computation could mean. From wartime codebreaking and ENIAC's plugboards to the stored-program designs of EDVAC, ACE, and EDSAC, this episode traces how Turing, John von Neumann, and several engineering teams contributed to the architecture of modern computers.
