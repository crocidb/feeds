The origins of hacker culture reach back to early programming communities and MIT's PDP-1 enthusiasts, who created software such as Spacewar!, text editors, and music programs. Project MAC, the AI Lab, ITS, and ARPANET helped this culture spread and influence today's open-source hacker community.
