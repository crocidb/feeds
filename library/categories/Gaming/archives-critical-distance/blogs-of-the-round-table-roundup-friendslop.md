Welcome back, everyone!

I am extremely pleased to present to you the entrants in the Blogs of the Round Table “Friendslop” Essay Jam—all **twenty-four** of them! This has been an incredible turnout for a jam that was really just meant to put out feelers for future, non-jam editions of this feature. Thank you all for showing up, showing out, and showing us the power of focused and directed criticism. I can’t yap for too long, though, because it’s time to get into the reading—and there is *so much* to read! Enjoy these pieces on “friendslop” from incredible critics from around the world.
