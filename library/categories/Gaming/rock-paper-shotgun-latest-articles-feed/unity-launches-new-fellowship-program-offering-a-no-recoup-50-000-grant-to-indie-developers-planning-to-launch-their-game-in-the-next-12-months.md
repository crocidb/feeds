[Unity](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/companies/unity)'s next venture, amidst [rumours](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/after-taking-over-ea-saudi-arabia-and-their-private-equity-cronies-have-reportedly-held-talks-about-buying-unity) of it being bought out by the Saudi Arabian Public Investment Fund, is the [Unity Fellowship](https://unity.com/unity-fellowship), a grant program that offers $50k in cash to Unity-using indie devs that plan release a game within a year - without asking for recoup, revenue share, creative control or IP ownership in exchange. I don't need to tell you that all sounds too good to be true, even with the Unity engine requirement, but in fairness, the company has form for handing out cash. The [Unity for Humanity](https://unity.com/humanity) grant, just earlier this year, [spread $600K](https://unity.com/blog/unity-for-humanity-2026-winner-announcement) across ten different indie projects focused on societal and environmental issues.
