Split-screen multiplayer is often treated by developers as a forgotten vestige of a bygone age, something to be left behind as the standardisation of online play makes it redundant, especially following the pandemic. It's considered unlikely that you'll have another human being in your home to pick up a controller with you, and since you've inevitably got an internet connection, why not just use that to connect to anyone in the world? I find there's a unique beauty in playing something shoulder to shoulder on a couch with another person, and it seems Fuse Games sees that light too, with [Star Wars: Galactic Racer](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/star-wars-galactic-racer) set to be [updated](https://bsky.app/profile/starwarsgracer.bsky.social/post/3n2gweii57j2e) to add local splitscreen multiplayer sometime early next year.
