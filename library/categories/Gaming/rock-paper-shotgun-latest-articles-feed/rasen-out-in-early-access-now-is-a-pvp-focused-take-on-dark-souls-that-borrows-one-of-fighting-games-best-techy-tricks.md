 The idea of [Dark Souls](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/dark-souls) PvP has always scared me a tad. You can replace Dark Souls with [Bloodborne](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/bloodborne) or [Elden Ring](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/elden-ring-boss-locations), I've typically just been a coward when it comes to the idea of fighting another, real person. They're probably going to be better than me! I don't use tactics in Souls games, I use brute force until I get my way! That being said, I do have to admit I quite like the look of Rasen, a game that is essentially just the PvP component of Dark Souls (or PvE if you just want to take on some computer-controlled enemies) that just launched into early access.
