Following a round of [layoffs](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/gravity-well-lays-off-more-than-40-developers-after-losing-its-funding) last month that resulted in the loss of 40 jobs, which itself came after a previous source of funding pulled out in the Spring, independent studio Gravity Well has now announced that it is going into "hibernation" for the foreseeable future. The team was largely made up of devs who'd left [Titanfall](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/titanfall) and [Apex Legends](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/apex-legends) studio Respawn Entertainment, and under the Gravity Well banner had been working on a new, still-unannounced game for around six years.
