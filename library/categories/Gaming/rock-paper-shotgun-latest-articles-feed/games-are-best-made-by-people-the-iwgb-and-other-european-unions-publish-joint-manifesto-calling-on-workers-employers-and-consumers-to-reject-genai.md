As part of a summit meeting in Manchester yesterday, the IWGB Game Workers union have joined up with other unions spanning the Republic of Ireland's GWUI, Germany's GDRT, Italy's FIOM-CGIL, France's STJV, and Spain's CSVI to publish a [manifesto](https://www.gameworkers.co.uk/ai-manifesto/) that aims to "bring to light the risks and dangers genAI poses to the workforce" for consumers, workers and publishers alike.

The manifesto states that "GenAI is currently harming the games industry, its workers and the games they produce," and suggests actions that can be taken by all to reduce harm done to the industry. The manifesto specifies the "severe harm to the environment, the destruction of communities and the exploitation of the Global South" as wider harms of the technology, but maintains its focus on the industry itself.
