I'm a reasonable man. I don't expect *every* PS1-styled, cosmic horror, nakedly Alien: Resurrection-homaging retro FPS to suffocate me under a blanket of misery. Some of the lighter moments in Derelikt, which [released last week](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3810510/DERELIKT/), even land: I was grinning my cheeks off as I gorged on the Eldtrich knowledge sealed within an alien obelisk, while repeatedly hanging up on the aghast scientist who kept videocalling me to stop.
