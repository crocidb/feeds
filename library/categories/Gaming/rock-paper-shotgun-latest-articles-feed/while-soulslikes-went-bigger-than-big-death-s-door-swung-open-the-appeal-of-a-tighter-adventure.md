Declaring a Best Way of Doing Things is a fool’s game, though in recent years my favourite entries in the Tricky Hitting genre – inclusive of soulslikes and their hybridised disciples – have been the big, bulging ones. [Elden Rings](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/elden-ring) and [Silksongs](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/hollow-knight-silksong), with vast kingdoms and deep, rattling toolkits for surviving them. The reason it’s a fool’s game is because you can *also* have something like Death’s Door, a taut, compact, lighthearted little actioner that fills eight hours instead of 80 and yet never feels underwhelming or compromised for it.
