 Since the beginning of the year, I have watched almost 150 films. I know this, because over the past few years, I've become one of those people… a Letterboxd user. Gasp! I know, I know, but I don't think I'm *too* insufferable about it. I'll only do a joke review in the hopes of like farming if I think it's genuinely funny! But many of these films were watched digitally, meaning I don't have a bunch of DVDs to admire on a shelf. So what am I to do? Well, I could check out Alt Video, a not quite job simulator where you can use your Letterboxd account or media server to fill up a digital, faux Blockbuster.
