Creative Assembly's [Total War: Medieval 3](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/total-war-medieval-3) is going to do a lot more with diplomacy, the developers promise. Diplomacy! The soul of any Total War game. They're going to build on the example of [Total War: Three Kingdoms](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/total-war-three-kingdoms), which does allow for some gratifying backroom dealing, in fairness. During my first Thringdoms runthrough, I played Cao Cao, a ruler gifted with the ability to hypnotise people into liking him while hating each other. I spent many idle hours instigating proxy wars. Medieval 3 is apparently going to take all that to "new heights". It is going to make Han politicking seem as sophisticated as texting "get your filthy hands off my bae" to everybody you know on Valentine's Day.
