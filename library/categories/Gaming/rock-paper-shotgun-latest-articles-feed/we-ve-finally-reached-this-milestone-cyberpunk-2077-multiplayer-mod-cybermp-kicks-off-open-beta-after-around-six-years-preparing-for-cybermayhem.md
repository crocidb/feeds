After a couple of years of [regular testing](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/we-feel-were-reaching-a-new-level-cyberpunk-2077s-multiplayer-mod-just-completed-its-most-stable-and-successful-test-yet) behind closed doors, [Cyberpunk 2077](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/cyberpunk-2077) multiplayer mod CyberMP is now out in open beta, giving anyone who fancies a bit of multi-merc action a chance to give it a go. Though, if you do, be prepared for bugs and crashes, as work on the mod is far from over with the release of this "true beta".
