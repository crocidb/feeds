 For publicly traded game developers, there is surely one question, with all manner of variations, that will surely be coming up again and again over the next few months: what impact will [GTA 6](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/grand-theft-auto-vi) have on your games? CD Projekt Red were subjected to such questioning in their latest earnings call, part of which was related to sales figures of The Witcher 3 and [Cyberpunk 2077](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/cyberpunk-2077), that boring kind of thing. But CEO Michal Nowakowski did also respond to a comment about how GTA 6 may affect the development of [The Witcher 4](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/the-witcher-4) and Cyberpunk 2.
