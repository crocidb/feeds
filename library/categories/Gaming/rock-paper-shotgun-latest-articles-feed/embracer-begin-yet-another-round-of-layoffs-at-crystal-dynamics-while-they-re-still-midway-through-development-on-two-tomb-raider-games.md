**Update 18/11:** Crystal Dynamics have provided the following statement to RPS, confirming it cut eight staff members:

 "Crystal Dynamics made a difficult, but necessary business decision to reduce our current studio staff by 8 people. Whenever we phase roles off a project, we make every effort to find new spots for our great colleagues first. Everyone affected receives a full severance package as well as job placement assistance to aid them through this transition. We wish them the best and hope to work together in the future."

The original article continues below.

As initially reported by [Vertical Slice](https://www.verticalslice.news/report-embracer-studio-crystal-dynamics-has-seemingly-begun-its-fifth-layoff-round-in-two-years/) (thanks, Chris!), the workforce of [Tomb Raider](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/shadow-of-the-tomb-raider) studio Crystal Dynamics is being cut as part of a new set of layoffs by owners Embracer. As far as is currently known, those affected include senior game designer Joshua Mitchell, senior environment technical artist Kevin Baker, and UI software engineer Garin R. K. Richards, all of whom shared news of their redunancies on LinkedIn. There may be more affected, but neither Crystal Dynamics nor Embracer have offered comments on the reduction of staff as yet.
