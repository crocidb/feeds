 I often find it quite dull that the main takeaway far too many people have taken from [Dark Souls](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/dark-souls), aside from thinking that the most interesting thing is its combat, is that it's all so bloomin' grimdark. I'm not saying I need sunshine and rainbows, I'd just like a vibe that's even remotely trying to be different from FromSoftware's seminal work. And so! I am quite pleased to discover MycoRelic, a [Soulslike](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/the-best-soulslike-games) that pits you as a robot that covers its body in mushroom parts that offer elemental powers.
