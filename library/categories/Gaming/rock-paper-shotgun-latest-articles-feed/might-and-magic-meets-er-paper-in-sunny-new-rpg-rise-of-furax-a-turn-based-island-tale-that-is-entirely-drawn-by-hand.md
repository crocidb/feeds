Literal decades ago, I tried to make a first-person RPG in HyperCard. I got as far as a fateful town gate confrontation before hitting the cap on slides for the shareware version. I mention this because new, 100% "hand-drawn" first-person dungeon crawler Rise of Furax is uncannily redolent of my long-lost HyperCard game.

This risks being a) a terrible indictment of the game's visual direction, and b) an insinuation that developers Rap2h are dastardly rip-off artists, so let me elaborate that c) I think Rise of Furax looks far, far lovelier than my lousy primordial hypermedia confection and d) it is a legally distinct creation. That unfinished HyperCard concept never left the confines of a Macintosh Performa that is even now rusting on a riverbed. If only I'd pitched it somewhere, I'd probably be a MILLIONAIRE by now. A million pounds is still a lot of money, right? Anyway, here's a teaser video for Rise of Furax.
