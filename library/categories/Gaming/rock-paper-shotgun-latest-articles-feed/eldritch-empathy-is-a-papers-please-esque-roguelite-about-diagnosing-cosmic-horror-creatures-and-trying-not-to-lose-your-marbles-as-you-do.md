 Oh I do love an eldritch horror creature, I do, especially one that at first glance may only be slightly different from what I'm used to seeing. [Eldritch](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/eldritch) Empathy, then, seems like a grand ole game to satisfy my wanton need to experience such things. It's a puzzle [roguelite](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/best-roguelike-games-pc) that isn't exactly a [Papers Please](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/papers-please)-like but certainly feels reminiscent of one, where you have to "analyse corrupted creatures, purify their emotions and learn how to communicate with an eldritch being."
