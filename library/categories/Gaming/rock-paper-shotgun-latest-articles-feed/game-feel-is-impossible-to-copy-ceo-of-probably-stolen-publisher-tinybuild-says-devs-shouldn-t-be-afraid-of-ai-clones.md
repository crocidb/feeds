[GenAI game clones](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/endless-pile-of-needle-in-haystack-games-begins-to-emerge-as-viral-hit-imitators-devs-claim-their-code-was-nicked-by-a-genai-using-clone) are apparently becoming a real enough threat to the industry that Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick [pre-warned](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/ahead-of-gta-6-and-as-concerns-over-genai-pc-ports-grow-take-twos-ceo-says-theyre-revving-up-their-technical-defences-and-legal-activites-over-piracy) "this is likely to invade our business to a greater or lesser extent," while threatening legal action against anyone who naughtily tinkered with [GTA VI](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/grand-theft-auto-vi). More recently, one or more apparent crypto grifters claim to have naughtily tinkered with GTA VI, sparking attempts to [vibe-recomp](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/the-torch-passes-to-the-open-source-recomp-and-emulation-projects-gta-6-leaker-cyberleek-claims-to-have-released-crimsims-xbox-executable-and-denies-crypto-grifting) an unoffical PC version. However, contrasting Zelnick's position is tinyBuild CEO Alex Nichiporchik, who quite confidently stated in an interview with [Gamesindustry.biz](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/one-way-to-stave-off-ai-clones-minimise-the-gap-between-announcement-and-launch) that "devs shouldn't be afraid of it."
