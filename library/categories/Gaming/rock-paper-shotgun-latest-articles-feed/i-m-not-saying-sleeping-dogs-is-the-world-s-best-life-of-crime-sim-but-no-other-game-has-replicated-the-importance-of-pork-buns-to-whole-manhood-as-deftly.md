For absolutely [no reason whatsoever](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/in-a-reveal-that-might-worry-eventual-pc-players-glancing-at-their-gpus-gta-6-will-target-30fps-on-all-consoles), I’ve recently fired [Sleeping Dogs](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/sleeping-dogs) up again. The United Front Games-developed, Square Enix-published open-world crime romp is still as good as it’s always been, and still occupies its own corner in the dingy bar where the GTAs and GTA-likes hang out. Back in 2015, RPS co-founderman Alec Meer (RPS in peace) [referred to it](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/have-you-played-sleeping-dogs) as “the halfway house between GTA's sneering arrogance and [Saints Row](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/saints-row)'s chaotic nonchalance”. Fair enough, I’ve never been that big a Saints Row guy, but I see slumbering canines a bit differently.
