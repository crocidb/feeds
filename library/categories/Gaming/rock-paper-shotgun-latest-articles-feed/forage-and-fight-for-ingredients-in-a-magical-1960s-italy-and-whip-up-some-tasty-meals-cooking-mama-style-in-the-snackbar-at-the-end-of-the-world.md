 If a game is about cooking, at minimum I'll have to give it a quick testspin. Outside of this whole writing about video games thing, my other lot in life is soul bound to food, so I'm always curious to see how the two can be mixed together. And earlier this week, The Snackbar at the End of the World was revealed, a wonderfully cartoonish action-adventure game set in a magical, slightly post-apocalyptic version of 1960s Italy where you go out and forage for ingredients while fending off monstrous crops before turning them into tasty meals back in your struggling restaurant.
