Fine Monday to ya, reader. In the interest of allowing Edwin more time to spend with his interview transcripts, you’ve got me on Maw duty this week. Not much chance of it going ungorged, in any case – while the continued bellows of [Grand Theft Auto VI](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/grand-theft-auto-vi) have frightened away most of the air-quotes "big" games from this late November habitat, many others remain, undaunted. Or because they probably thought the Venn diagram of GTA fans and grand strategy expansion fans was just two rings.

There’s still some Vice City-adjacent matters we’ll be covering this week – chief among them the [Rockstar North unionisation vote](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/uk-based-rockstar-devs-will-be-putting-their-union-recognition-to-a-vote-during-gta-6s-launch-next-month), which is set to close in the coming days – but as I said to Edwin and Mark this morning, the cool thing about the continued lack of a PC version is that it leaves open a space to be filled with other things. Judging by what’s out this week, there’s certainly no shortage to choose from.
