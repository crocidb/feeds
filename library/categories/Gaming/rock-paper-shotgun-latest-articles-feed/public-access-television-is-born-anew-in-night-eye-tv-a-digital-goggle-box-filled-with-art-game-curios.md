 I am both a bit too young and a bit too very not American to have ever experienced public-access television, but it's something that's always fascinated me. For those too young to have even heard of it, or just not in the know, it essentially allowed anyone to broadcast their own shows through cable TV. A proto YouTube, if you will. It's obviously not what it was any more, given the advent of the internet, but in [NIGHTEYE.TV](http://NIGHTEYE.TV), it may be making a digital, more interactive return.
