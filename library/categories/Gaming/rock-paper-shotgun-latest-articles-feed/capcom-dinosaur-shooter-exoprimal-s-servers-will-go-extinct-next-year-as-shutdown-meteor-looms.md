[Exoprimal](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/exoprimal), Capcom's 2023 team shooter pitting exosuited fighters against T-rexes, is the latest game to be handed an expiration date. Its online services will cease as of late September 2027, rendering it unplayable, with the game set to be delisted from stores in March.
