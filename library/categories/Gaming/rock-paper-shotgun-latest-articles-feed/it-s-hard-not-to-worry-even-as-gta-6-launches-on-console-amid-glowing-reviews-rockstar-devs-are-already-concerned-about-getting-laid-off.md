[GTA 6](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/grand-theft-auto-vi) launches on console today. It's widely expected to sell more copies than main character Lucia Caminos has [skin pores](https://www.instagram.com/p/DfkbBcTAa6f/), more copies than there are words even in [Eurogamer's five-star review](https://www.eurogamer.net/grand-theft-auto-6-review), which I believe Chris Tapsell has written specifically to exhaust and destroy such neurones as I have remaining. And yet, staff at Rockstar Games are apparently worrying about being laid off, as development winds down and investors start to bicker about Line Going Up Even More.

In an article supporting this month's union vote at Rockstar North, one of the game's animation programmers argues that "workers across the sector are helpless to resist the waves of redundancies crashing around us, until we get organised."
