Dancing Dragons Games and Hooded Horse have announced Symphony of War II: Dominion, the sequel to 2022's [Symphony of War: The Nephilim Saga](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/symphony-of-war-the-nephilim-saga). [Sin noted the original](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/the-rally-point-symphony-of-war-makes-everyone-on-the-battlefield-feel-important) as pretty special for being "a *good* strategy game", highlighting the unique importance of taking care of each and every army member, rather than just benching the ones that are less effective and/or pretty to look at. The Nephilism Saga is also somewhat rare in being a turn-based tactics game made in [RPG Maker](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/rpg-maker), as seems to be the case with its sequel, which is expanding to include naval combat.
