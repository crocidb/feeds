[GTA 6](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/grand-theft-auto-vi) leaker or leakers Cyberleek claim to to have released the main executable file for an alleged Xbox build of the game that they [asserted they'd lobbed onto the net](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/gta-6-leaker-claims-to-have-lobbed-a-near-complete-xbox-build-of-rockstars-crimsim-onto-the-net-shouts-that-the-community-will-have-it-emulated-inside-a-week) last week. In a fresh statement, the leakers alluded to the excutable being used in attempts to create an unofficial PC port of the crimsim, and denied that their use of cryptocurrency is just a grift.
