It's a joyous day for lawyers representing plucky little game and console makers Sony. A UK class action lawsuit which accused the company of using the PlayStation Store's dominant market position to fuel "excessive and unfair" prices for digital games has been dismissed in its entirety by the [Competition Appeal Tribunal](https://www.catribunal.org.uk/) in London.
