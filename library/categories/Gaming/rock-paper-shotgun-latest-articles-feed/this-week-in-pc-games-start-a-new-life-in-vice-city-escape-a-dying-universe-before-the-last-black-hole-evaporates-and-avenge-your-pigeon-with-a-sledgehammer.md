Happy this week, all! Please throw yourself upon the floor in consternation, clawing at your face and hair, for the release of [Grand Theft Auto 6](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/grand-theft-auto-vi) is nigh. In just a few short days, every gaming vertical under the sun will be publishing features about the exact depth of Jason Duval's ripped abs and what this Says About America Today, or how many bottle caps you must gather to unlock Lucia Caminos' Gundam mode.

Fortunately for my sanity, Rockstar's crime colossus isn't yet nigh on PC, though the word is that modders driven nuts by Claude will [soon be along with an unofficial "port"](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/its-a-brave-new-world-gta-6s-pc-port-will-release-sooner-than-you-think-to-avoid-losing-money-to-ai-rip-offs-says-former-rockstar-tech-director). As such, while we do have a few Grandiosely Thievish articles in store this week – not least, coverage of [the on-going unionisation situation](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/uk-based-rockstar-devs-will-be-putting-their-union-recognition-to-a-vote-during-gta-6s-launch-next-month) at Rockstar – we can afford to spend a few hours writing about non-GTA things. The appraisal of mushrooms, for example, or the solubility of rubber hosing, or the parrying of spells. Without further ado, here's this week's uncommonly flavourful crop of new PC games. Don't worry, dear Maw. You'll get to eat Jason eventually.
