Despite being announced back in 2022 with an initial release window of 2025, NEKCOM's action RPG [Showa American Story](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/showa-american-story) has been set for a late 2026 release date for a while now. That seems to have changed, however, as the release date on its [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1687540/Showa_American_Story/) has slipped to 2027 without any sort of announcement as to why.
