Your next gaming monitor should be an OLED display. There, I've said it. And I know the notion won't please everyone. After all, a large grey proboscidean lumbers into the room whenever OLED technology is mentioned. Put simply, almost any risk of the dreaded burn-in is an immediate deal-breaker for some. I do get that.

Likewise, despite my musings just the other day on [how affordable gaming monitors have generally become](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/amid-soaring-pc-hardware-prices-gaming-monitors-are-getting-cheaper-and-cheaper-heres-why-and-what-to-buy) and the fact you can now snag an OLED panel, specifically, for sub £300 / $300, it's a stretch to say OLED gaming comes cheap. And yet here I am arguing firmly in favour of OLED.
