"Nobody was hurt" in a shooting last week near the California office of [Clockwork Revolution](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/clockwork-revolution) developers InXile, both the studio and local police have confirmed. The incident, which took place on November 11th, is currently being investigated and police have reportedly taken a suspect into custody.
