[Goat Simulator](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/goat-simulator) and [Satisfactory](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/satisfactory) outfit Coffee Stain Group have bought the intellectual property rights and assets for Chronicles: Medieval, the [Tom Hardy-garnished](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/chronicles-medieval-is-a-strategy-rpg-with-massive-battles-from-witcher-and-grand-theft-auto-alumni) strategy RPG that was originally in development at now-bankrupt Raw Power Games. The game will now be made by a new developer, owned 25% by a few former Raw Power devs and 75% by Coffee Stain. Coffee Stain will fund the game's on-going development.
