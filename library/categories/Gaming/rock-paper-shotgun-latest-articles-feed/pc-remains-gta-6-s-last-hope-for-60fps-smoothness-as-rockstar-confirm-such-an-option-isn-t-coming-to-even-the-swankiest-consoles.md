As you've probably heard, [GTA 6](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/grand-theft-auto-vi) is out on consoles this week and [targeting 30 FPS](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/in-a-reveal-that-might-worry-eventual-pc-players-glancing-at-their-gpus-gta-6-will-target-30fps-on-all-consoles) on that hardware. Despite [reports claiming](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/gta-6-pc-release-planned-for-early-2027-report-claims-which-could-mean-were-playing-in-time-for-gta-online-2-vice-city-4-u-or-whatever-they-call-it) one may not be far away, there's still no official word on a PC port, hence why we've not got a review that delves deep into analysis of just how central to the experience the [smell reaction system](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/gta-6-has-a-smell-reaction-system-which-immediately-joins-red-dead-redemption-2s-dynamically-shrinking-horse-balls-on-rockstars-list-of-immersive-symbols-of-excess) is.

The wait for confirmation of a PC version continues, then, and it looks like that might well be the only chance people get to play the crime sandbox at 60fps, with Rockstar having confirmed that higher frame rate modes aren't en-route to even the most beefy of consoles.
