An update for [The Binding of Isaac](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/the-binding-of-isaac) packed with wink wink, nudge nudge parody of [GTA 6](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/grand-theft-auto-vi) has backfired rather spectacularly, with publishers Nicalis having to make some hasty modifications. This free addition to the Repentance+ version of the roguelike featured a Grand Terry Auto-inspired daily run, complete with cops, guns, and a soundtrack players soon highlighted as sounding AI-generated. Sure enough, Nicalis subsequently announced the "removal of generated music files" in response to the backlash.
