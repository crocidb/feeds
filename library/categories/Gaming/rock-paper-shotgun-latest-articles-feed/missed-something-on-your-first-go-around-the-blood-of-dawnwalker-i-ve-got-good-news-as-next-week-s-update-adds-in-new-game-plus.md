 A big part of the point of [The Blood of Dawnwalker](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/dawnwalker) is that you're on a fixed time limit. You've got 30 in-game days and nights to try and save your family, and if you don't manage to see everything the game has to offer, well, [the game's director is fine with that](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/youre-probably-not-going-to-see-everything-your-friend-does-in-the-blood-of-dawnwalker-and-its-director-is-just-fine-with-that). But that doesn't matter as much now, as [The Blood of Dawnwalker](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/the-blood-of-dawnwalker) is getting its 1.2 update next week that adds in a new game plus mode.
