 The promise of user-generated content, this idea that you could host a platform that grows infinitely because the people that interact with it make things for it and you don't even have to pay anyone, is an alluring one. And its siren call has come for [Dead by Daylight](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/dead-by-daylight), as developer Behaviour Interactive have outlined the future of the game, which includes a way to make your own bits for the multiplayer [horror](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/the-best-horror-games) game.
