 There can be such a wonderful charm that comes from fan games because the people that make them really are just in it for the love of the (literal) game. They ain't gonna make any money from this thing! So I'm quite tickled by OpenZ, a 3D remake of the 1996 [RTS](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/the-best-strategy-games-on-pc) game [Z](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/z) from The Bitmap Brothers that's completely open source (and, thankfully, hand made).
