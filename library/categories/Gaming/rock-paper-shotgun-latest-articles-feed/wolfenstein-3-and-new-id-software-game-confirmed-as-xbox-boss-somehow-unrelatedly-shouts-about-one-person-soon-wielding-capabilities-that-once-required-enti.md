Xbox have confirmed that Wolfenstein 3 is [indeed in production](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/wolfenstein-3-is-reportedly-in-the-works-at-machine-games-possibly-alongside-a-rainbow-six-siege-ish-multiplayer-thing) at [Indiana Jones and the Great Circle](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/indiana-jones-and-the-great-circle) devs MachineGames and that [Doom](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/doom-the-dark-ages) makers id Software have started work on their next game after [losing a bunch of staff](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/doom-devs-id-software-were-reportedly-kicking-around-ideas-for-a-new-perfect-dark-a-john-wick-inspired-game-and-multiplayer-demonslaying-prior-to-layoffs) to the [mass layoffs](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/xbox-are-parting-ways-with-double-fine-arkane-undead-labs-compulsion-and-ninja-theory-in-the-course-of-thousands-of-layoffs)[](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com) across Microsoft's gaming division this year.

"A single person will soon be able to wield capabilities that once required entire nations," isn't part of the marketing spiel for either of those games. Instead, it's something Xbox CEO Asha Shama has bellowed amid a rant outlining how she sees the company's future.
