This week sees the release of [Grand Theft Auto 6](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/grand-theft-auto-vi), the open-world crime 'em up that's been in development for at least eight years and has been waited on by fans for even longer. Naturally, over on PC we're still waiting for a port to be announced, let alone a release date to circle in our calendars. But, as happens whenever a new GTA is on the horizon, I think about [Mafia: City of Lost Heaven](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/mafia).

Released less than a year after [Grand Theft Auto 3](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/grand-theft-auto-iii), Mafia is perhaps a more prescient example of Rockstar's future than their own.
