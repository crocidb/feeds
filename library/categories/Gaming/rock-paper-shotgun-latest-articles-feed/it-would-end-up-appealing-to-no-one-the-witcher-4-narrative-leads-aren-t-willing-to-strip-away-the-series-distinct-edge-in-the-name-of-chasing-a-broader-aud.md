In a recent [interview](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/28929/the-witcher-4-a-new-protagonist-but-the-essential-witcher-identity-remains) with Korean outlet Inven, CD Projekt Red's narrative director Philipp Weber and vice president of story Marcin Blacha shared some words on [The Witcher 4](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/the-witcher-4), focusing on their awareness that not only will it inevitably be different from [The Witcher 3,](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt) but also that it needs to be in order to justify its existence. Simultaneously, there's a certain something to The Witcher as a series that they feel is difficult to make tangible, but is something they want to ensure they don't lose sight of.
