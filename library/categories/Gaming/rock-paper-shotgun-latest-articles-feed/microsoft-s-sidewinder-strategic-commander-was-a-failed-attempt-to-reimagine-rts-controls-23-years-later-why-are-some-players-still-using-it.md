PC players have been conditioned for decades to accept the keyboard-and-mouse setup for [RTS games](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/genres/strategy-real-time-strategy). One hand has to handle movement and commands through a keyboard, while the other points, drags, and selects with a mouse. Peripheral makers have, usually with the addition of more buttons, made many minor variations to this time-honored arrangement, but there's only ever been one real attempt to rethink it entirely for the PC: the Microsoft SideWinder Strategic Commander.
