 Mucky and disreputable kiosk game [Probably Stolen – Cyberpunk Shopkeeper Sim](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/probably-stolen-cyberpunk-shopkeeper-sim) is getting three big updates over the next few months that add extra starting scenarios, building on the existing wealth of goods, materials, vocations and techs. Those starting scenarios range from peddling human meat to running the finest cyber-frappucino stall this side of Tannhäuser Gate. There are no hard release dates, and the contents of each update may alter, but developers Questing Goose Studio have shared some rough timeframes.
