Freshly published bankruptcy documents for perennially troubled MindsEye developer Build A Rocket Boy have spelled out the scale of the company's debt, with around £61.7 million owed to creditors. The documents also touch on founder Leslie Benzies and CEO Mark Gerhard's claims that MindsEye is the victim of an organised sabotage campaign. The company's leaders have apparently taken out even more loans in order to fund legal action against the suspected perpetrators, with the promise of a healthy payout. I'm not holding my breath.
