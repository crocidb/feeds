 I like to think I'm difficult to hypnotise, but I confess, I'm a sucker for those Youtube adverts for the mobile vertical shooters. You know the ones: boglins pour towards an auto-firing player, who must dodge about to grab upgrades while whittling down the horde. The player in the advert always does pretty well for 20 seconds, but ahhhh, then they get just a tiny bit overconfident, mistime how long it'll take them to erode the health of an ogre midboss, and: bam!

 Show me one of those ads, and I'll always watch to the end in the hope that this time, the protagonist will Study The Blade, conquer their harmatia, and slay the damn ogre. I've never actually played any of the mobile shooters in question, mind you. I'm far too cultured for that, you dope! Except that I've just been playing the demo for Korridor, which is supposedly "based off" of the ads, but might as well be one of the games they're promoting, for all I know.
