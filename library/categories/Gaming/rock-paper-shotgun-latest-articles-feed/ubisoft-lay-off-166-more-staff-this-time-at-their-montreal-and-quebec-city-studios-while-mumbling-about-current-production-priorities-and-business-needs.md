Ubisoft have laid off 166 developers, with this latest round of cuts at the Assassin's Creed and [Far Cry](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/far-cry) publishers taking place at two of their studios in Eastern Canada. Both studios affected, Ubisoft Montreal and Ubisoft Quebec, had a hand in last year's [Assassin's Creed Shadows](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/assassins-creed-codename-red), with Montreal especially being a major and long-tenured [development hub](https://montreal.ubisoft.com/en/our-games/) for the company.
