Late last week, [reports emerged](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/nvidias-latest-graphics-card-driver-appears-to-be-unnecessarily-writing-to-pc-ssds-something-thats-dumb-annoying-and-definitely-warrants-reverting) of some folks having Nvidia's latest GeForce driver, 621.24, needlessly write vast amounts of data to their PC's SSD, a bug that could affect the component's lifespan at a time when [replacing SSDs certainly isn't cheap](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/ssd-prices-in-yet-more-trouble-as-two-of-the-biggest-hard-drive-makers-have-already-sold-out-their-2026-stock)[](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com). The immediate short-term solution for affected users was to roll back to a previous driver, 621.01. Sadly it appears that might still be the best way to go despite Nvidia having released a hotfix.
