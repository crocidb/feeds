[Gloomwood](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/gloomwood), the Victorian survival horror immersive sim (or, wait, I'm getting reports that we [shouldn't call it an immersive sim, apparently](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/system-shock-and-deus-ex-they-were-rpgs-people-called-them-rpgs-gloomwoods-publisher-isnt-too-keen-on-using-the-term-immersive-sim-despite-it-being-one)) by Dillon Rogers is a game that is quite happily inspired by its predecessors such as Thief, Resident Evil and [Dishonored](https://www.rockpapershotgun.com/games/dishonored); you could call it 'Thief with guns', if you really wanted to. (Sorry, I've just seen that the domain for Gloomwood's site used to be 'residenthievil.com'. Wonderful). Now, that last inspiration has come full circle, as publisher New Blood Interactive's latest update post confirms that the newest member of the team is none other than former Dishonored designer Julien Eveillé.
