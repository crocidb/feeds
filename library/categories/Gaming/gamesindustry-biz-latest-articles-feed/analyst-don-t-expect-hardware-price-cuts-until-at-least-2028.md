Manufacturers are unlikely to drop console prices until at least 2028, according to a new analysis by S&P Global Market Intelligence Kagan, which judged that planned expansions in manufacturing capacity for DRAM – a key component in gaming hardware that has rocketed in price due to the demands of the AI industry – will not come online until then.
