Self-publishing is now firmly established as a viable route to market for indie developers, with many only looking to publishers for help with specific issues – chiefly marketing, based on conversations with *GamesIndustry.biz* this year. Audrey Leprince, co-founder of Cairn developer The Game Bakers, argues that while working with third parties is essential, self-publishing is ultimately the only route to sustainability for indie developers.
