A new episode of the still-somewhat-irregular *GamesIndustry.biz* podcast features a fireside chat with Rebecca Lautner, CBO at Landfall Games, recorded at [Game Republic New Horizons](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/events/game-republic-new-horizons-2026). In conversation with features editor Lewis Packwood, Lautner says that even though Peak has now sold more than 23 million units, the developer is keen to move on to something else.
