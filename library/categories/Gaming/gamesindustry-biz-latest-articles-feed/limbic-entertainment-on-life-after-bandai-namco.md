In December 2025, [German developer Limbic Entertainment was sold by Bandai Namco to a "private investor"](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/bandai-namco-sells-limbic-entertainment-to-private-investor). This news came just over three years after [the Japanese giant acquired a majority stake in the studio for an undisclosed sum](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/bandai-namco-europe-takes-majority-stake-in-limbic).
