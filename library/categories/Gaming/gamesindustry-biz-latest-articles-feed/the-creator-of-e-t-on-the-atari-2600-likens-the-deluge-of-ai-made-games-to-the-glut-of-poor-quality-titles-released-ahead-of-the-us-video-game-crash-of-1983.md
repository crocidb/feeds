At the DevGAMM Lisbon conference this week, veteran game designer Howard Scott Warshaw gave a talk called "From Atari to AI". Warshaw was behind a number of famous titles for the Atari 2600, including the classic Yars' Revenge, but infamously he also designed the game E.T. the Extra-Terrestial, the poor quality of which has been cited as a factor in the US video game crash of 1983, when US games industry revenue plummeted from over $3 billion in 1983 to around $100 million in 1985.
