Matt Webster, CEO of Fuse Games, is "thrilled" with the critical reception to Star Wars: Galactic Racer, which is currently sitting on a [Metacritic score of 88](https://www.metacritic.com/game/star-wars-galactic-racer/) for the PS5 version. Speaking to *GamesIndustry.biz* at [Game Republic New Horizons](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/events/game-republic-new-horizons-2026) this week, he says it "felt like we had something good", but you "never really know" how a game will land.
