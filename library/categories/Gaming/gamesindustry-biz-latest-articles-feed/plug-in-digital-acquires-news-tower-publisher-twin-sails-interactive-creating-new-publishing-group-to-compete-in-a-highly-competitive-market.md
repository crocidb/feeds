French publisher and distributor Plug In Digital has acquired Paris-based indie publisher Twin Sails Interactive - formerly the digital division of boardgame publisher Asmodee, and publisher of [News Tower](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1649950/News_Tower/) - and announced the two will combine to form an indie-focused distribution and publishing group that combines "Plug In Digital’s global scale with Twin Sails Interactive’s hands-on creative collaboration."
