The number of games released through Steam's Early Access program has risen significantly in recent years. According to [SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/stats/releases/?tagid=493), 2,924 Early Access games launched on Valve's platform in 2026 (as of mid-October), an 18% increase from 2025 and a 60% increase from 2020.
