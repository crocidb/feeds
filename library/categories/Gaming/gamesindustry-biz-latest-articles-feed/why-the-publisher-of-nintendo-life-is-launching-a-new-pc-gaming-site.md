Games media publisher Hookshot has launched [Steam Hype](https://www.steamhype.com/), a new PC-focused media outlet. It is the fifth site launched by Hookshot – in which *GamesIndustry.biz* parent IGN Entertainment holds a minority stake through its acquisition of Gamer Network – and completes the firm's platform lineup alongside Nintendo Life, Push Square, Pure Xbox, and retro brand Time Extension.
