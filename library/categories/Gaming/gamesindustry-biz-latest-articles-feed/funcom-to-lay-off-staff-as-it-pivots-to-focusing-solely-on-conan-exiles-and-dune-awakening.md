Norwegian developer Funcom has begun a restructuring process following a decision to [focus solely on live operations for Conan Exiles and Dune: Awakening](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/dune-awakenings-creative-director-on-copying-hollywood-in-game-advertising-and-why-style-beats-photorealism), with an unknown number of staff affected. CEO Rui Casais, who has lead the developer for 11 years including through its 2020 acquisition by Tencent, will leave the business at the end of the year, to be succeeded by Chief Product Officer Scott Junior.
