When a draft of the European Commission's Digital Fairness Act leaked earlier this week, some industry reactions had a distinctly end-of-days flavour. The official responses now [winding their way out of trade bodies](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/german-games-industry-association-warns-digital-fairness-act-could-cause-double-regulation-and-legal-uncertainty) after the official draft of the act was released are only ratcheting down the tone a little; less apocalyptic, but nonetheless grim warnings of a terrible fate for European games companies, muttering darkly about the stripping of supposed freedoms from players, parents, and everyone else within a very wide radius.
