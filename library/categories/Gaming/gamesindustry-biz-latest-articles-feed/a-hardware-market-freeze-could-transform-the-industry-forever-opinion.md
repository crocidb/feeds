Right about now is the moment when the console business should be getting one hell of a second wind. Six years deep into a hardware generation, with probably the most anticipated video game launch of all time just a few weeks out, this should be when the platform holders make hay, extending those mature install bases out into ever-wider audiences as fence-sitters finally pick up the hardware they've been holding out on for years.
