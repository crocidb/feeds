Earlier this month, [Yodo1 Games](https://www.yodo1.com/en) announced a strategic digital licensing partnership with the American firm AMC Global Media, which is behind television series such as The Walking Dead and Anne Rice's Interview with the Vampire. Yodo1 is tasked with finding and supporting Chinese game developers and publishers in order to create video games based on AMC's franchises.
