The European Commission's [publication of its draft Digital Fairness Act](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/what-the-digital-fairness-act-means-for-the-games-industry) (DFA) last week prompted uproar within the games industry, with many notable voices candidly expressing their concerns. Opinions in the wider industry were more measured, possibly because some of the practices targeted by the DFA – such as loot boxes or pay-to-win – are wildly successful economically, but not uniformly popular amongst players.
