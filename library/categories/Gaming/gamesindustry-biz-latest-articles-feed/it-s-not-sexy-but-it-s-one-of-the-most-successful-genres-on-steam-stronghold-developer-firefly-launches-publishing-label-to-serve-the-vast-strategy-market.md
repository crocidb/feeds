Stronghold developer Firefly is the latest developer to move into publishing, debuting a new strategy-focused label Wuselfaktory and its first title Fool King as part of UKIE's For One Night Only showcase earlier this year. The title, from first-time developer Loot Donkey, describes itself as a "tactical autobattler" with a roguelike edge, in which players build and position their army before watching battles play out.
