There is a perfectly logical way in which you can look at an election result – even a truly seismic election result – and say that it is largely irrelevant to the business decision-making of the games industry. Elections do not change your customer demographics; all of Washington or Westminster or Nagatachō may change overnight, but the people who bought your games yesterday are still the people who will buy them tomorrow, aren't they?
