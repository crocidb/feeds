IGN Entertainment, the parent company of *GamesIndustry.biz*, has released the [Official Online Guide for GTA 6](https://www.ign.com/gta), created through direct collaboration with Rockstar Games. The free guide, which was published on IGN.com, includes an extensive interactive map built using the firm's [MapGenie](https://mapgenie.io/) platform, along with checklist trackers for completed tasks and detailed guides on missions, mechanics, and the game's vast open world.
