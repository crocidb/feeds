Tokyo Game Show's 30th anniversary badged itself as "the longest five days of nonstop play", a showcase of the future of gaming. In reality, the event in Japan's largest gaming convention center, Makuhari Messe, ended after only four, [the final day canceled due to the risk of an oncoming typhoon](https://kotaku.com/typhoon-dujuan-forces-tokyo-game-show-2026-to-shut-down-a-day-early-2000735879), and seemed to be mightily obsessed with the past, only otherwise hinting towards a more uncertain future.
