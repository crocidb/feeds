The news was announced via an [Xbox Wire post](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/09/22/continuing-our-reset/) this morning, and followed [earlier reports of planned layoffs](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/xbox-reportedly-set-to-announce-second-round-of-layoffs-this-week).
