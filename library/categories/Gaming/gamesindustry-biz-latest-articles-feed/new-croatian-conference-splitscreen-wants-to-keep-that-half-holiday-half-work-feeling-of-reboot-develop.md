A new Croatian development conference is aiming to fill the gap left by the recently-deceased and much-cherished Reboot Develop, which ended this year with [the organiser being served with a bankruptcy order](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/organiser-of-reboot-develop-issued-with-bankruptcy-order-underlining-fears-over-events-future). The "no-stress" [Splitscreen](https://www.splitscreencroatia.com/), which takes place in the Croatian capital on April 20–22, 2027, has been created by a new team, but seeks to capture the same spirit that made its forebear a popular destination for the global industry.
