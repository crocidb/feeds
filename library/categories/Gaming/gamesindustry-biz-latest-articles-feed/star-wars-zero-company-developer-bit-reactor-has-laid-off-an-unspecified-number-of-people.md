Bit Reactor furloughed "[many of its workers](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/bit-reactor-furloughed-many-of-its-workers-in-the-weeks-before-star-wars-zero-companys-launch)" ahead of the release of the turn-based tactics game Star Wars Zero Company in August, before starting the process of [returning furloughed staff to work in October](https://www.gamesindustry.biz/star-wars-zero-company-developer-bit-reactor-begins-bringing-furloughed-staff-back). Now, it has emerged that an unspecified number of workers at the developer have been laid off.
