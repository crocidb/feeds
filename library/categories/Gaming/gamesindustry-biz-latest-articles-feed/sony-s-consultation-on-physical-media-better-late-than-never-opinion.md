The news that Sony is surveying its development partners to get their input on its decision to end support for physical game releases ([first reported by Moore's Law is Dead](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XyKcIijowjQ)) is a little odd, at least from a sequencing perspective. You'd generally imagine that talking to your most important business partners would be something to be done *before* making a major public announcement of an enormous change that will impact all of them. Better late than never, I suppose, though exactly how much better remains to be seen.
